Global Robusta prices have soared to multi-decade highs due to supply issues in major coffee-producing countries like Vietnam and Brazil. According to news agency Reuters, the price of Robusta beans peaked at $4,667 per metric ton on the London-based ICE Futures Europe market in June, marking a 63 per cent increase this year alone. Notably, India's exports of tea saw little change despite expectations of gaining new markets following the economic crisis in Sri Lanka, one of the largest tea exporters in the world.
