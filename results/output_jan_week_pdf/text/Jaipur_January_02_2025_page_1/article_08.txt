Polls over, reunion calls from both NCP groups: Sharad Pawar is our deity, says Praful Patel

AMONG THE many prayers made on New Year's Day, one stands out in Maharashtra. On Wednesday, Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Ajit Pawar's mother Asha Pawar visited the temple town of Pandharpur and, in her own words, prayed for the reunion of Ajit and his uncle and NCP (SP) chief Sharad Pawar. "All disputes should end... Sharad Pawar and Ajit Pawar should reunite," Asha Pawar told reporters after visiting the Vithal-Rukmini temple in the temple town, adding that she had also prayed for "all wishes of Ajit Pawar" to come true.
