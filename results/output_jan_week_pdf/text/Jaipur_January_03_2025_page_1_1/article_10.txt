JANUARY 3, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

ASSEMBLY DISSOLVED

THE KARNATAKA ASSEMBLY was dissolved on January 2 by the Governor, A N Banerji on the advice of Chief Minister Ramakrishna Hegde who had also tendered his resignation. The Congress (I), the main opposition party in the state assembly, was against forming an alternative government preferring imposition of President's rule in the state.
