Iran minister in Delhi for talks to boost ties, trade via Chabahar

DAYS BEFORE Donald Trump returns to the Oval Office, Majid Takht-Ravanchi, Iran's Deputy Foreign Minister, has reached New Delhi for talks Friday with External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar and Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri. Iran is looking at increasing bilaterat trade with India, including via the Chabahar port. For Tehran, which has suffered from the economic sanctions imposed during Trump's first term, increasing trade with Indiais "very important". Tehran hopes to diversify to other areas, including agriculture and petrochemical products through the Chabahar port.
