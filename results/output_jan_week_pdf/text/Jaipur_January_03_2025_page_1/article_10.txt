Unless there is clear proof, we cannot take action: CM Naidu on Adani power contract

ANDHRA PRADESH Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu has said that the Solar Energy Corporation of India's agreement to purchase power from the Adani Group was an advantage for the state government and any action on bribery charges would be taken only after a thorough examination of the records, "We will move ahead as per the provisions oflaw after examining the records," Naidu said on Wednesday at the TDP's headquarters at Mangalagiri.
