FACING LOSS of support within his party and low popularity in polls, Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau announced Monday that he will resign as party leader and PM, but will stay on as the PM until the Liberal Party chooses a new leader. On Trudeau's request, Governor General Mary Simon agreed to prorogue Parliament until March 24. This will give the Liberal Party time to hold a shortened leadership contest.
