His ratings down, Trudeau announces resignation, opens a window to reset ties

Task easier said than done given Ottawa's hard line against New Delhi

FACING LOSS of support within his party and low popularity in polls, Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau announced Monday that he will resign as party leader and PM, but will stay on as the PM until the Liberal Party chooses a new leader. On Trudeau's request, Governor General Mary Simon agreed to prorogue Parliament until March 24. This will give the Liberal Party time to hold a shortened leadership contest. the assessment, going by opinion polls, was that he would have possibly lost the elections after being in office for 10 years - he became the Liberal Party's leader in 2013 and PM in 2015.
