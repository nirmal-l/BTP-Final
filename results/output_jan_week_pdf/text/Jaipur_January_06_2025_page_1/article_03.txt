PM vows BJP will continue welfare schemes if voted to power in Delhi

PARIVARTAN RALLY AHEAD OF DELHI ELECTIONS

They are lying that we will stop schemes: Modi hits out at AAP

WITH HIS focus firmly on the upcoming Assembly elections, Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Sunday said all ongoing welfare schemes in Delhi will continue if the BJP forms the government in the Capital. Addressing a Parivartan Rally at the Japanese Park in Rohini, Modi attacked AAP, again calling it the "AAP-da (disaster) government" - a term he first used on January 3, when he opened the BJP's poll campaign in Delhi with afierce attack on the ruling party.
