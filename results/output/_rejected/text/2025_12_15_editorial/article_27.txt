in line with RBI guidelines, Bank is in the process of carrying out periodic updation of KYC of existing customers and also in process of reviving relationship with the customers who are maintaining Saving Bank and/or Current Deposit account but not operating their accounts since long time. In the above process, Bank has observed that many accounts, having zero balance are turned inoperative (Dormant) on or before 30.09.2022. In order to prevent misuse of such Dormant accounts, Bank has decided to close all such accounts which were not operated since last 5 years and are in Dormant status since last 3 years. We bring to the notice of all such customers that Bank is keen to continue its relationship with customers who would like to revive their Zero Balance Dormant accounts to active status and hence hereby advise all such customers to contact the concerned/ nearest branch for updating their KYC and resumption of operations immediately within 30 days from the date of this notice. The following accounts shall be exempted from the above closure process: PMJDY (BSBDA/BSBDS) Scheme, A/c's frozen by Court Order /1.T. Dept or any other statutory authority, DEA Fund Accounts, Account linked to locker, Accounts with lien for any amount. Please note that Bank may proceed with closure of all such Zero Balance accounts which are not activated by submitting the KYC documents within 30 days from the date of this notice.
