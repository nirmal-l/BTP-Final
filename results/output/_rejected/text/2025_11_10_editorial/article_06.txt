Pune land deal: Ajit Pawar defends son, blames sub-registrar

Manoj More Pune, November 9

Chief Minister Ajit Pawar Sunday stepped out once again to defend his son Parth Pawar, whose firm is facing allegations of irregularities in the purchase of 44 acres of land in Pune, and sought to blame the sub-registrar forregistering the sale deed. "How did the individual in the sub-registrar's office register the deed? What prompted him todothe registration anddothe wrong thing? We will come to know through the probe (ordered by Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis)," he said. Insisting that no sale had

taken place, he said, "Without a rupee (exchanged)... only by giving figuresand papers, Ihave not understood till date how the transaction can take place." The Revenue department hassuspended the sub-registrar involved, Ravindra Taru, foralleged irregularities in the registration of the 44-acre "Mahar watan" land on behalf of Amadea Enterprises LLP in which Parth Pawar is a partner along with his relative Digvijay Patil. The Opposition has alleged that the land valued at about Rs 1,800 crore was sold illegally for just Rs 300 crore, and that stamp duty worth Rs 21 crore was waived.
