350 kg explosives, AK-47: J&K cops seize arms cache from doc's Faridabad home Two doctors held, part of 'interstate, transnational' terror module, say police

Bashaarat Masood & Abhimanyu Hazarika Srinagar, Faridabad, November10

IN A major security operation conducted over the past two weeks, J&K Police said they have arrested two doctors in Haryana and Uttar Pradesh who were allegedly part of an "interstate and transnational terror module" linked to the terror groups Jaish-e-Mohammad and Ansar Ghazwatul Hind (AGH). The two arrested doctors have been identified by J&aK Police as Dr Muzammil Ahmad Ganai from Pulwama and Dr Adeel Majeed Rather from Qazigund.
