PM unveils Ram statue

PM Narendra Modi with Goa CM Pramod Sawant after unveiling a 77-foot bronze statue of Lord Ram in South Goa district on Friday. The unveiling was part of the 550th year celebrations of the Shree Samsthan Gokarn Jeevottam Mutt. Earlier in the day, the PM also visited the Sri Krishna Mutt in Karnataka's Udupi district. pt!
