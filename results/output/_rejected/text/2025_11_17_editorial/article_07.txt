Diagnosis and treatment coverage The Global TB Report shows significant improvement in India's treatment coverage, which stood at 92% in 2024, increasing from 85% in 2023. "India's innovative case finding approach, driven by the swift uptake of newer technologies, decentralisation ofservices and large-scale community mobilisation, has ledto the country's treat-mentcoverage to surge to over 92% in 2024 from 53% in 2015," the Union Health Ministry said ina statement. The expansion of treatment coverage came due to initiatives such as the BPaL regimen, which cancut the treatment time for resistant infections to six months from the previous 18 to24 months.
