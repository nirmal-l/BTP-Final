"Tamil Nadu has shown the way through its TN-KET programme. By triaging, adults with very severe undernutrition, respiratory insufficiency, poor performance status are being identified and managed. Once a triage-based strategy is effectively implemented, comorbidity management components can be added, like management of severe diabetes, tobacco addiction, alcohol addiction, one step at atime," said Dr Hemant Shewade, senior scientist at ICMR-National Institute of Epidemiology.
