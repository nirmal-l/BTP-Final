Before NDA, UPA too discussed halting work, sharing cost

Manoj CG New Delhi, December 15

AMONG THE major changes that the NDA government has proposed to the National Rural Employment Guarantee Act architecture is halting the scheme during peak farming seasons and moving it froma Centre-funded model to acostsharing arrangement with states. While the Opposition, including the Congress, hashit out at the government, these issues were points of debatesand contention during the UPA government as well. In 2011, Sharad Pawar, as Agriculture Minister in the UPA-II government, had called for halting NREGA works during sowing and harvesting seasons, saying the scheme was adversely affecting the agriculture sector by drawing out farm labourers from agricultural operations.
