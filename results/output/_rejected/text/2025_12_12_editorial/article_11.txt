Now, Cong Govt in Karnataka warns of bulldozer action in drug cases

Kiran Parashar Belagavi, December li

KARNATAKA HOME Minister G Parameshwara on Thursday said the Congress government was taking "decisive action" in drug-related offences and was prepared to take strong steps, including bulldozing the homes of drug peddlers. Responding to a question byMLCK Abdul Jabbar during

the Winter Session of the Karnataka legislature here, Parameshwara said, "Alot of foreign nationals, many of them from African countries, have been caught smuggling and peddling drugs. We don't know how they bring it in orsupply it Weare monitoring their movements and have also identified the landlords who have rented houses to these people. We are prepared to go to the extent of

bulldozing even the homes wherethe drug peddlersliveon rent."
