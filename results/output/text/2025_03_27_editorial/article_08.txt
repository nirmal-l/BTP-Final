MARCH 27, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

THE ONE-DAY BANDHin Punjab and Haryana over the killing of Chandigarh BJP leader Krishan Lal Manchanda by suspected terror-ists evoked varied responses and passed off peacefully. In Punjab, its impact was felt mostly in urban areas, whilerural areas virtually ignored it In Haryana, it evoked a massive response in Ambala, Karnal and Yamunanagar, but was ignored in Sonipat.

OPPOSITION WALKOUT ALMOSTTHE ENTIRE Opposition walked out of the Lok Sabha during zero hour on Tuesday

in protest against the Deputy Speaker's ruling disallowing an adjournment motion. This was moved by Madhu Dandavate (Janata) to discuss the Prime Minister's reported allegation that a frustrated Opposition was responsible for the communal trouble in Ahmedabad.
