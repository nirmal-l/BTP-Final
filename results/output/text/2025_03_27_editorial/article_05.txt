It is also vital to recognise that collective workers' rights remain the most assured form of protection. Evolving forms oftechnology have promised inclusion for domestic workers but have reinforced societal inequalities, Police verification processes, based on finger-printing, have caricatured the worker as an archetypal "buffoon" in the past, asking for physical "deformity/peculiarity" and "pet words of speech". As researchers such as Nitin Sinha have found, these initiatives further criminalise domestic workers. Urban Company has rebranded Insta Maids to Insta Help following a social media backlash, claiming to believe in the "dignity of labour". Its advertising, however, follows older apps such as DekhoSeekho that attempted to transform the worker into amodern housekeeper by including cooking videos catering toglobal tastes. In both these initiatives, what is prioritised is the comfort of the employer and a cosmetic makeover of the "maid" without ensuring fair, humane work conditions and legally enforced protections.
