THE SUPREME COURT should have two separate divisions - a constitutional and a legal division - the Law Commission has recommended in its95threport, which was tabled in Lok Sabha. The Commission, headed by Justice K K Mathew, has also recommended that the constitutional division should have atleast seven judges and that appointmentof judges should be made t separate divisions.

THE TWO-YEAR-OLD alliance between the Janata Party and the BJP, which survived many

vicissitudes while sustaining the minority Janata goverment in Kamataka, came to an end when talks about seat adjustments between the two parties broke down. The two major parties, which fought the recent Lok Sabha elections unitedly on the basis of seat sharing, will now confront each other in the assembly poll in Kamataka, unless the Chief Minister, RamaKrishna Hegde, is abe to pulloff alast-minute salvage operation when he meets the national president of the BJP.AB Vajpayee, in New Delhion February 1.
