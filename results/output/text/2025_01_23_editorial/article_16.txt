JANUARY 23, 1985, FoRTY YEARS AGO

PM ON MINORITIES

PRIME MINISTER RAJIV Gandhi declared in the Lok Sabha that his government would tackle the problem of minorities witha view wendingit "once and for all". Reptying to the debate on the President's address to Parliament, he said economic disparity was the main cause of the periodic enuptionofriots. His reference to the minority problem came immediately after his assertion that the govemment was determined to find a solution to the problems of Punjab and Assam. At the outset, the Prime Minister charged the opposition members with making wildcharges after losing badly in the elections.
