Anti-Corruption Bureau arrests MLA 'caught accepting ~20-lakh bribe'

RAJASTHAN

RAJASTHAN'S ANTI-CORRUPTION Bureau on Sunday said it arrested first-time MLA Jaikrishn Patel, 37, for allegedly accepting a Rs 20-lakh bribe in lieu of withdrawing Assembly questions on mining. The ACB said this is the first instance of it arresting an MLA in the state. ACB Director General Ravi Prakash Meharda told journalists that Patel, the Bharat Adivasi Party (BAP) MLA from Bagidora in Banswara district, and middleman Vijay Kumar Patel were caught "red-handed" at the MLA quarters in Jaipur while accepting the bribe. However, just before he wasnabbed, the MLA managed to hand over the money to an aide who escaped, Meharda said.
