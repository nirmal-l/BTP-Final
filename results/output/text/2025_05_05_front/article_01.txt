INDIAN AIR Force chief Air Chief Marshal A P Singh met Prime Minister Narendra Modi Sunday andis learnt to have briefed him onthe overall preparedness of the IAF in the context of retaliatory options being weighed by the government following the Pahalgam terror attack. While there was no official statement on the meeting, Air Chief Marshal Singh and the Prime Minister were said to have discussed the security scenario and the options on the table. The IAF chief's meeting at 7, Lok Kalyan Marg took place hours after Navy chief Admiral Dinesh K Tripathi spoke to the Prime Minister on the situation in the northern Arabian Sea.
