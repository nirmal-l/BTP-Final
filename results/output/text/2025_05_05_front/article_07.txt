Workload, eye on future: no leadership role for Bumrah on England tour

INDIA'S FAST bowling figurehead and vice captain Jasprit Bumrah is unlikely to be givena leadership role on the Test tour to England starting June-end, sources in the BCCI told The Indian Express. The aceseamer had led India to theirsole winon the Australia tour at the turn of the year, apart from leading in two other Tests. But since Bumrah is unlikely to feature in all the five Tests in England as part of his workload management, the selectors are likely to seek a player who will play through the tour.
