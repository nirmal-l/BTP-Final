US probing Adani link to Iran LPG trade: report; firm says 'baseless'

AdaniGroup Chairman Gautam Adani

PROSECUTORS IN the US are investigating whether Indian businessman Gautam Adani's companies imported Iranian liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) into India through the Adani Group's Mundra port, despite US sanctions on Iran, The Wall Street Journal reported on Monday. This comes at a time when the Trump administration is rolling back onthe enforcement of certain white-collar crimes, including cases related toforeign bribery and sanctions evasion. Tankers travelling between Mundra in Gujarat and the Persian Gulf exhibited traits common to ships evading sanctions, the WSJ report said. The US Justice Departmentis reviewing the activities of several LPG tankers used to ship cargoes to AdaniEnterprises, it said.
