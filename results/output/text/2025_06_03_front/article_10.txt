Foreign Embassy staffer put up 'Wanted' posters of Netanyahu: Delhi Police report to MHA

THE DELHI Police have approached the Union Home Ministry after they found that a foreign citizen working with an embassy of a Western European country had allegedly put up posters carrying a photo of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu alongside the word "wanted" in the national capital's Chanakyapuri area, The indian Express has learnt. After scanning multiple CCTV cameras, it has been revealed that he left from one of the flats in Sardar Patel Marg," the officer said.
