Before Shukla's space mission, anxiety, excitement and a little prayer at home

Set to take off with 3 other astronauts on Axiom Mission-4 in Florida tomorrow

FOR THE past few days, the sprawling white single-storeyed Anshu Niwas in one corner of Lucknow's Triveni Nagar has been seeing a steady stream of friends, relatives, well-wishers and the media. They are all there for one reason - to offer their good wishes to the owners of the house, the Shuklas, for their 39- year-old Air Force Group Captain Shubhanshu 'Gunjan' Shukla's

maiden flight to space. On June 10, over 10 months after the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) announced his name as one of four astronauts as part of Axiom Space's fourth commercial mission to the Intemational Space Station, Shubhanshu will take off on a two-week Indo-US mission.
