Conceived as an international institute of excellence where the old and new knowledge systems coexist, the new Nalanda University was established under the Nalanda University Act, 2010, by the Ministry of External Affairs. Then PresidentA PJ Abdul Kalam was

"match the architectural and geographical setting the ancient Nalanda University would have provided".
