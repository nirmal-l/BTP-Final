Members of Public Service Commissions must not be wedded to any ideology: V-P

Criticises practice of granting extension in service, post-retirement appointment

SPEAKING OUT against appoint-ments "driven by patronage and favouritism" in Public Service Commissions, Vice-President Jagdeep Dhankhar on Saturday said PSC members should not be "wedded to a particular ideology orindwidual". Dhankhar also criticised the practice of granting extensionsin service, calling it a "setback to those who are in line" and a move that "defies the logical principle of extension". In this context. he also spoke out against post-retirement assignments, saying such "largesse... is antithetical to what was visualised by framers of the Constitution". Dhankhar was delivering the inaugural address at the 25th National Conference of Chair-personsof All State Public Service Commissions in Bengaluru.
