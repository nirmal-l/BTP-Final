In a first, Taliban foreign minister to visit Delhi after UNSC's go-ahead

IN A historic first, Taliban-ruled Afghanistan's Foreign Minister Amir Khan Muttagqjis set to visit India. The UN Security Council hasallowed him to travel to New Delhi from October 9 to 16. The approval of the UNSC committee, currently chaired by non-permanent member Pakistan, was granted September 30, and it was made official Thursday.
