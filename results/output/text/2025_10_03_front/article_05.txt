Everyone has own beliefs... be careful not to disrespect: RSS chief Bhagwat Warns against 'show of strength' to 'provoke a particular community'

DRAWING FROM the experience of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh over its 100-year journey, its chief Mohan Bhagwat placed communal harmony and social responsibility at the heart of his vision for India. He urged citizens to recognise their shared identity as a nation and to conduct themselves with respect, restraint, and vigilance in preserving societal unity.
