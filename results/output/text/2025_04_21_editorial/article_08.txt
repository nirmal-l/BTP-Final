TWOPERSONS, INCLUDING a woman, were killed and five others injured in the wake of renewed mob violence in Gujarat. The anti-reservationists refused tohold talks with the government till police atrocities stopped completely. One woman and three youths were injured as the police fired on a 5000- strong mob of anti-reservationists disrupting school examinations at Kathlal town in Kheda district. One seriously injured person was brought to Ahmedabad for hospitalisation. An indefinite curfew was reimposed in the industrial suburb of Saraspur following heavy stone-pelting,

TWOIMPORTANT AMENDMENTS proposed to the draft declaration on Namibian independence call for the intensification of the struggle at all levels including political, economic and military. The two amendments add a new dimension to the declaration, calling for direct action by all countries to isolate the racist regime of South Africa. The intensification of the struggle comes after South Africa's move to install an interim government in Namibia excluding the South West African People's Organisation (SWAPO).
