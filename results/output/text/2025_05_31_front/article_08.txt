Taken to court by L&T, MMRDA says scrapping tenders for mega infra projects Cj bench accepts submission, Authority says fresh tenders to be issued

TAKEN TO court by Larsen & Toubro (L&T) Ltd which said it had been kept in the dark about the status of bids for two projects worth Rs 14,000 crore - the Thane-Ghodbunder to Bhayandar tunnel and elevated road - the Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA)informedthe Supreme Court Friday that it had scrapped the tender process "in the larger publicinterest" and fresh tenders would be issued in due course. Senior Advocate Mukul Rohatgi, appearing for MMRDA,

told the benchof Chief Justice of India B R Gavai and Justices AG Masih and AS Chandurkar: "We have decided to scrap the two tenders. The government will decide what they want todo."
