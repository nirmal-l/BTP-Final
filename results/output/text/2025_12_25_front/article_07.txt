India Gate link as Cabinet okays 3 new Delhi Metro corridors

Express News Service New Delhi, December 24

THE UNION Cabinet on Wednesday approved three new corridors of the Delhi Metro network, including one that will provide connectivity from the new Central government offices at Kartavya Bhawan - and transport visitors to India Gate and the National War Memorial in the heart of the capital.

The Cabinet, at a meeting chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, approved the three corridors at a cost of Rs12,014.91crore, of which the Union and Delhi governments will contribute Rs 1,759 crore
