Rajasthan HC paves way for registration of live-in relationships

THE RAJASTHAN High Court on Wednesday directed the state to establish an authority or tribunal to register live-in relationships until a law is enacted for the same. Hearing a number of petitions, a Bench of Justice Anoop Kumar Dhand said, "The live-in relationship agreement is liable to be registered by the competentauthority/tribunal, whichis required to be established by the govemment Till enactment of the appropnite legislation by the government, let competent

authority be establishedineach district of the state to look into the matter ofregistration of such live-in relationships." The court said the authority would "address and redress the grievances ofsuch partners/couples who have entered in such relationships and the children being born out therefrom. Let a website or web portal be launched in this regard for redressalof the issue arisingout of suchrelationships" Thecourt also said that until a law is enacted by the state as well as the Centre, "a scheme of statutory nature is required tobe formulated in legal format",
