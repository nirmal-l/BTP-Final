The mystery man who is believed to have supplied explosives to the suspected terrorists arrested at West Patel Nagar, Delhi, has been identified as Karam Singh Kohli, ayoung man who is about 25 years old. Kohli apparently did not have any consistent "front", sources said. He operated mainly from Amritsar, Ludhiana and Delhi. Hisdescription has been given confidentially to the police.

THE FLASH STRIKE by the DTC drivers and conductors of GT Karnal Road depot spread to seven other depots, paralysing bus service in North Delhi. The DTC crew refused to ply buses to protest against the termination of the services of two of their colleagues. A team of drivers and conductors went round various depots, deflated tyres and stopped those who wanted to ply. The bus service in south and west Delhi remained normal, however.
