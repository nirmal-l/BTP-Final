APRIL 2, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

ANOTHER GAS LEAK ARMS RACE & INDIA BJP Boycott IN PUNJAB INDIA-CHINA TIES

POISONOUS GAS AND acid fumes leaked again from the "Viller Carbide" pesticide plant in Bhopal, causing widespread panic. Hundreds of panic-stricken residents of shanty towns surrounding the pesticide pianit, which leaked toxicmethyliso-cyanate gas last December, killing about 2,500 persons, fled for safety following the leak.

INDIAAND CHINA expressed a mutual desire to improve relations, with India particularly stressing theneed tosettle theboundary question. This is evident fromtheceiebrations held tomark the 35th anniversary ofthe establishment of diplomatic relations - an event unthinkable a few years ago. The Chinese ambassador hosted a dinner to celebrate the occasion, where Minister of State for External Affairs Khursheed Alam Khan voiced the Indian viewpoint on the boundary question.
