Trump tariff: Exports to US dip 12% but China and UAE cushion blow

Total goods exports deficit highest ina year on gold, silver, fertiliser imports

EVEN AS India's goods exports to the United States registered a sharp fall of 12 per cent in September (year on year), the total exports registered a 6.74 per cent growth, buttressed by gains in the UAE and Chinese markets, However, a sharp surge in gold, silver and fertiliser imports widened the trade deficit to $31.15 billion, the highest in over ayear, according todata released by the Commerce and Industry Ministry on Wednesday.
