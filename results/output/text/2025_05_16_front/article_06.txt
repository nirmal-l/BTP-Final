As Beijing, Washington dial down, Goyal to lead team for US trade talks

A "LARGE Indian trade" delegation led by Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal is set to visit the US for talks next week to potentially finalise the contours of an interim trade agreement between the two countries, a senior government official said Thursday. The fresh round of trade talks with the US assumes significance asit follows the conclusion of asurprise US-China trade deal that saw substantial reductions in the "tit-for-tat" tariffs.

"The deal has only resulted in a de-escalation of tensions. But substantial tariffs on China remain. India is focusing on its competitiveness," an official said in response to aquestionon the impact of the US-China deal on India. Onthe recent action by India to propose retaliatory tariffs against the 25 per cent US duties on steel and aluminium at WTO, the official said that India aimed to reserve the right to retaliate even as negotiations withthe US were ongoing.
