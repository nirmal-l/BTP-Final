First action against Turkey: India arm of airport firm loses its security status

IN THE FIRST official action against Turkey post the Pahalgam terror attack, the Bureau of Civil Aviation Security (BCAS) Thursday revoked with immediate effect the security clearance of ground handling firm Celebi Airport Services, the Indian arm of Turkey-headquartered Celebi Aviation Holding, in theinterest of "national security".

The regulator's decision comes amid a backlash in India over Turkey's open support for Islamabad and widespread use of Turkish drones by Pakistan against India during the four-day military conflict. Many tour operators and airlines have also seen cancellation of bookings by Indian tourists to Turkey.
