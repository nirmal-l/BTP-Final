Trump frowns on Apple push to make in India; claims Delhi to drop all tariffs

US PRESIDENT IN DOHA; REMARKS COME AS APPLE SHIFTS FROM CHINA

Says told CEO Cook: 'Don't want you to build in India, itis one of highest tariff nations in world'

US PRESIDENT Donald Trump said Thursday he has told Apple CEO Tim Cook that he does not want the company to expand its manufacturing operations in India, unless it is to cater specifically to its domestic market. He also claimed that India has "agreed to charge us literally no tariffs" on US goods. Speaking at a business roundtable in Qatar's capital Doha while on a four-day visit to the Gulf region, Trump said, "I

had a little problem with Tim Cook yesterday. I said, 'Tim you are my friend, I have treated you very good, you are coming here (in the US) with a $500-billion announcement, and now I hear you are building all over India. I

don't want you building in India. You can build in India if you want to take care of India, because India is one of the highest tariff nations in the world' Soitisvery hard to sell in India."
