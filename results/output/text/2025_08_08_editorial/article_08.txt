THE GOVERNMENTANDthe Opposition could not arrive at a consensus on the question of holding elections in Punjab before the expiry of the present term of President's Rule. While regional parties from the South and Congress (S) were in favour of early elections, all other opposition parties opposed. Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi said that the government had an open mind on the issue.
