Two soldiers killed as anti-terror operation in Kulgam enters Day 9

TWO SOLDIERS were killed and two injured overnight in south Kashmir's Kulgam during anencounter between terrorists and security forces that entered its ninth day on Saturday. The encounter broke out after security forces launched an anti-terror operation on August

1. It has now become one of the longest such operations in the Valley in recent years. The Army said Lance Naik Pritpal Singh and Sepoy Harminder Singh were killedinan intense gunfight on the intervening night of Friday and Saturday. "Their courage and dedication willforeverinspire us.
