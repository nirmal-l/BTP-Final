SGPC PROBED

THE CBIIS holding an inquiry into the foreigndonations totheShiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee amounting to about one million rupees since 1978, accordingto authoritative sources. The CBlin this respect has reportedly sought records from certain banks in Amritsar to verify whether the SGPC sought permission from the government for receiving such donations as required under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 1976. Meanwhile, the SGPC has sought "general exemption and realisation" under the Act to avoid infringement of the law and to facilitate relief work.
