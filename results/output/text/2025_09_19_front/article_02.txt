ADELHI court Thursday set aside a September 6 order against four journalists which restrained them from publishing alleged defamatory content on the Adani Enterprises Limited (AEL) and directed them to remove "unverified" posts. The four journalists - Ravi Nair, Abir Dasgupta, Ayaskanta Das and Ayush Joshi - who were among the individuals and entities restrained by the September 6 order had challenged it. A fifth journalist, Paranjoy Guha Thakurta, had filed a separate challenge which was heard by a different court that posted the matter to September 22.
