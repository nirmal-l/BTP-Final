Fast bowler who switched to javelin, UP's Sachin Yadav finishes 4th at world meet

AS A 19-year-old, when Sachin Yadav decided to give up fast bowling and take up the javelin, his family in Khekada village, in Baghpat district of Uttar Pradesh, was worried. But, although cricket was a far more attractive career option than track and field, Sachin's father, Naresh Yadav, respected his son's decision, even borrowing money to buy him a pair of javelin spikes. That was six years ago. On Thursday evening, Sachin, now 25, narrowly missed out on a podium finish, coming fourth at the World Athletics Champion-shipsin Tokyo. He outperformed several celebrated javelin throwers, including defending champion Neeraj Chopra and Olympian Arshad Nadeem from Pakistan.
