Voter, Aadhaar databases will link, opt-out option to stay But voters declining to share Aadhaar number will have to explain why

EC, HOME, LAW, IT, UIDAI MEET

THE ELECTION Commission of India (ECI) will work with the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) to link its voter records with the Aadhaar data-base, while the Law Ministry will amend Form 68 to clarify that providing Aadhaar details remains voluntary, though voters declining @ share this information must explain their reasons, The Indian Express has leamed. These decisions were made ata high-level meeting held on Tuesday between senior officers ofthe ECland the Home Minstry, Law Ministry, IT Ministry and UIDAL

The meeting the first onebetween the Commission and the Union Government this year, was attended by Home Secretary Govind Mohan; MEITY Secretary S Krishnan; Legislative Department Secretary Rajiv Mani and UIDAICEO Bhuvnesh Kumar.

How Form 6B will @ change

FORM 68 lacks options for electors to abstain from providing Aadhaar, offering only two choices: either provide Aadhaar or declare, '1 am not able to furnish my Aadhaar because I don't have Aadhaar number." This will be amended but voter will have to offer an explanation why she is not providing her Aadhaar.
