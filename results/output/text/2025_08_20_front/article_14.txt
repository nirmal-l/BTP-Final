ICSSR to issue notice to CSDS over 'manipulation' of Maharashtra poll data

THE INDIAN Council of Social Science Research (ICSSR) on Tuesday said it willissuea show cause notice tothe Centre for the Study of Developing Societies over an erroneous claim made by Sanjay Kumar - professor and co-director of Lokniti, a research programme at the CSDS - inasocial media post that two constituencies in Maharashtra had shown a significant fall in the numbers of voters between the Lok Sabha and Assembly polls last year.
