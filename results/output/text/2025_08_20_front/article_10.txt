Centre set to ban online real money gaming, Bill likely in Parliament today Seeks to promote competitive e-sports, game development via proposed law

THE MINISTRY of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has drafted a legislation which could place a blanket ban on all online real money gaming services - where users can play games after depositing money in the form of a stake or a wager - owing to growing concerns about alleged money laundering through these services and their harmful societal impact, The Indian Express has learnt. The proposed law, learnt to be titled 'The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Bill, 2025', has been cleared by Cabinet andis likely to be tabled in Parliament on Wednesday.
