"WHAT'S THE PROBLEM in cooking for afew people? It just takes two hours. enjoy cooking." I've seen many men post something along these lines on social media since the movie Mrs released on February 7. The fact that this was their only take-away made me angry, although it didn't surprise me. My mother was a working woman who managed the household as well. At 40,my father quit his job and told us he would handle the house while mom worked. He figured he could write his book in the free time he would have left. The first week went well. He moved cupboards to clean undemeath them and got two chapters of the book done. Then came the week of baingan-aloo. He sat down one night and made enough for the week. have not eaten this dish since. Dad was soon miserable, as the reality of what Staying at home began to sink in - it was about more than just housework. He had lost the balance of power.
