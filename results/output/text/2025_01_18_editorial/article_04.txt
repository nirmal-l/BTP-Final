What the Lancet report underlines, in fact, is how littke we understand about the combination of bones, flesh and blood that is the mortal coil - and by extension, how limited our understanding is about the actual state of our bodies. Space may be the final frontier, but the original, unconquered frontier remains the human body. The problem is that this acknowledgement of the gaps inmedicine - thattherght diagnosis, the best cure, the fullest understanding, are still in the future - fails to trickledown int the widerculture, evenas medical jargon/tools become widely-used by laypersons to self-(mis)diagnose.
