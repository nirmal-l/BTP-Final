APRIL 17, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

ARMY IN GUJARAT INDIANS ABDUCTED

THE ENTIRE WALLED city of Ahmedabad was brought under indefinite curfew as the Army was called out to quell the violence that erupted resulting in nine deaths in police firing. Six companies of the Central Reserve Police airlifted from Delhi. This is the second time that the Army has been called in after March 19 todeal withthe violence that broke out following the "jail bharo" call issued by anti-reservationists.

THE MULTI-NATIONAL Union Carbide Corporation whose Bhopal pesticide plant

leaked poisonous gas last December killing over 2,000 people is deliberately trying todelay the proceedings of the judicial commission inquiring into the world's worst industrial disaster. Although almost a month has passed, the Carbide counsels have failed to file or produce the documents requested by other parties appearing on behalf of the gas victims.
