PM's visit to J&K to flag off Vande Bharat Express postponed

PRIME MINISTER Narendra Modi's scheduled visit toJammu and Kashmir to flag off the Vande Bharat Express from Katra to Srinagar on April 19 has been postponed. Sources said the visit was postponed amid a forecast of adverse weather conditions during the period. The India Meteorological Department office in Srinagar has forecast rain across Jammu and Kashmir from April 18, with the most significant impact expected on April 18 and 19.
