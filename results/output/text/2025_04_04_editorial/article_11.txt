STATES LOW ON FUNDS

MOSTOF THE states barring Maharashtra are facinga tight resource position despite a substantial increase in central assistance in the current year. The Centreis understood to have suggested mobilisation of at least Rs 2,000 crore of additional resources in the budgetsto be presented by the state governments in the next few weeks, or face acut. Some of the chief ministers, however, are stated to be reluctant to undertake extra resource mobilisation.
