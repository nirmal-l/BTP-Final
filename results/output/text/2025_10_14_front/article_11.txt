SIGNALLING A likely thaw in trade talks with the US, Indian negotiators are set to reach Washington this week to bridge differences with American negotiators and inch closer to a trade deal, a senior government official said Monday. This comes after newly appointed US Ambassador-designate Sergio Gor met Prime Minister Narendra Modi, External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar, as well as Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal over the weekend. While it's unclear if the upcoming meeting with the United States Trade Representative (USTR) will be a formal round of negotiations, India has expressed willingness to step up energy imports from the US to resolve Washington's concerns over New Delhi's imports of Russian oil.
