JUNE 5, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

DHAKA TALKS FAIL PM's 5-NATION TOUR

INDIAAND BANGLADESH failed to reach an agreement on sharing their common river, the Ganga, despite three days of talks in Dhaka, officials said. The talks had been called to work out an agreement on sharing the Ganga's waters and augmenting the river's flow during the lean season. Bangladesh foreign affairs adviser Humayun Rashid Choudhury termed the talks "inconclusive".
