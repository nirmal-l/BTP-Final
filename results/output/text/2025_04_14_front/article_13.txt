IITs, IMs and NITs among 17 top Central institutions being run by interim heads V-Cs, directors serving ininterim Capacity or on extended tenures

ATLEAST 17 of India's 130 premier higher education institutions under the Union Ministry of Education, including central universities, IITs, IIMs, NITs and IISERs, are being run by Vice-Chancellors and directors who are either serving in an interim capacity or on extended tenures. The Indian Express reviewed appointments to top posts at all the 48 Central Universities (CUs), 23 IITs, 21 IIMs, 31 NITs and

seven IISERs under the Ministry - and found interim heads in eight CUs, three IIMs, two IITs, three NITs and one IISER. All of them carry the tag of Institutions of National Importance. At least eight of these 17 institutions have been helmed by interim leaders for over a year: IIM Calcutta, IIM Shillong, NIT Srinagar, NIT Andhra Pradesh, SER Thiruvananthapuram, IGNOU, Sikkim University and Rajiv Gandhi University in Arunachal Pradesh.
