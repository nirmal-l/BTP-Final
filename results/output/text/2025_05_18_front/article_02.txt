AS PART of its efforts to rally global support for India's war against terror emanating from Pakistan and moving to isolate Islamabad internationally, the government announced Saturday that seven multi-party delegations would visit different countriesto convey India's message post the Pahalgam terror attack and Operation Sindoor. Three of these delegations will be headed by Opposition leaders.

The delegations will be headed by Congress's Shashi Tharoor, BJP's Ravi Shankar Prasad and Baijayant Panda, JD(U)'s Sanjay Kumar Jha, DMK's Kanimozhi, NCP (SP)'s Supriya Sule and Shiv Sena's Shrikant Eknath Shinde. The leaders - four from the ruling NDA and three from the Opposition INDIA bloc - are seasoned lawmakers with long stints in public life.
