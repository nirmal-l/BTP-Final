SUV driven by Cong leader runs amok on busy Jaipur street, 3 killed

AN SUV allegedly ploughed through a busy street in Jaipur on Monday night, killing three people and iniuring at least six others, According to police, around 10 pm, Usmaan Khan (62), a factory owner and a Congress leader, was driving his vehiclein the Walled City area, the lanes of which are typically congested. Police are probing if he was under the influence of alcohol. Following the incident, the Jaipur district Congress committee submitted a memorandumto Jaipur Collector Jitendra Soni, whichstated: "It was learnt from the newspapers that Usman Khan, the vice-president of Jaipur City District Congress Committee, ran over several people while driving recklessly, in which three people died and many were injured. Therefore, Jaipur City District Congress Committee demands from the state government that proper compensation be given to the dead and injured and the culprit be given the harshest punishment." The incident took place underthe jurisdiction of Nahargarh police station. SHO Rameshwari said, "The driver was caught while trying to escape... We detained him and conducted a medical examination. An investigation is underway and we will take action accordingly." Police said the accused hit several vehicles, including twowheelers, over a500-metre distance. He first hit a scooter near Santoshi Mata temple. Around 10.30 am on Tuesday, BJP MLA from Hawa Mahal, Balmukund Acharya, led a protest in front of the police station, demanding compensation for families of those dead.
