Don'ttake the rage bait, don't give in

NTHEbeginning there was "clickbait",a desperateattempt byonlinecontent creators to get viewers to fall for their pitch, hook, headlineand sinker. Now, itistheera ofthe "rage bait", plucked out from the clamour of the internet, crowned the word ofthe year by Oxford University Press (OUP), and defined as "onlinecontent deliberately designed toelicit angeroroutrage by being frustrating, provocative or offensive". In a culture in which provocation has become routine and ambient, "ragebait", first used online on Usenet in 2002, turfed out two close contenders - "aura farming" and "bio hack" - to institutionalise the zeitgeist. The linguistic elevation offers a portrait of the modern information ecosystem that is increasingly shaped by emotional manipulation.
