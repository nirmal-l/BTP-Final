APRIL 12, 1985, FoRTY YEARS AGO

QUOTA DEADLOCK DIALOGUE WITH AKALIS ELECTION REFORM

THE LEADERS OF the anti-reservation agitation in Gujarat ruled out negotiations with the state government unless the student leaders detained under the National Security Act are released unconditionally. The other two preconditions are revocation of the termination orders against the striking junior doctors of Ahmedabad Civil Hospital and cancellation of the appointments made in their place.

THE ELECTION COMMISSION will soon proposetwochanges in the law todisqualify persons with criminal records from contesting electionsand to combat the menaceofbooth capturing and proxy voting. In Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, several persons detained under the National Security Act or charged with serious offences contested the last Lok Sabhaand state elections, and some won comfortably.
