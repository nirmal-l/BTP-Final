In 2010, China's consumption-to-GDP ratio was 35 per cent. On every criterion, especially on the joint criteria of mercantilist indicators, China holds the world record for the level and the length of time it has maintained mercantilist policies, These policies have been successful. In 1960, the US was the only country "standing" post the devastation of World War II. Withthe rest of the world either poor or devastated, the US share of manufactured goods exports (MGE) reached 25 per cent. In 1996, 18 years after China began its economic reforms, China's MGE share was just 4 per cent. Today, this share is 30 percent with the rest of the world richer and more advanced than it has ever been. The US reaction to the emerging hegemon?
