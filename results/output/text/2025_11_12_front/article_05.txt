Trump: Will bring down tariffs on India, 'pretty close' to reaching deal

US PRESIDENT Donald Trump Tuesday said the US will bring down tariffs on India, and that Washington is "pretty close" to reachinga "fair trade deal" with New Delhi. India currently faces 50 per cent tariffs, the highest on any country globally, primarily dueto the 'penalty' duty of 25 per cent for purchasing Russian oil.
