PRIME MINISTER NARENDRA Mopi's FIRST ADDRESS TO THE NATION SINCE OPERATION SINDOOR:

IN HIS firstaddressto the nation since the commencement of Operation Sindoor and intense aerial strikes before the armed forces of India and Pakistan agreed to stop military actions, Prime Minister Narendra Modi drew the red lines for Pakistan Monday, promising counter-strikes as a response to acts of terror without differentiating between terrorists and the regime that sponsors them, and of not yielding to Islamabad's nuclear blackmail. Underlining that Indian military actions have only been suspended, not concluded, Modi said India will closely watch Pakistan's activities, and any future talks will only be on the issues of terror and Pakistan-occupied Kashmir. He told the nation that India decided to temporarily suspend its counter-operations against Pakistani terrorist and military installations because "Pakistan, in its appeal, assured it would cease all terrorist activities and military aggression against India".
