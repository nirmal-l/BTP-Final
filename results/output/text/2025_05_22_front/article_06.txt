Jal Shakti schemes: MPs meet today to discuss issues,

concerns

JALSHAKTI Minister CR Patil has invited around 60 Lok Sabha members to hold aninformal interaction Thursday on the points and concerns they had raised during a discussion on his Ministry's Demand for Grants for financial year 2025-26. The meeting, which will be held at the Parliament House Annexe building, is likely to see members discussing issues related to different schemes of the Ministry including the Jal Jeevan Mission and Namami Gange. The meeting comes ata time when the Centre has decided to send 100 teams of Central Nodal Officers (CNO) for ground inspection of Jal Jeevan Mission schemes across the country amid concerns over irregularities in several states.
