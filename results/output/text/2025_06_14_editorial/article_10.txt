US-INDIA DEALS

THEUS HASdecided to provide advanced military technology and weaponry in aneffortto end the 20-year hiatus in US military sales to theworld's largest democracy. The Washington Post reported that the new policy was conditional onIndianacceptance of strict safeguards. PM Rajiv Gandhi and Defence Minister PV Narasimha Rao will discuss the supply of US military technology to India with Defence Secretary Caspar Weinberger andjohn Messey, chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff.
