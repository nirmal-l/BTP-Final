But I never thought of it, just like you never think of your parents growing old. Now, that great disruption has come to pass. Dad has retired, my parents have packed up 40 years' worth of life and moved into a flat outside the campus. And I no longer know where my anchor is. To be a child from Bihar is to know you will leave your roots behind. But what do you do when your roots are not open to be revisited? Some other family lives in my childhood bedroom now, some other child probably talks to the trees my mum and] tended to. If the witness to your childhood disappears, does the childhood survive? To be the ambitious immigrant child ofan ambitious immigrant father is to lose your home twice over. But it is also to carry the strength of enterprising generations within you. It is to learn that home is within you, in your memories, in the love you've given and received. It is to know that people and places can belong to you, even ifyouneverbelong tothem.
