TOBEBORNin asmall town in Biharisto know that your success willbe measured by how far from the homeland you can "establish" yourself. Every town has an entire industry to polishits products for the world - English coaching classes, UPSC coaching classes, Physics-Chemistry-Maths coaching, bank-exam coaching. Nostalgia comes pre-baked into all your experiences; you know you will leave behind these lanes and haunts. And yet, you love. You love the town you are already slightly ashamed of, you love the school that is teaching you to leave without looking back, you love the streets where everyone knows your parents. Igrew up twice removed from my immediate surroundings. My dad, from a village in Bihar, workedin Jamshedpur(it was yetto become part of Jharkhand). Jamshedpur was unlike the rest of Bihar; an industrial town where the promise of jobs attracted talent from across the country. Growing up, I knew my city of beautiful parks and clubs andswimming pools was unlike Muzaffarpur or Bhagaipur or Paina with their inheritances of loss, But even within Jamshedpur, lived inside the National Institute of Technology(NIT)campus, cocooned off from the rest of the city.
