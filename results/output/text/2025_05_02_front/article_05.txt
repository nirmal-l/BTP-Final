Use your power to arrest sparingly, and not at an early stage: ASG to ED Says hasty arrest leads to default bail, courts faulted grounds many times

ADDITIONALSOLICITOR General S V Raju, who has appeared in courts for the Enforcement Directorate in many high-profile cases of alleged money laundering, cautioned the agency Thursday against hasty arrests, saying "use your power to arrest not liberally, but sparingly" and do "not use it at an early stage" ofacase. Addressing ED officials at an event to mark the agency's foundation day at the Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, Raju suggested that arrests made in the early stages of a case were not only counterproductive to investigation but also to the outcome of the casein courts.

"Arrest is very crucial for all agencies, But it is the threat of arrest that makes people disclose details ina case. After they are arrested, their conduct changes because the threat is over.
