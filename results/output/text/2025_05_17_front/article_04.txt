Pak goods in transit via third country being banned

THE DIRECTORATE of Revenue Intelligence (DRI), the anti-smuggling body under the Finance Ministry, has begun blocking in-transit goods originating from Pakistan that are being routed through third countries such as the UAE, a government official told The Indian Express.
