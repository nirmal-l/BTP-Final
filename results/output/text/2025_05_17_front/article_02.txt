UNDERLINING THAT environment conservation is key to development, the Supreme Court Friday struck down and held as "illegal" a 2017 Ministry of Environment notification that allowed grant of environmental clearance (EC) to projects ex-post facto i.e., after commencement of work. It also struck down a 2021 Office Memorandum(OM) issued by the Ministry in pursuance of the 2017 notification. A bench of Justices A S Oka and Ujjal Bhuyan also restrained

The 2017 amnesty was applicable for six months between March 2017 and September 2017.
