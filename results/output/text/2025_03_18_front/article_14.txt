THEIR HALL tickets secured to their transparent clipboards, four teenagers hopped into an open green trolley in Pithoragarh's Gharuri village with practised ease. Around 150 metres across the turbid Gori Ganga, a school teacher tugged the trolley's rope. Getting an education is no child's play in Uttarakhand's Gharuni village.
