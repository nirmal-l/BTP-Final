SEPTEMBER 16, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

LEADERS OF THE four-group Eelam National Liberation Front (ENLF) are leaving for New Delhi to meet the Prime Minister, Rajiv Gandhi, an ENLF spokesman told PTI. The ENLF received a message from the Prime Minister's office asking the militant leaders to come to Delhi for talks. According to ENLF sources, the Front leaders are likely to meet Gandhi after the prime minister returns from electioneering in Punjab.
