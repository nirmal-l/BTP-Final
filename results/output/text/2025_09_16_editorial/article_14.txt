WHEN SURYAKUMAR YADAV and his men decided not to shake hands with the Pakistan team at the Asia Cup, the last shaky distinction between sports and politics ended. It used to be said that sports shouldn't be mixed with politics, that players only want toget on with the game - "yeh sabsiyasat ka khel hai'. But in a game between nations that is kicked off with national anthems, the denial of the connection was hanging bya thin thread. When Yadav said that "the government, BCCI, we are all aligned", that thread snapped. Cricket, politics, and the politicking in the sport have all aligned now.
