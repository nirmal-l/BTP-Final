THE DAIRY TOWN of Anand was placed underan indefinite curfew following large-scale arson since daybreak. The police opened fire

twice during the day to quell rioting mobs indulging in violence and arson there. Over two dozen shops were set ablaze by the rampaging crowds in the town since moming.
