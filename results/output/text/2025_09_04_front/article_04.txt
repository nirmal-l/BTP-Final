Rajasthan Assembly passes Bill to regulate coaching centres

THE RAJASTHAN Assembly Wednesday passed the Bill to regulate coaching centres in the state amid criticism by Opposition MLAs who said that it fails to address the concerns raised earlier. Deputy Chief Minister and Higher Education Minister Prem Chand Bairwa called the Rajasthan Coaching Centres (Control and Regulation) Bill, 2025, "historic", saying that they are showing the way tothe coming generations, He said that in

the last four years, 88 students in coaching centres have died by suicide, ofwhich 70were in Kota, 14 in Sikar and rest in other places. "It is notjust a figure, but evidence of our collective failure," he said.
