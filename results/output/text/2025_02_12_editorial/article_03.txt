Trump is also reported to have talked about unveiling "reciprocal tariffs" over the next few days. The tariffs on steel and aluminium will hurt a large swathe of countries - Canada, Mexico, Brazil and South Korea are among those likely to be affected - and have already evoked strong reactions. Canada's industry minister is reported to have said that the country will give a "clear and calibrated" response, while Ursula von der Leyen, Presidentof the European Commission, has said that the imposition of tariffs "will not go unanswered".
