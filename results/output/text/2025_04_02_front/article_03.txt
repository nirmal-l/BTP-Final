Meet the girl who ran with books: 8 yrs, wants to become 'TAS adhikar?'

FOR EIGHT-YEAR-OLD Ananya Yadav, the schoolbag containing Hindi, English and "ginti ki" (maths)books is her most prized possession, a medium to one day become an "IAS adhikari" and "protect the country". So, whena shed close to where the bag was kept caught fire during ademolition drive in Uttar Pradesh's Ambedkar Nagar on March 21,

she rushed to save it. Little did Ananya know that a video of her running with the bag would catch the attention of thecountry'stop court. During a hearing on Tuesday, a Bench of Justices A SOka and Ujjal Bhuyan cited the video that is viral online and said it had "shocked everyone."
