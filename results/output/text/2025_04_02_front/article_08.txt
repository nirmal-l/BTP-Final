Delhi court orders FIR against Kapil Mishra on 2020 riots complaint

ACITY court Tuesday directed the registration of an FIR against BJPleader Kapil Mishra, now the Delhi Law and Justice Minister, onacomplaintalleginghe hada role in the 2020 riots in Northeast Delhi. Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate Vaibhav Chaurasiaallowed the application of the complainant, Yamuna Vihar resident Mohammad Ilyas, who had sought an FIR against Mishra and the then SHO of Dayalpur police station and five others, including BJP MLA Mohan Singh
