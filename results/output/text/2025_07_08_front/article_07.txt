Domicile certificates most in demand - but lakhs pending in a week

The irony: Aadhaar invalid as voter ID but needed for domicile certificate

AS VOTERS scramble to put together their papers for the Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision(SIR) drive for new Bihar electoral rolls, the document most in demand is No.6 on its listof 11 - adomicile or residential certificate. One reason for this is that applicants are largely being allowed to submit just Aadhaar - the most commonly available document - as proof of residence to geta domicile certificate.
