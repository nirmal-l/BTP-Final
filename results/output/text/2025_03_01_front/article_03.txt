For the full financial year FY25, the GDP growth rate, as per the second advance estimates, isseen marginallyhigher at 6.5 percent as against the 6.4 percent inthe firstadvanceestimates released in January. This is significant since it hascome despite a sharp upward revision of GDP data for FY24. As perthe latest estimates, GDP growth in FY24 has been revised up to 92 per cent from 8.2 per cent earlier.
