In India team's chess win, story of a recreational player from IM-Calcutta

IT WAS just another day for Atharvaa Tayade, a 24-year-old pursuing an MBA degree at IIM-Calcutta, when a message popped up on his chess.com account in March: "Weare participating as Team MGD1, would you be interested to play?" The message was from N

Srinath, the gold medal-winning captain of India's Budapest Olympiad team, seeking a "recreational player" for the only team from India at the FIDE World Rapid and Blitz Team Championships. The championship requires six players per team, with one compulsory spot each for a woman and a recreational player (someone who has never achieved FIDE Classical, Rapid or Blitz Rating of 2000 Elo points).
