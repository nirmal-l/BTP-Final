NOT SOlong ago, paralympic silver medallist Pranav Soorma would enter the Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium, seated in his wheelchair, and struggle to make his way to the throwing area over the bumps and potholes on the way. "But nowit has changed.I can propel my wheelchair smoothly and sometimes without anyone's assistance," Soorma, a silver medal winner in the club throw event at the Paris Paralympics, told The Indian Express.
