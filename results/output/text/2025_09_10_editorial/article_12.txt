SEPTEMBER 10, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

THE PRIME MINISTER, Rajiv Gandhi, asserted that the Congress (1) was fighting the Punjab elections seriously, would come back to power and provide an effective government to the state. It had put up candidates stronger than those of other parties, he said. Talking to a group of editors, Gandhi said that the complaint that the party had put up weaker candidates was from a group of senior partymen but Congress would be stronger in the field without them.
