US trade deal still work in progress, negotiators back from Washington Deal not contingent on any date, US unwilling to budge on steel: Official

THE INDIA-US interim trade deal remains elusive with less than a week to go before the July 9 reciprocal tariff pause runs out. Indian negotiators returned Friday, a government official said, adding that the negotiations are not contingent on any specific date and that both countries will sign a deal only if it is mutually beneficial. "The trade negotiators have just returned. The talks are ongoing, but the deal is not contingent on any date.
