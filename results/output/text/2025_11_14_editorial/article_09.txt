Cyclonic storm hits Madras

MADRAS CITY and the neighbouring Chengalpet district lay crippled as continuous downpour in the wake of a cyclonic storm left 63 people dead, more than 300,000 homeless and caused extensive damage as the Army, the Navy and the Coast Guard got cracking on rescue and relief operations.
