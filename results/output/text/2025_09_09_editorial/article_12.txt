SEPTEMBER 9, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

ELECTORAL ORDINANCE

THE PRESIDENT PROMULGATED an ordinance amending Section 52 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, to provide for the countermanding of an election only incase of the death of a candidate set up by a recognised political party. The effect of the ordinance, which limits the scope of Section 52, is that an election will not be countermanded henceforth in the event of the death of an independent candidate.
