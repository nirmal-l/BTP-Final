PM invited to Gaza summit in Egypt today, India sends MoS as special envoy

New Delhi's decision follows summit invitation to Pakistan PM as well

INDIA HAS sent Kirti Vardhan Singh, Minister of State for External Affairs, as the Prime Minister's special envoy to the Gaza peace summit being held Monday at Sharm el-Sheikh in Egypt. In a post on X Sunday night, Singh said,: "Arrivedin the historic city of Cairo as special representative of Prime Minister Narendra Modi to attend the Gaza Peace Summit at Sharm el-Sheikh." The decision to send Singh was taken after Modi received a last-minute invitation Saturday from US President Donald Trump and Egypt President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi to attend the summit at Sharm el-Sheikh.
