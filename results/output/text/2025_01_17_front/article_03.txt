Satellites dock inspace, ISRO gets a boost for Moon & beyond

SPADEX MISSION ASUCCESS India becomes fourth country after US, Russia and China to achieve feat

THE INDIAN Space Research Organisation(ISRO)on Thursday moming docked two satellitesin space, demonstrating acapability that iscritcal to executing the more ambitious future missions like the building ofa permanent space station or landing humans on the Moon. With the successful docking of the two satellites - Chaser and Target - as partof the Space Docking Experiment (SpaDeX), India has become the fourth country after the US, Russia and China to accomplish the feat.
