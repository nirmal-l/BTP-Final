Remembering Nehru

THE NATION remembered Pandit Jawaharlal Nehruon his 96th birth anniversary, also celebrated as Children's Day, througha variety of functions ranging from aseminar recallinghis contribution tothe country and an exhibition onhis life and legacy to paintingcompetitions, cultural programmes anacainivats for children. The tributes began with floral offer-ings at his samadhi, Shantivana, early inthe morning. Among those who placed flowers at the samadhi were his grandson, the Prime Minister, Rajiv Gandhi, andhis family, the President, Zail Singh, the Vice President, Ramaswamy Venkataraman, and the Lok Sabha Speaker, Balram Jakhar.
