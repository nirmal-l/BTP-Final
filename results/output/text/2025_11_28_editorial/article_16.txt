Golden Temple priest shot

THEHEAD priest of the Golden Temple, Giani Sahib Singh, sustained bulletinjuries when an attempt was made on his life in the temple parkarma by three unidentified assailants. His bodyguard, Nanak Singh, who was following him witha stun gun in his hand, was killed on the spot. Two of the three assailants opened fire from revolvers as Giani Sahib Singh bowed towards the sanctum sanctorum of the most sacred Sikh shrine at about 4.40 am.
