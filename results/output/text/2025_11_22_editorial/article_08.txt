Trump's Ukraine plan crosses Kyiv's red lines

S PRESIDENT Donald Trump's 28-point plan to end the war in Ukraine offers substantial concessions to Russia. In mid-October, he had urged Kyiv and Moscow to "stop where they are" and proposed an end to the conflict by allowing the Donbas region, the majority of which is under Russian control, to "be cut the way it is". The new plan advances that proposal. Its first sub-point under item 21 states: "Crimea, Luhanskand Donetsk will be recognised as de facto Russian, including by the US."
