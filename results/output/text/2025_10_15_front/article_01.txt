20 feared dead after bus on way to Jodhpur catches fire in Jaisalmer

A MOVING AC sleeper bus caught fire on the Jaisalmer-Jodhpur Highway in Jaisalmeron Tuesday, and around 20 people are feared dead, while several others have been referred to hospitals in Jodhpur, officials said. The fire quickly engulfed the vehicle, and according to eyewitnesses, passengers jumped from the moving bus to escape. Jaisalmer District Collector has requested family members of the passengers to come forward toidentify the deceased.
