As a result, though power relations are segregated along caste lines, sustained social dialogue on everyday experiencesof caste remains absent, precluding the emergence of an effective and power-fulDalit counter-culture. Such a scenario allows the practice of different forms of everyday casteism, like the frequent use of casteist slurs like cramar and chotolok. The fact that most people use these words casually, often unaware of their casteist connotations, shows the extent of casual casteism. Though caste riots are not frequent in Bengal, whenever it happens, it's conveniently presented as a law-and-order problem.
