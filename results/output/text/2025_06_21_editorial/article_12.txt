ATLEAST SEVEN people, including amember of Parliament, were killed and over 20 injured in five bomb explosions which rocked the Nepalese capital. The bombs went off at the west and south gates of King Birendra's palace, in the lobby ofthe Parliament building and the central secretariat and a five star hotel, Annapoorna, partly owned by the royal family.
