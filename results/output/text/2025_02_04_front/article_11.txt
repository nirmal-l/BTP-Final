Retired soldier killed, wife & niece injured in Kashmir militant attack

ANEX-SERVICEMAN waskilled, and his wife and niece were injured, when gunmen opened fire at them in south Kashmir's Kul gam on Monday. Thisis the first militant attack in the Valley thisyear According to police sources, the unidentified gunmen shot at theex-serviceman, identified as retired Lance Naik Manzoor Ahmad Wagay (45), and his family outside their residence at Behibagh villagein Kul gam. The bullets hit Wagay, his wife and her niece. While Wagay was hit intheabdomen, the two women suffered wounds in the legs. All three were immediately

taken to hospital where Wagay succumbed to his injuries. The other twoare in stable condition.
