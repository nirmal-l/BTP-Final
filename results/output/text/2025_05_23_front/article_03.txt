Modi's mind stays cool, but blood runs hot, sindoor in my veins: PM in Bikaner

'OUR ENEMIES SAW WHAT HAPPENS WHEN SINDOOR TURNS INTO BAAROOD'

Befitting reply for each terror attack, will view Pak's state, non-state actors as one: PM

PRIME MINISTER Narendra Modi said on Thursday that the Indian Armed Forces made Pakistan kneel during Operation Sindoor and that the world and enemies of the country have now seen what happens when "sindoor (vermilion) turns to gunpowder", adding that garam sindoor runs through his veins now, He said India's response is a "new form of justice" and emphasised that there will be no trade or talks with Pakistan now. Significantly, the PM said that the recent developments have made three things clear: that there will be a befitting reply for each terror attack; that India will not be scared by threats of an atom bomb; and that India will view Pakistan's "state and non-state actors" as one.
