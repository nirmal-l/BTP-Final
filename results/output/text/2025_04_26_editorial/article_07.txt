APRIL 26, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

BYPOLL RESULTS

NON-CONGRESS (1) PARTIES HAVE apparently reversed the Congress(1) performance in the recent Lok Sabha and assembly elections, winning 12 of the 21 assembly seats and one of the two Lok Sabha seats, Of the three Lok Sabhaelections, polling for which was held along with 36 assembly elections, Congress (I) won one, leaving one to Janata.

THE BHOPAL PESTICIDE plant of Union Carbide, closed since the December 3 disaster in which over 2,000 persons died, has

sacked 44 ofits officers and members of supervisory staff. Plant sources told ENS the sacked officers have been retrenched with effect from April 26. Notices terminating their services were served on April 22. The sacked officers have been assured that their provident fund will be paid within amonth.
