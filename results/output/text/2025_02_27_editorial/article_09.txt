Raj NARAIN MISSING DU POLICE STATION INDIA BEATS ENGLAND A VOTE AGAINST ZIA

DISTRICT AUTHORITIES IN Bharatpur have launched a vigorous search for Rashtriya Janata Party leader Raj Narain, who has been reportedly missing after the Deeg firing incident on February 24. In an official statement, the state government said that Raj Narain delivered an inflammatory speech on February 24 when the police fired on a violent mob resulting in the death of three peopl. District Collector Salauddin Ahmed said that if Raj Narain was traced, he would be arrested under the National Security Act.

IN WHAT APPEARS to be a clear rejection of

Gen Zia-ul-Haq's eight years of martial law, Pakistan voters havecast their ballots against many important federal ministers and supporters of the present regime. As the results ofthe restricted, non-party elections trickled in, Zia announced the "dissolution" of his cabinet but in fact only effected a reshuffle in which all the ministers who contested were dropped andthose whodid nat were retained.
