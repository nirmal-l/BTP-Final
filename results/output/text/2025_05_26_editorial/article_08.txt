May 26, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

AN EARLY ACCORD on the Mizo issue is expected after the return of Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi from the Soviet Union. Congress-I chief minister of Mizoram, Lal Thanhawla, whohas been in the Capital, has been asked to stay in Delhi till the arrival of the Prime Minister. The accord was earlier scheduled to be announced during Gandhi's visit to Mizoram on May 10, but it was cancelled.

THE CENTREIS likelytoconvene a meeting of the chief ministers sometime later this year

to discuss the knotty issue of reservation for the backward classes, which had resulted in agitations in Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh. However, before such ameeting, all efforts will be made to create a consensus on the overall policy of reservation. The idea is first to prepare the political ground fora consensus.
