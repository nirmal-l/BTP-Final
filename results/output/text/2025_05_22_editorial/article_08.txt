ON APRIL22, when Pakistan-linked terrorists attacked tourists in Pahalgam, it was a strike on our national conscience. Their brutality shocked thecountry. Earlier, they hadtargeted military posts - these were alsoacts ofterror - but this time, they attacked innocent civil-ians. Everyone, irrespective of party, stood by the government. A strong response was needed. Indian forces carried out precision strikes on terror bases in Pakistan, followed by strikes on airbases in response to Pakistani escalation. Subsequently, we accepted Pakistan's ceasefire proposal, But we can hardly believe its assurances. Past instances show that Pakistan has never kept its promises. The wide gap between whatit says during diplomatic meet-ings and its actions revealsits unpredictability. On May 23, 2019, after the results of the Indian general elections, Imran Khan, then Pakistan's Prime Minister, tweeted, "I congratulate Prime Minister Modi on the electoral victory of the BJP and allies. Look forward to working with him for peace, progress and prosperity in South Asia." However, such commitments didn't find any resonance on the ground.
