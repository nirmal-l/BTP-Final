In Saudi Arabia, we have seen how games can bridge cultures and create opportunities. Guided by HRH the Crown Prince's Vision 2030, we are fostering a unified, nationwide gaming strategy. As such, the summit marks a timely opportunity to deepen ties between our countries and shape a future led by new voices andalliances, Under the guidance of Vision 2030, Saudi Arabia views games and esports as key pillars of our future economy. Our National Gaming andEsports Strategy, launched in 2022, aims to transform the Kingdom into a leading global hub by 2030, with concrete goals including over 39,000 jobs, a $13-billion GDP contribution, and more than 100 professional teams already active among our 21 million gamers.One of our flagship initiatives, the Esports Worid Cup, announced by His Royal Highness Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in 2023, debutedin Riyadh with 200clubs, 1,500 athletes, two million visitors, and 500 million viewers. Beyond competition, the Esports World Cup symbolises how innovation, investment, and international collaboration can come together to shape a thriving, future-focused industry. This goal of nurturing global parmerships is reflected in gatherings like the WAVES Summit and events such as the New Global Sport Conference (NGSC), returning in 2025

As we showcase our unified gaming strategy at WAVES, our message is one of partnership and possibility. We are demonstrating how government support, private investment, and global cooperation can work together. Saudi Arabia is excited to collaborate with India and other innovators to shape a future where gaming and esports reflect the diversity and ambition of the Global South.
