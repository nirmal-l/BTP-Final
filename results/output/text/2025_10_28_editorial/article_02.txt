This is in addition to Rs 675 crore for district-level road expansion and Rs 3,822 crore for the four-lane Sahebganj-Areraj-Bettiah corridor. Six more connectivity projects will target logistics efficiency and improved border access, Rail infrastructureis receiving unprecedented capital injection. Projects worth Rs 2,192 crorefordoubling the Bakhtiyarpur-Rajgir-Tilaiya line, seven new train servicesincluding Amrit Bharat Express routes, and nearly Rs 1 lakh crore in ongoing rail capacity projects, including the planned high-speed Buxar-Bhagalpur corridor, have been undertaken. Bihar's aviation map is being redrawn through a state-AAl agreement for six regional airports. There are also plans for 15 operational facilities and agreenfield international airport near Patna. These physical linkages augur well for a tourism expansion. However, there is no coherent strategy that can draw on Bihar's rich cultural endowments toattract domestic and foreign interest.
