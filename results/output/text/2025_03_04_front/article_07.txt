EMPOWERING STUDENTS in Arunachal Pradesh with technology so that the classroom becomesa hub for innovation, helping farmers in Tamil Nadu take their produce to the world, floatingcommunity-based water conservation efforts in Jharkhand, starting projects for women in Odisha - these are some of the works undertaken by District Magistrates across India that are among those to be acknowledged at The Indian Express Excellence in Governance Awards in New Delhi on Tuesday.
