BLACK Box MYSTERY

THE IRISH GOVERNMENT said that it was doubtful that the "black box" flight recorders had been located at the crash site of the Air India jumbo jet. In London, Britain's defence ministry maintained that the Irish had been over-hasty in concluding that faint, intermit-tentsignals picked up by a British naval seabed operations vessel, HMS Challenger, came from the recorders.
