Marcu 20, 1985, ForTY YEARS AGO

ARMY IN AHMEDABAD ATTACHE STILL MISSING ZIA MAKES CHANGES

THE ARMY WAS called out in Ahmedabad and the entire walled city was placed under an indefinite curfew following innumerable incidents of looting and arson reported from the city as asequel to the killing of six people in groupclashesand police firings in the Dariapurand Kalupur localities. The police fired 40 rounds and more than 360 shells of tear gas.

PAKISTAN MILITARY RULER Mohammad Ziaul Haq decreed a constitutional amendmentsaying thatall orders heissued after he seized power in 1977 would not be repealed or altered without a presidential sanction. He issued another decree, before the openingof anew Parliament relaxing his own stiff conditions for future constitutional amendments.

BUDGET & INFLATION CONTRARY TO THE assurances being given by the government, the Union budget is bound to have an inflationary impact. Over and above the huge budgetary deficit, the Union govemment has advésed all publicsector units to generate additional internal resources to finance their plan programmes.

The search was extended to cover every nook andcranny of the city and policemen visited hotels, guest houses and privatenursing homes.
