Not required by law to provide list of those omitted from draft electoral roll: EC to SC

THE ELECTION Commission has informed the Supreme Court that it was not required by lawto publish alist of electors whose names have beenremoved from theelectoral rolls of Bihar, opposinga petitioner's plea for it to release the names and details of the 65 lakh electors who didnot makeit tothe draft published on August 1. Inreply to an application filed bytheAssociation for Democratic Reforms (ADR), the EC said the draft roll had been published on the basis of the enumeration forms received and that "no inquiry" isdone at this stage.
