For second year, no death penalty passes SC test AJOY SINHA KARPURAM

REFLECTINGA divergenceindecision-making on death penalty at different levels in the judiciary, the Supreme Court - forthe second consecutive year - did not confirm a single death sentence in 2024 while trial courts awarded 139 such verdicts, according to a report by Project 39A, a criminal justice programme at the National Law University Delhi. The SC heard six appeals in 2024 - it commuted five death sentences to life imprisonment
