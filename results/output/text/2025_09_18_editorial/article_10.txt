DRAFT SEVENTH PLAN

THE PLANNING COMMISSION has approved the draft Seventh Plan. The commission which met under the chairmanship of the Prime Minister, Rajiv Gandhi, envisaged a total public sector outlay of Rs 180,000 crore. Apart from the full-time members of the commission, the meeting was attended by the Defence Minister, PV Narasimha Rao, the Minister of State for Planning, K R Narayanan, V P Singh, the Finance Minister, and Buta Singh, the Agriculture Minister, who is also a member of the planning body.
