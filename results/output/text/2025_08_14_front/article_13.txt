15 ventilators at neurosurgery dept of Delhi's Lok Nayak, but only 1 working

ONLY ONE out of 15 ventilators is currently functional in the neurosurgery department of the Delhi government's 2,153-bed Lok Nayak Hospital, the largest such facility in the National Capital, official records reviewed by The Indian Express show. Weekly status reports main-tained by the hospital show there were 14 ventilators in the department till Tuesday - none of them was functional. One functional ventilator was added on Wednesday.
