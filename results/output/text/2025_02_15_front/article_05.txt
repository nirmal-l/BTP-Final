INDIA AND the United States have agreed to take steps towards making Washington "a leading supplier of oil and gas to India", which could help bridge the trade deficit between the two countries, US President Donald Trump said after his meeting with Prime Minister Narendra Modi in Washington Thursday. Trump added that the US will "hopefully," be India's top oiland gas supplier. India is already a significant buyer of American oil and gas. The USis India's fifth-largest supplier of crude oil, although its market share has been in low single digits.
