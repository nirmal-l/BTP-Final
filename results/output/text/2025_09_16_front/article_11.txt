Nearly 45% of Quality Control Orders issued in 3 years, MSMEs complain

IN JUST three years, the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) has issued as many as 84 Quality Control Orders (QCOs) based on the recommendations of various ministries and departments, which is almost 45% of all 187 QCOs administered by it. These 84QCOs cover 343 products. QCOs are legal orders issued by departments or ministries

under the BIS Act, which make it mandatory for domestic and imported products, to conform with certain quality standards. The products cannotbe sold unless they get a certification from the BIS.
