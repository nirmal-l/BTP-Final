APRIL 22, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

THE REPRESENTATIVES OF 83 non-aligned countries,meeting in New Delhi, adopted a declaration stating that they will not only impose sanctions against South Africa but also step up assistance to the South West African People's Organisation. The meeting of the coordinating bureau of non-aligned countries on the question of Namibia thus leads to the intensification of the movement on all fronts against South Africa
