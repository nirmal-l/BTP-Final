WEIGHING ITS response to the Pahalgam terror attack, the government is working on a multipronged military strategy to reestablish deterrence against Pakistan's hostile behaviour, The Indian Express has learnt. This has been the main and clear brief given to the military and security establishment by the political top brass over the last week of consultations. The process, sources said, will look at all credible options, includinga military retaliation, but the objective of "re-establishing deterrence" is firmly set. There is a growing realisation within the top levels of the government that the deterrence established by the Balakot air strike in February 2019 has "wornoff", and itis time tore-establish that, sources said.
