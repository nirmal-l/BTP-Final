Noida villagedacoity

AGANG of about 10 dacoits armed with country-made guns and sticks raided and looted at least 10 huts in the fields of Chilla village near Noida. The dacoits who brought ropes with them tied upand beat up at least seven persons, including three women, during their hour-long raid. They escaped with gold jewel-lery, clothes and cash worth several thousand rupees.
