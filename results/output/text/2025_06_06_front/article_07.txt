Made-in-India Rafale fuselage from 2027-28: Tata, Dassault ink pact for Hyderabad facility

IN A boost to defence and aero-space manufacturing in India, the country is set to become the first nation outside France where the fuselage of the Rafale fighter aircraft will be manufactured. Undera partnership between Dassault Aviation and Tata Advanced Systems (TASL), the latter will set upa "cutting-edge" production facility in Hyderabad for the manufacture of key structural sections of the Rafale, including the lateral shells of the rear fuselage, the complete rear section, the central fuselage, and the front section.
