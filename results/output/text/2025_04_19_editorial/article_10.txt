'KHALISTANI GUESTS INDO-PAK MEETING ANTI-RESERVATION STIR

A RULING PARTY member, K K Tiwari, told the Lok Sabha that some well-known protagonists of Khalistan were guests at Rashtrapati Bhavan some time ago: Deedar Singh Bains and Bhajan Singh Jogi, who had "dubious links" with J S Chauhan and GS Dhillon, had stayed in the complex in 1983.
