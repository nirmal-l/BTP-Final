Delhi wiil take on Railways for the last group match on Thursday to Sunday, a match that now finds itselfin the spotlight due to Kohli's presence as the stadium looks set to break theattendance record for recent Ranji games. Tuesday was a sign of things tocome. At 9 am, Pradeep Rana, a Delhi Police Sub-Inspector assigned on stadium duty, was taken by surprise at the number of mediapersons gathered outside the Virender Sehwag Gate. He made a quick call to his supe-nior: "Do log hi hai, zyada security fag sakti hai, kuch aur log bhej do (We are only two persons here, we may need more security, please send more people}." A few minutes later, the buzz outside the stadium grew as Kohli made his entrance. And the buzz remained for the next few hours, as he trained, joked, chatted and bonded -- with his team, manager, some former teammates whohad dropped by, a few familiar faces, but mostly new.
