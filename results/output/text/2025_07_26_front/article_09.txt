1in 3 US smartphone imports now made in India, China's lead shrinks

INDIA'S SHARE in US smartphone imports surged to nearly 36 per cent in the first five months of 2025, from about 11 per cent in 2024. China, which continues to dominate the product category, saw its share drop from 82 per cent to 49 per cent over the same period.
