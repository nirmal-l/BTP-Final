OCTOBER 30, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

BARNALA ON CBI CASES CONG LEADER KILLED

THEUNION FINANCE Ministry has compulsorily retired four commissioners of income tax and action hasbeen initiated against four others. The retirements were effected under fundamental rule 56 (J), which empowers the Govemmient to retire officials on attainmentof

50years ofage. Highly placed ministrysources said a list of 30 commissioners of income-tax (CIT) has been drawn up for action. Sixteen inspecting assistant commissioners (IAC) have been picked upfordismissal. Over 100income-tax officers have also been listed for action to give the department what ministry circles de-Scribed as a "clean and efficient" image.

THE PUNJAB CHIEF Minister, Surjit Singh Barnala, said the state government could not withdraw cases investigated and prosecuted by the Central Bureauof investigation. The Chief Minister was replying to a supplementary on his statementin the Vidhan Sabha on a call attention notice of Kirpal Singh (Janata Party )concerning the reported statement of the Prime Minister thatthe persons invoived in confrontation with the Army would not be freed.

FISHERMEN ROBBED

TWOSUSPECTED TERRORISTS shot dead Yash Paul, a Congress (I) leader of Naushehra Pannuan in Amritsar district, a report received in Chandigarh said. Yash Paul, who was proceedingtoTam Taranon ascooter, wasattacked near Pidi village. Paul, a trader, died on the spot. The assailants escaped on his scooter. Senior police officials have reached the scene.
