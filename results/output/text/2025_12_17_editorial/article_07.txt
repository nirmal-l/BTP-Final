When the Right to Education (RTE) Act was being drafted, there was hope that it wouldoutlaw the assignment ofnon-teaching work to teachers. However, it included Section 27, which permitted the use of teachers in election-related work. The glimmer of hope that RTE would establish teaching asa serious profession faded with time.
