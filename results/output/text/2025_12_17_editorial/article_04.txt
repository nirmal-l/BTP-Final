RAJAMANDALA BY C RAJA MOHAN

HIS YEAR marks the 100th anniversary of two very different organisations that have shaped modern India: The Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) and the Communist Party of India (CPI). The contrast in their centenary momentscould not be starker. The RSS, now the establishment, celebrated its milestone with fanfare. The CPI, once a formidable force in national life, passed its 100th year almost unnoticed - too diminished even to mount a modest commemoration. That silence invites a larger question: Has Indian communism reached the end of its long political journey, or is its apparent eclipse merely another phase in a turbulent history?
