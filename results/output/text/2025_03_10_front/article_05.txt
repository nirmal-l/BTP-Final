16 days after tunnel collapse in Telangana, one body recovered

ON THE 16th day of the search for the eight men trapped in a collapsed tunnel in Telangana, rescuers on Sunday recovered the body of one of them. He was identified as Gurpreet Singh from Tam Taran in Punjab. Telangana Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy announced ex gratia of Rs 25 lakh tohis family. The eight men had been trapped since the Srisailam Left Bank Canal (SLBC) tunnel in Nagarkumool partiallycollapsed on February 22.
