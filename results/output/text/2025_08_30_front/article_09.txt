EIGHT OPPOSITION-ruled "like-minded" states - Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and West Bengal - which met in New Delhi on Friday said they explicitly and unambiguously supported the Centre's GST rate rationalisation proposal, but were concerned about revenue loss. In a joint statement, the states projected revenue loss of

between Rs 85,000 crore and Rs 2 lakhcrore a year and sought "protection of revenue interest and fiscal stability in a federal structure". They said states should be compensated for revenue loss - anythinglower than 14 percent revenue growth - for aminimum five years. States were also worried if benefits of rate rationalisation will actually reach the common people or lead to "windfall profits" for just a few companies.
