THE MINISTRY of Defence (MoD) on Friday sanctioned the grantof free medical facilities under the Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme (ECHS) to officer cadets who are medically discharged for suffering varying degrees of disabilities during military training. The decision comes as a huge relief for these cadets who were not eligible for ECHS benefits so far.
