Rescue teams were conducting searches for people who were reported missing. District Collector Jitendra Dudi said: "Six suffered serious injuries but their condition is stable. Some are reported to be missing as per the local villagers' account." The district administration said search operations led by the NDRF will continue through the night. "We will continue the search till we find the last body.
