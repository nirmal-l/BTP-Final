RAVIDUTTA MISHRA & SHUBHAJIT ROY NEW DELHI, APRIL 11

TOMAKEGOOD the pause on reciprocal tariffs announced byUS President Donald Trump, India is aiming to sign an interim trade agreement with the US during the 90-day window, a senior government official said. "An agreement is possible within 90 days if it's a win-win for both sides. By then, the broad contours will be finalised. The Terms of Reference (ToR) have been signed, and India is significantly ahead of other countries seeking a deal with the US," said the official, who did not wish to be identified.
