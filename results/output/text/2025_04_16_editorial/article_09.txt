APRIL 16, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

TRIPURA OBSERVED A complete bandh to condemn the activities of the Tribal National Volunteers(TNV), which killed nine security forces personnel in the first week of the monthat Saikar, about 140 km from Agartala. While the ruling Left Front had given a call for a 24-hour bandh, the Congress (1) had called for a bandh for only half the period, starting from 6 amin the morning.

CREW TESTIMONY THE CREW OF the twolndian Airlines planes that were hijacked to Lahore in 1981 and

1984 will leave for Lahore on April 17 to depose as witnesses in the trial of 14 hijackers now being held there. The decision to send the crew to Lahore was taken at the highest level in the government following an assurance from the Pakistani authorities that they would be provided withall security. The deposition of the airline's crew is very important inthe case as the outcome of the trial will depend on their evidence.
