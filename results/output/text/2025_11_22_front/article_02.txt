4 LABOUR CODES REPLACE 29 CENTRAL LAWS; PM HAILS ECOSYSTEM TO PROTECT WORKER RIGHTS, SPUR GROWTH

Aanchal Magazine & RaviDutta Mishra New Delhi, November 21

CLEARED BY Parliament more than five years ago, four labour codes covering wages, industrial relations, social security and working conditions were implemented by the Centre Friday, signalling the biggest and long-overdue overhaul of worker laws in the country. Welcoming the announcement, Prime Minister Narendra Modi described the codes as "one of the most comprehensive and progressive labour-oriented reforms since Independence".
