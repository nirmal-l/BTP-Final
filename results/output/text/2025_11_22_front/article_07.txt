Since 2002 SIR, steepest rise in voters in Bengal's border dists

VOTER ROLL REVISION UNDERWAY IN POLL-BOUND STATE

Ravik Bhattacharya Kolkata, November 21

BETWEEN 2002, when the Special Intensive Revision(SIR) was last conducted by the Election Commission of India, and 2025, West Bengal has seen a 66% increase in the number of registered voters - from 4.58 crore to 7.63 crore. Eighteen districts that comprised the state then, and ECI data shows that of the top 10 with the highest increase in the number of voters, nine districts border Bangladesh.
