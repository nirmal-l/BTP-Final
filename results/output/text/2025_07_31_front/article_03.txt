PRESIDENT DONALD Trump's announcement on Wednesday of a25 percent tariff rate on India from August 1 has triggered concerns in the government that electronics exports to the United States, particularly the growing smartphone exports - which currently attract no customs duty - couldbe affected. A senior government official said a review meeting on Section 232 of theUS Trade Expansion Act of 1962 is coming up in two weeks, after which there will be more clarity on whether smartphone exports could be impacted. In 2018, the US had imposed an additional tariffof 25 percentand 10 per cent on steel and aluminum respectively, globally underthesame provision, which had impacted Indian exports.
