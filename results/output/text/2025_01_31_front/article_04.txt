As bodies start returning home, grief echo at site of stampede

MUDDY FOOTWEAR, some single, some in pairs, are strewn near the Sangam ghat. So are colourful clothes and backpacks ~ telltale signs of the stampede thatclaimed atleast 30 lvesaday agoand left another 60 injured at the MahaKumbh in Prayagraj. Asa dense fog envelops the ghat, families and friends move in single file, holding on to a security rope and looking behind nervously tocheck on their loved ones.
