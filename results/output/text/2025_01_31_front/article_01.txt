India braces for AI challenge, Govt says own foundational model in 4-8 months SOUMY ARENDRABARIK

DAYS AFTER an upstart Chinese artificial intelligence (Al) lab launched DeepSeek, a low-cost foundational model, India has decided to build adomestic large language model (LLM )ofits own as part of the Rs 10,370 crore IndiaAI Mission, Union IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said Thursday. The goverment has also selected 10 companies to supply 18,693 graphics processing units or GPUs - high end chips needed to develop machine leaming tools - that can go into developing a foundational model. This is more than the initial aim of the IndiaAl Mission,

under which the government was looking to procure 10,000 GPUs.

The companies empaneled to provide the GPU services include Jio Platforms, the Hiranandani Group-backed Yotta, Tata Communications, E2E Networks, NxtGen Datacenter, CMS Computers, Ctrls Datacenters, Locuz Enterprise Solutions, Orient Technologies, and Vensysco Technologies.
