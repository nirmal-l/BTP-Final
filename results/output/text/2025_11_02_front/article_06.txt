Maoists now down to just 3 districts, India will soon be rid of menace: PM

STATING THAT India would "soon be rid of the Naxal menace", Prime Minister Narendra Modi said Saturday that the number of districts affected by Maoists has dwindled from 125 to just three in the past 11 years. In his speech at the Chhattisgarh Rajat Mahotsav in Nava Raipur to mark the 25th anniversary of the state's formation, Modi also took a veiled dig at the Congress and Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi, saying those who "showed off a copy of the Constitution and shed crocodile tears inthe name of social justice had, for decades, done injustice" by leaving the State to "suffer".
