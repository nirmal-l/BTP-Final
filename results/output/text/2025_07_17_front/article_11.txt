Indonesia pact gives us full access, India deal on same lines: Trump 'FEELS LIKE HOME'

AS INDIAN negotiators engage with the US in extended talks for an interim deal this week, President Donald Trump said Wednesday that the US has signed a trade deal with Indonesia, opening the country to trade withthe US, and that an agreement "on the same lines" is being worked on with India. After what Hasan Nasbi, spokesperson for the Indonesian President, called an "extraordinary struggle" by their negotiators, the US and Indonesia announced that the reciprocal tariffs faced by the Southeast Asiancountry would be reduced from 32 per cent to 19 per cent.
