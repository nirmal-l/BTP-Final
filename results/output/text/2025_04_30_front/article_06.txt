HAVING FACED a tough battle in negotiating a statement at the UN Security Council where Pakistan is currently a non-permanent member, India has reached out to the other nonpermanent members of the UNSCas part of its diplomatic offensive in the wake of the Pahalgam terror attack. Jaishankar spoketo Foreign Ministers

Jaishankar spoke to Foreign Ministers of Algeria, Greece, Guyana, Panama, Sierra Leone, Slovenia and Somalia - all are currently on the UNSC fora twoyear term as non-permanent members.

Agreed onthe importance of accountability. India is resolved that the perpetrators, planners and backers of this attack are brought to justice."
