'Very, very disturbing': SC stays Lokpal decision to probe HC judges ANANTHAKRISHNANG

CALLING IT "something very, very disturbing", the Supreme Court Thursday stayed the Lokpal's January 27 order which held it had the power to look into complaints against sitting judgesof High Courts. The top court had taken suo motu cognizance of theJanuary 27 order and listed it for hearing before a bench headed by Justice B R Gavai and also comprising Justices Surya Kant andA S Oka "Something very, very disturbing," Justice Gavaisaid as the bench issued notice to the Centre, Lokpal and the complainant in the matter. It directed its registrar judicial "to mask the identity of the complainant and serve him through the Registrar Judicial of the High Court where the complainant resides". Itinjuncted the complainant from disclosing the name of the judge against whom thecomplaint had been filed, or from disclosing contents of the complaint.
