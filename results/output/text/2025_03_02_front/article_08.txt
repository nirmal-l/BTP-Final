Beawar case: Shutdown in Ajmer, right-wing groups seek CBI probe

AJMER OBSERVED a complete shutdown on Saturday as protests erupted, demanding strict action against the accused in the alleged Beawar rape and blackmail case. Various Hindu right-wing organisations demanded a CBI inquiry into the matter. A massive 'Aakrosh protest' rally was organised in the city, culminating at the collector's office around 11:30 AM.
