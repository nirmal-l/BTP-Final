HC quashes Karnataka move to drop cases of 2022 Hubballi riots

THE KARNATAKA High Court on Thursday struck down the state govemment's decision to withdraw 43 criminal cases, including those related to the 2022 riots in Hubballi. A division bench of Chief Justice N V Anjaria and Justice KV Aravind passed the order in response toa public interest litigation (PIL) filed by advocate Girish Bhardwaj. 'The Government Order dated 15.10.24 is hereby set aside. It is declared that the order shall stand non est (non-existentor invalid) from inception. Consequences in law shall follow," Chief Justice Anjaria said. The detailed order is awaited. The Government Order withdrawing the cases against the Hubballi riots accused, pro-Kannada activists, and farmer leaders, among others, had set offa political controversy when it was passed last October. The BJP had staged protests and accused the state's ruling Congress of "minority appeasement". The Congress had defended the move, saying the government was following "due process".
