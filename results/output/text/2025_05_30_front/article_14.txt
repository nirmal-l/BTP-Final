Rising obesity, hypertension among students, more in pvt schools: Study

RED-FLAGGING the rising rates of obesity and hypertension among school-going children, a new study by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences({Al-IMS) has found that the prevalence of obesity was as high as 24.02 per centin private schools in Delhi, more than five times the number for public schools. According to the study, which looked at 3,888 students of Delhi schools aged 6-19 years, anaverage of 13.4 per cent students were found to be generally obese while 9.2 per cent had belly fatrelated (central) obesity.
