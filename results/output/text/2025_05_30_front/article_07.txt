Could have done a lot more, showed restraint: Rajnath on Op Sindoor

DEFENCE MINISTER Rajnath Singh said Thursday that India could have done "alot more" beyond destroying terrorist hide-outsand Pakistani military bases in Operation Sindoor, but presented a great example of "coordination of power and restraint". He said the use of indigenous systems during Operation Sindoor proves that India has the

power to penetrate any armour of theenemy. Addressing the inaugural plenary of the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) Annual Business Summit, Singh also said that Pakistan-occupied Kashmir is a part of India and people who got separated geographically and politically will, sooner or later, voluntarily return to India.
