Trade deal: First tranche by Fall, but no relief yet on reciprocal tariffs Terms of Reference being discussed

THE FOUR-DAY India-US trade talks concluded Saturday with substantial convergence on the contours of a trade deal but without finalising the Terms of Reference (ToR), a document that kickstarts negotiations by defining the scope and objectives of a bilateral trade agreement (BTA), a government official told The Indian Express.
