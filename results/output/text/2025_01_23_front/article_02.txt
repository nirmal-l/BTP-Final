Were spooked by rumour of firein train: Minister

AT LEAST 12 passengers of Mumbai-bound Pushpak Express, who got off the train after an alarm chain-pulling incident, were run over by another train coming from the opposite direction on the adjacent tracks in Maharashtra's Jalgaon district Wednesday evening. Ten more passengers were injured, four of them critically.
