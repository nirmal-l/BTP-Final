Walkoutin Rajya Sabha

MEMBERS FROM opposition parties, barring those from the AIADMK, walked out of the Rajya Sabha as the stalemate over the sealing of party offices in Parliament House continued. The walkout came after chairman R Venkatraman allowed M S Gurupadaswamy (Janata) to make a submission. The Oppositionshould be allowed to functionwithout any obstacles in a democratic manner, he said. The chairman said, "This House has no jurisdictiontodiscuss this matter."
