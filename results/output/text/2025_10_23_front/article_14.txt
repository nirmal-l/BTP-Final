At 91, Muivah remains the face of one of South Asia's oldest insurgencies, and in these parts, a hero. He joined the Naga National Council back in 1964 to fight for Naga autonomy; went on to form the NSCN (National Socialist Counal of Nagaland)in 1980 along with S S Khaplang and Isak Chishi Swu, both of whom have now passed away; entered a ceasefire with the govemment in 1997; and has since been at the helm of peace talks with the Union.

Now, in the autumn of his life, Muivah has returned to his village - barred to him for a long time by the government - fora week-long visit. On Wednesday, Muivah made his first halt at the Ukhrul district headquarters along with his wife Pakahao, where a home-coming reception had been organised for "avakharar(the head of the family, in the local Tangkhul dialect)".
