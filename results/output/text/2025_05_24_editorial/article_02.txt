It was an assault on India's social cohesion, economic momentum, democratic ethos, and a deliberate attempt to wound the idea of India itself. India's response, Operation Sindoor, launched on May 7, along with Prime Minister Narendra Modi's May 12 national address, has given India's counter-terrorism doctrine dlarity, coherence, and credibility.
