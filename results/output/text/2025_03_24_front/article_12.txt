Paralympics medallist sets target for teen rival who lost limbs at 5

SHEETAL DEVIis onlyoneof two armless athletes to win a medal at the Paralympics The story of this 18-year-old archer from Jammu, however, is not just about beating the oddsand winning medals She is also inspiring others in similar or more challenging circumstances to takeup the bow - and beasgood as her. On Sunday, Sheetal edged past 17-year-old Payal Nag inthe final to win gold in the 2025 Khelo India Para Games. But then, had # not been for Sheetal's exploits at the 2024 Paris Paralympics, where she won bronze, Payal would not have taken up the sport at all.
