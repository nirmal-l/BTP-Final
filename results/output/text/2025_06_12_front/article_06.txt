Trump says China trade deal 'done': 55% tariff, supply of rare earth minerals

PRESIDENT DONALD Trump said Wednesday that the United States has reached a trade agreement with China, including an easing of curbs imposed by Beijing on export of rare earth minerals and magnets that are key inputs for industries ranging from automobiles to electronics. "Our deal with Chinais done, subject to final approval with President Xi and me," Trump said ina post on Truth Social. He said China would supply "any necessary rare earths" and magnets, while the US would make

concessions on allowing Chinese students to attend American universities. The Trump administration had recently begun to clamp down on the presence of Chinese nationals on US college campuses. "We are getting a total of 55% tariffs, China is getting 10%. Relationship is excellent!" Trump wrote, without elaborating.
