CEC: India's electoral roll preparation most rigorous, transparent

IN HIS first remarks after Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi's recent allegations of irregularities in the Maharashtra elections, including in its electoral roll, Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar has said the process of preparing theelectoral roll in India is among the "most rigorous and transparent" in the world.
