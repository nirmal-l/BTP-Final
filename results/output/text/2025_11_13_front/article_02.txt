Food 5% cheaper compared with last October, pressure on RBI tocut rates

Siddharth Upasani New Delhi, November 12

CONSUMER PRICES rose a mere 0.25 per cent in October compared with the same month last year as the Goods and Services Tax (GST) rate cuts and a favourable base effect helped drive down the retail inflation rate to an all-time low, according to government data released Tuesday. Rural inflation was in the negative territory for the first time ever, with overall consumer prices 0.25 per cent lower in October compared to last year.
