DECODING CVR

THE PRELIMINARY HEARING of the cockpit voice recorder (CVR) of the ill-fated Air India Jumbo, Kanishka, established that the snapping of communication was "sudden and abrupt". The preliminaryrun was done at the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre. The spokesman and secretary to the court of inquiry, S NSharma, said the preliminary run of the tape was "pretty good and normal".
