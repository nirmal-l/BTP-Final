That year, everything changed. Someone in our coach slipped a brand-new cassette into a bulky tape player. The album was called Anamika. The singer, Zubeen Garg. His voice was raw, restless, young, uncontainable, and it set our entire compartment ablaze. We sang along, out of tune but unabashed, our voices mingling with the rumble of the train wheels.
