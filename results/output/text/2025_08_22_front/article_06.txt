CM gets CRPF security after attack, Delhia new police chief

ADAY after Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta was attacked during a 'Jan Sunwai' (public meeting), the Union Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) on Thursday appointed Satish Golcha, a 1992- batch Indian Police Service (IPS) officer, as the Capital's new Police Commissioner. Following the attack, the Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) has been handed charge of the CM's security, which has been upgraded to 'Z category.
