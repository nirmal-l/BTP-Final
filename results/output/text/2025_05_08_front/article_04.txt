Four children, soldier among 13 killed in Pak shelling near LoC

INA sharp escalation in cross-border shelling, which has gone from small arms fire to heavy artillery, Pakistan on Wednesday targeted the civilian population residing near the Line of Control, killing at least 12 people and leaving 40 injured. Late Wednesday night, the Indian Army confirmed that a soldier had died in Pakistani shelling. While intense artillery fire was also heard in the Uri and Tangdhar sectors of Kashmir Valley, the worst was in Poonch town in Jammu, which saw artillery shells hit residential and government buildings, besides public places, for the first time since the 1971 India-Pakistan war.
