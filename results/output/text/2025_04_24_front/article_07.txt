5 terrorists, including 3 from Pak, behind attack, indicates probe

AT LEAST five terrorists, including three from Pakistan, are suspected to have opened fire on tourists at the Baisaran valley meadow in Pahalgam Tuesday, The Indian Express has learnt. According to sources, eyewitness accounts and intelligence reports suggest that at least two of the attackers are suspected to be local militants. "The Urdu that they (the foreign terrorists) spoke is from certain parts of Pakistan... At least two local militants are suspected to be accompanying them," an officer from the security establishment told The Indian Express.
