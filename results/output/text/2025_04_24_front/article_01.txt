Govt suspends Indus treaty, expels Pak advisors, cancels visas, closes Attari

UNDERLINING THE "the cross-border linkages of the terrorist attack" near Pahalgam that killed 26 people, India Wednesday put on hold the Indus Waters Treaty, downgraded diplomatic relations with Pakistan by reducing the mission strength by half, expelled diplomats and top defence officials from the Pakistan High Commission in Delhi, cancelled all visas granted to Pakistan nationals under a SAARC scheme and directed them to leave in 48 hours, and shut the Attari-Wagah border. It also withdrew its own advisors and staff from its mission in Islamabad.
