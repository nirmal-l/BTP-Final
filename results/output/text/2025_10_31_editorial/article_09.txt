OCTOBER 31, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

THE HOMAGE TO Indira Gandhi on her first death anniversary will begin at 4am when the Congress members of Parliament assemble at the AICC-I office on Akbar Road for a silent march toherresting place, Shakti Sthal, The procession willleave Akbar Road on foot at 4.30 am so that the march will be at Shakti Sthal by 6 am to pay homage and offer prayers at the late prime minister's samadhi along with her son, the Prime Minister, Rajiv Gandhi.
