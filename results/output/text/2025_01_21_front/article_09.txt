Uttarakhand Govt clears UCC rules, set to be rolled out after local body elections AISWARYARAJ

TATKAL REGISTRATION, doorto-door servicesin remote areas, and a state-wide mock drill on January 21 - these are some of the key provisions in Uttarakhand's Uniform Civil Code manual of rules, which the Pushkar Singh Dhami Cabinet approved Monday. The move drew criticism from the Opposition Congress, which called it aviolation of the Model Code of Conduct in place ahead of local body polls. According to sources, the rules manual, which was approved at the Cabinet meeting convened for the purpose Monday, is expected to be announced after the election Thursday. The training of the officials is also in the final stages. Once these are rolled out, Uttarakhand will become the first state toimplement the UCC.
