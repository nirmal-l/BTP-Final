From land to votes, how a private firm took over acricket association

Lalith Kalidas Puducherry, New Delhi, December 8

THE CRICKET Association of Pondicherry (CAP) is a relatively small unit of the BCCI but, inthelast seven years, it has become a showcase of how the lines between a private enterprise andsports administration blur - raising serious questions over governance, and conflict of interest in finance, land ownership and voting control.

Atthe heart of CAP, holding all its strings, is P Damodaren, the owner of Siechem Technologies Ltd, a wires and cables company headquartered in Chennai with a factory in Puducherry. The CAP presidenttill August, he is also atthe centre ofacomplaintlodged by the Bharathidasan Pondicherry Cricketers' Forum, acollective of former local players, with the BCCI's Ombudsman and Ethics Officer.
