To qualify for admission under SEA, candidates should have won at least one medal ata nationdlor intemationatevent ina sport recognised by federations under the sport ministry in the last four years, scored at least 75% in their Class 12 board exams and cleared the JEE (Advanced) test. SRL is a point system, under which an international gold medal means 100 extra points and a national gold medal means 35 points.
