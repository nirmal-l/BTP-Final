Many deportees were detained from US border in last two months

MANY OF the Indians deported from the United States had reached the Mexico-US border just last month or towards the end of December, The Indian Express haslearntafter speaking to several of them and tosources in the police. AUS military aircraft carrying 104 deported Indian nationals landed at Amritsar airport Wednesday aftemoon. Of the 104, 30 were from Punjab and 33 from Gujarat. At least 15 each from Punjab and Gujarat were detained at the Mexico-US border as they allegedly tried to enter the US amid stricter border control under the new administration of President Donald Trump.
