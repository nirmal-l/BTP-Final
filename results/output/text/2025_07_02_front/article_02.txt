ATTHE first Quad foreign ministers' meeting after Operation Sindoor in May, External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar said on Tuesday that India expects its Quad partners to understand and appreciate that the country has every right todefend its people against terrorism, and it will exercise that right. "A word about terrorism in the light of our recent experience - the world must display zero

tolerance. Victims and perpetrators must never be equated. And India has every right to defend its people against terrorism, and we will exercise that right. We expect our Quad partners to understand and appreciate that," Jaishankar said in remarks to the media ahead of the meeting in Washington DC with his counterparts from the US, Australia and Japan.
