FIR against Himachal minister for assaulting NHAI officials, Gadkari seeks 'exemplary action'

HIMACHAL PRADESH Minister Anirudh Singh has been booked for allegedly assaulting two National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) officials during a visit to the site of a building collapse in Shimla district, police said Tuesday. The FIR was lodged Monday night on acomplaint filed by Achal Jindal, 35, a manager at a four-lane project of the NHAI in Shimla, who alleged that he and his site engineer, Yogesh Verma, 27, were called to a room by Singh and assaulted. Both NHAI officials were admitted to Indira Gandhi Medical College (IGMC), Shimla, for treatment.
