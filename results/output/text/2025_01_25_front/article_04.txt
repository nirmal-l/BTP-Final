Foreign borrowings turn dearer for India Inc as Rupee falls

THE FALL in the rupee's value is expected to increase the financial burden on Indian companies that have borrowed funds from overseas lenders. With the rupee depreciating, these companies that benefited from the Reserve Bank's then borrower-friendly foreign exchange management and the resultant strong rupee, will now have to shell out more money to repay their foreign debts.

Furthermore, companies that have nothed ged their overseas borrowings to compensate forthe mupee's decline could incur significant losses. The ongoing depreciation of the rupee will erode the value of their repay-ments leaving them with asubstantial financial shortfall.
