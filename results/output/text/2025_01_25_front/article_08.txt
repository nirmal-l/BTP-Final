Eight workers killed in blast at ordnance factory in Maharashtra

A MASSIVE explosion on Friday morning at the ordnance factory in Jawahar Nagar, in Maharashtra's Bhandara district, killed eight factory workers and left five injured, officials said. The blast, which wok place at around 10.45 am, was felt in nearby villages and heard up to five km away. The cause of the explosion wasnotyetdetermined, officials said.
