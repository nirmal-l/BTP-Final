Do we give intruder a red-carpet welcome, asks Supreme Court

PIL ON ROHINGYA

Ananthakrishnan G New Dethi, December2

WONDERING HOW people who enter India illegally can seek due legal process fortheir expulsion, the Supreme Court, in oral observations Tuesday, said "if an intruder comes, do we give ared-carpet welcome, saying we would like to provide all facilities". Hearinga PiLby rightsactivist Rita Manchanda who claimed that some Rohingya people had been deported illegally, Chief Justice of India Surya Kant, who wason a bench with Justice Joymalya Bagchi,

said, "You cross the fenced borderillegally, you dig a tunnel, and you enter India. Then you declare that 'nowthat I have entered, your laws must apply to me, that I should be issued a show-causenotice, lamentitled

to be provided food, Iam entitled tobe provided shelter, my children are entitled to this and that'. Do we want to stretch the law like this?".
