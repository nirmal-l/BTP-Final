THE CITY AND I

T ask Chennai: Is it possible at all? For human impulses to co-exist with the rest of life? It offers, a short drive away, Vedanthangal, where for centuries farmer folk have protected the waterbirds. It suggests a peek at the Theosophical Great Banyan, kept effortfully safe and alive for hundreds of years. Our truest impulse, Chennai memories remind me, is to playmate and co-create with non-humanness. Someday, we'llremember.
