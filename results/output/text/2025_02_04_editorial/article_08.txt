In present Chennai, I drink two coffees Siowly across the dining table frommy partner and we chitchar, la into the morning, about essays we've both read in quiet. it's a clear-skied Sunday. We realise we want to be outside. Wetalk overa prawn curry lunch about where togo. Kotturpuram Urban Forest, tosee the flying foxes in the cluster of fig trees? Or Coc Bank, to the gharials and muggers and komodos? We settle ona popular beach cafe - a third coffee would be nice - but when we getthere, the music is too loud to hearthe sea so we setoff shore-walking. We'reon abitof Panaiyur Beach I haven't seen in my 16 years here. Egrets are picking at fish drying on sheets. Crowsare diving for clams. Barnacles have made homes of abandoned chappals, bottles, driftwood. Couples canoodie, toddlers chase waves, ice cream and balloon and juice vendors gaze seaward. Thisis familiar, gorgeous stuff. This ishome. Then, by the tide line, we see an Olive Ridley sea turtle, shell dented by trawler. Then another, eyes bulged open from trawbnet suffocation. Reflected in those eyes, I see its Chennai for the first time: Treacherous, drowning, blue.
