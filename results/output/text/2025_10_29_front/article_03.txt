MEHLI MISTRY, a close confidant of Ratan Tata, ison his way outof Tata Trusts after a majority of trustees voted against his reappointment to the boards of the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust and the Sir Ratan Tata Trust. These two trusts hold over 51 per cent in Tata Sons Ltd, the principal investment holding company and promoter of Tata companies. Three ofthe six trustees in the two trusts opposed Mistry's renomination through a circular resolution circulated last week. These three trustees, sources said, are Tata Trusts Chairman Noel Tata, Venu Srinivasan, chairman of the TVS Group, and former Defence Secretary Vijay Singh. Mistry was first inducted as trustee in 2022, with his current three-year term expiring on October 28. He will have to step down from the governing boards of the Tata Trusts.
