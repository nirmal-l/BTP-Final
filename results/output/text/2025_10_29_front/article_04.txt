SANTOSH SINGH & RAVIK BHATTACHARYA PATNA, OCTOBER 28

HOURS AFTER The Indian Express reported that Prashant Kishor has his name enrolled in the voter lists of two states - West Bengal and Bihar - the Returning Officer for the Kargaharassembly constituency has issued him a notice, seeking an explanation within three days. The poll strategist-turned-politician's Jan Suraaj Party is contesting the Bihar Assembly polls this time.
