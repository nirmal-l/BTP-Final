AS WE SAY goodbye to Justice Abhay Shreeniwas Oka, who started his legal practice at the Thane District Court, it is an occasion to let his judgments do the talking. He championed free speech, liberty andequality. He has been instrumental in relaxing bail conditions in special statutes like the UAPA, and often came down heavily on authorities for arbitrary and illegal demolitions. In the Shaikh Zahid Mukhtar case(2016), a bench that included Justice Oka struck down two amendments to the Maharashtra Animal Prevention Act, 1976. Itwas held that Section 5D (incriminating possession of flesh of any cow, bull, etc, slaughtered outside Maharashtra) infringed on the right to privacy. This was before privacy was recognised as a fundamental right inthe KS Puttaswamy Arrests of students and political activists have become frequent and securing bail has become a tedious exercise. But in Jalaluddin Khan vs Union of India(2024), Justice Okaheld that even under the UAPA, bail is the rule and jailis the exception. In February 2025, pulling up the EnforcementDirectorate(ED) forkeeping Indian Telecommuinication Service officer Arun Kumar Tripathi in custody under the PMLA, Justice Oka said that the "concept of PMLA cannot be to ensure that a person should remain in jail".
