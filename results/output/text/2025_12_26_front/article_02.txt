Top Maoist with Rs 1-cr bounty among six killed in Odisha

POSTAL REGN. NO. JAIPUR CITY/001/2024-26

Sreenivas Janyala Hyderabad, December 25

TOP MAOIST leader Paka Hanumanthu, alias Ganesh alias Chamru, and five other Maoists were killed in an encounter in Odisha in the early hours of Thursday, a development the Centre hailed asa significant breakthrough. Ganesh, 67, from Telangana's Nalgonda district, was a Central Committee memberof the outlawed Communist Party of India (Maoist), a member of the Central Regional Bureau, and the only member of the Southern Regional Bureau. He carried a reward of Rs1 crore and went by several aliases, including Rupa, Rajesh Tiwari, Chamru, Pakka Hanumantu, Ganeshanna and Somar. Those killed alongside him are yet tobe identified.
