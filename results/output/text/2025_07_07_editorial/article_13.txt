THE TOURISM INDUSTRY is facing an unprecedented crisis following sharp decline in foreign tourist traffic to India after Indira Gandhi's assassination last October. According to officials in the travel trade, the tourist traffic has dropped by 20 to 30 per cent since November 1984, resulting inthe loss of crores of rupees in foreign exchange. They said that group after group cancelled their scheduled trips to India after the assassination and the subsequent violence that erupted all over the country.
