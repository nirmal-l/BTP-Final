Even though itis an elector's responsibility to ensure that she is not enrolled in more than one place, the EC] has been tolerant of this while working on methods by which such duplication can be detected and deleted. It has not filed a single case against electors who may have committed this irregularity because it was confident that its procedures would not permit electors to vote more than once. It has innovative ways of facilitating voters embracing modern technologies to make the system failproof. The Indian electoral system has faced a fundamental challenge in determining where an elector should vote. EPIC recognises an elector's eligibility but is not sufficient to vote till the elector's name appears in the electoral roll of a particular constituency. The EC has been flexible in allowingan elector to be enrolled ina place of his ordinary residence.
