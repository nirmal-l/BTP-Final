Having spent many years of my academic life in Australia, and living in Melbourne's Kooyong electorate during atime of dramatic transition, I have been an eyewitness to the shifts in the politics in the country. Once abas-tion of Liberal orthodoxy and hometoformer treasurer Josh Frydenberg, Kooyong is now represented by the Teal Independent Monique Ryan - a former paediatric neurologist. Ryan's re-election is symbolic of a broader transformation: A growing class of voters, especially in urban and affluent constituencies, who demand integrity, climate action, and a departure from the adversarial politics of the past.
