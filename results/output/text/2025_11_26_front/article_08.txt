'MISFIT FOR ARMY' Christian officer fired for refusing to enter all-faith place of worship

Ananthakrishnan G New Delhi, November25

CALLING IT the "grossest kind of indiscipline by an Army officer", the Supreme Court Tuesday declined to interfere with an order of the Delhi High Court which had upheld the termination of service of an Army officer who, citing his monotheistic belief as a Christian, had allegedly refused to enter the regimental Sarva Dharma Sthal, symbolic of all religions and faiths. The Delhi High Court, in its May 30 order, upheld the termination of service of Samuel Kamalesan, commissioned as a Lieutenant in the Army in 2017, saying that keeping religion above alawful command froma superior was "clearly an act of discipline". On Tuesday, abench of Chief Justice of India Surya Kant and Justice Joymalya Bagchi said the actions of Kamalesan were the "grossest kind of indiscipline by an Army officer".
