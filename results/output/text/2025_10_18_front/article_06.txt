2 journalists picked up from Bhopal for reports against Rajasthan Dy CM Reports false and defamatory, bid to extort: Jaipur Commissioner's office

THE RAJASTHAN Police on Friday picked up two journalists from Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, claiming that they had carried false and defamatory stories against Rajasthan Deputy Chief Minister Diya Kumari and demanded Rs5 crore to take them down. Anand Pandey, Editor in Chief of The Sootr, and Harish Divekar, the Managing Editor of the news website, were picked up by the Rajasthan police from Bhopal, where it is headquartered.

Speaking to The Indian Express, a spokesperson for The Sootr termed the police action "shocking and unlawful". Jaipur police claimed their "investigation revealed that the accused had made an illegal demand of Rs Scrore to remove false news" from the portal and that they had planned to launch a 'Destroy Diya' campaign.
