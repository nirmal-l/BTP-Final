Don't suspect foul play in Zubeen death, say police in Singapore

THE SINGAPORE police, in a statementissued on Friday, have said that "based on preliminary investigations", they "do not suspect foul play" in singer Zubeen Garg's death. Garg, 52, died in Singapore on September 19. He was there as a cultural brand ambassador for the North East India Festival, Singapore, which was scheduled to take place on September 20 and 21. Before the festival began, on September 19, he went on a yacht outing with members of the Assam _ Association Singapore, and lost consciousness while he was swimming. He was declared dead when he was taken to Singapore General Hospital. The death certificate issued by Singapore authorities states that the cause of death was "drowning."
