058-cr digital arrest: Victim was targeted months after he got ~50 cr from shares

JUSTMONTHS before a 72-yearold man in Mumbaifell preytoa Rs 58-crore digital arrest scam, one of the largest cyber cons in the country, he received Rs 50 crore for liquidating his sharesin a pharmaceutical company where he was a partner, The Indian Express has learnt. While the money came into his account "earlier this year", officers associated with the probe told this newspaper, the man was first contacted via video call by digital scamsters on August 19. Subsequently, sources said, the scamsters forced the victim to visit four banks where he held accounts for 27 days across a40- day period to transfer money to various "mule accounts" while they listened in on an "active phone call" to "keep a tab".
