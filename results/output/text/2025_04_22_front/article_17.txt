Delhi Police to check on govt school dropouts, for 'counselling, career'

DELHI POLICE will soon knock on your doors if anyone at home has dropped out of a government-run school in the city. The Delhi government has directed the Education Department to prepare a standard operating procedure (SOP) to enable automatic sharing of data on school dropouts with Delhi Police every six months. Once the data is shared, a senior officer said, police will scrutinise it and find out why a student has dropped out of school. After that the local police will meet the dropout.
