'Stripped, forced to eat chilli paste': Palwal SHO held for torture

HARYANA POLICE has arrested a suspended Station House Officer (SHO) for allegedly assaulting and torturing a man accused of fraud in custody at the Palwal city police station in December last year. Inspector Radhey Shyam allegedly beat Mohammed Abid, 31, from Kota in Rajasthan, forced him to consume chilli paste, and also injected it into his private parts,

Abid, who worked as a masseur, and his friend allegedly got into an argument with one Rambir over payment of Rs 12,000 for availing his services. After Rambir filed a complaint, the SHO allegedly called Abid into a room, asked him to remove his trouser, beat him with a pipe after tying him up, and asked him tohave halfa kilo of ground chillies in water that were later put in his private parts, according to thecomplaint filed by Abid. The complaint is part of the FIR against the Inspector. Despite protests by another policeman, the SHO continued with the torture and also allegedly called Abid ajihadi.
