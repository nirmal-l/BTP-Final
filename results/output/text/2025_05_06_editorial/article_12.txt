May 6, 1985, Forty YEARS AGO

THE CONGRESS (1) president, Rajiv Gandhi, in his concluding remarks at the AJCC-I session announced that organisational elections from grassroot-level upwards will be heldin the beginning of the next year and the process will be completed within a few months. The membership campaign beginning now will end on December 31. Gandhi reiterated that the party had not deviated from the path of socialism and self-reliance.
