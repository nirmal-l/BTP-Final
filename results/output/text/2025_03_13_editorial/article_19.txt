AKILLER DRINK tragedy claimed at least 14 lives in Ahmedabad while 30 others were fighting for their livesin the civil hospital. The victims, who belonged to the labour areaof Rajpura-Gomtipur, are believed to have consumed a brew called "cologne" in an illicit joint at Rajapura. Most victims were blind and unconscious when they were rushed to thehospitals 10 persons in critical condition were brought to thecivil hospital.
