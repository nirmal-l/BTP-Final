Indian emigrants encountered severe hardships during the pandemic, including job losses, wage cuts, overcrowded living conditions, and heightened health risks due to inadequate sanitation and protective measures. Despite job losses and wage cuts, remittances to India remained resilient, sustaining households reliant on migrant earnings. The pandemic has reinforced the notion that remittances will remain a crucial driver of development in India and some of the emigrant-sending states.
