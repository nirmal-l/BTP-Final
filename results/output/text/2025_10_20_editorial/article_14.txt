OcTOBER 20, 1985, ForTY YEARS AGO

BLACK MOBS, ANGERED by the execution of militant Benjamin Moloise, overwhelmed police in downtown Johannesburg, beating up white women and looting shops during a four-hour chaos. It was the first time black mobs roamed unchecked through Johannesburg, the nation's largest city. Two policemen suffered stab wounds, a number of whites were beaten, and the police shot at least one black man in the leg.
