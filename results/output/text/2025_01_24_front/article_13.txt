Trump backs H-IB: Want very competent people... at all levels

BACKING THE H-1B visa programme forthe second timeina month, US President Donald Trump said the country needs "very competent" and "great" people, and this is possible through this visa programme. Speaking at the White House during a joint news conference with Oracle CTO Larry Ellison, Softbank CEO Masayoshi Son and Open Al CEO Sam Altman, Trump said on Wednesday: "I like both sides of the argument, but I also like very competent peoplecoming int our country,
