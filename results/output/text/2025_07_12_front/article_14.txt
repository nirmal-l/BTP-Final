'Our phones checked for Bangladesh contacts': Bengal migrants freed from Odisha detention

"WHEN WE showed them our voter ID cards and Aadhaar cards, they said it was not enough. They wanted birth certificates and school documents. They even seized our phones and checked for phone numbers from Bangladesh."

Bengal, was among the migrant workers picked up by Odisha Police near Brajarajnagar in Jharsuguda district. He was also one of several people who have since been released following intervention by West Bengal Police and the state administration.
