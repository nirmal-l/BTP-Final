Four ex-CJIs raise red flag: EC power to delay simultaneous elections may not pass muster

APURVA VISHWANATH

APPEARING BEFORE the Joint Committee of Parliament on 'One Nation, One Election' (ONOE) Bill, four former Chief Justices of India have red-flagged the vast powers that the Election Commission holds in the proposed law for holding simultaneous polls. Itis learnt that on Friday former CJIs J S Khehar and D Y Chandrachud expressed doubts whether the power of EC to debeen given unrestricted powers to decide the schedule of simultaneous polls.
