MUMBAI POLICE on Friday registered a non-cognizable (NC) complaint against Shiv Sena MLA Sanjay Gaikwad for assaulting a canteen worker after he was allegedly served stale food in his room at the MLA Hostel Tuesday, The Marine Drive police station lodged a suo motu complaint against Gaikwad, who represents Buldhana, and his aide, who is also seen assaulting the canteen worker in avideo of the incident. "We have lodged an NC(noncognizable complaint) under sections 115(2), 352 and 3(5) of the BNS," a Marine Drive police station officer said. The sections deal with charges of voluntarily causing hurt and intentional insult with intentto provoke breach of peace, among others. Following the incident, while Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said his behaviour had tarnished the image of the legislature and assured that action would be taken, Gaikwad's party leader and Deputy Chief Minister Eknath Shinde said he had reprimanded the MLA. On Tuesday, Gaikwad entered the MLA hostel canteen and confronted the staff claiming he was served sour dal and spoiled rice. In the video of the incident, Gaikwad is seen slapping and punching the canteen operator and asking others to leave their bills unpaid. His supporters, too, joined him and assaulted the canteen staff. An unapologetic Gaikwad later said he had complained about food quality on several occasions, but no action was taken.
