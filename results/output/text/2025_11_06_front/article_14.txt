For those who dropped in, the drawing room was a favourite photo spot because it is lined with pictures of Harmanpreet with other cricketers. There is one with her and 1983 World Cup-winning captain Kapil Dev, another with Sachin Tendulkar and Virender Sehwag. Bollywood legend Amitabh Bachchan is also in the gallery.
