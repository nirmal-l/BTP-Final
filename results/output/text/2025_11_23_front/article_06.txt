Reaches out to Africa with skills-multiplier initiative, proposes formation of global healthcare response team

Shubhajit Roy New Delhi, November 22

FLAGGING THE issue of drug trafficking including that of fentanyl, Prime Minister Narendra Modi told the G20 leaders' summit Saturday that this posesa seriouschallenge to global security and is a major

means of financing terrorism. To effectively address this global threat, he proposed the "G20 Initiative on Countering the Drug-Terror Nexus" to "weaken" the "drug-terror economy". Modi, whoarrived in Johan-nesburg Friday and was welcomed by South African President Cyril Ramaphosa at the summit, reached out to Africa by proposing a G20-Africa Skills Multiplier initiative that will train one million certified trainers in Africa over the next 10 years.
