SEVEN DAYS after searching multiple locations in Mumbai linked to Anil Ambani, the Enforcement Directorate (ED) hassummoned the industrialist on August 5 to join the investigation into a money laundering case linked to a multi-crore bank loan fraud involving companies of the Reliance Anil Dhirubhai Ambani Group. The ED has also issued a Look-out Circular (LOC) to prevent him from travelling abroad, officials said Friday.
