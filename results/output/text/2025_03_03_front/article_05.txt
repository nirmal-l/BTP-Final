Union Minister says daughter harassed at fair: 'Imagine what people go through'

A UNION Minister of State and BJP leader lodged a police complaint Sunday stating that some youths had "harassed" her daughter during a fair in Maha-rashtra's Jalgaon district, triggering a political firestorm with the Opposition slamming the BJPled ruling alliance for what they said was the "deteriorating law and order situationin thestate". Jalgaon Police said that acase under the POCSO and ITActs has been registered over the incident. "Two persons have been arrested and a minor detained. We are searching for other accused," Jalgaon SP Maheswar Reddy told The Indian Express. Reacting to the incident, Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis claimed that some of theaccused had political ties but he did not provide details.
