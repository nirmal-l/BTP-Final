As part of an initiative tooffer exposure to children from Ladakh, the students of Turtuk Valley School in Leh were brought to the AFAC School so that they notonly continue with theirclasses, but also get a taste of the bustling city. The students were accommodated at the AFAC School, where computer labs were converted into a makeshifi residential facitity forthem. Apart from classes, the children spent their after-school
