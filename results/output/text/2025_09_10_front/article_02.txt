Radhakrishnan is new V-P, Opp falls short of own tally amid invalid votes, cross-vote fears

Radhakrishnan was elected India's 15th Vice-President on Tuesday, defeating B Sudershan Reddy by a margin of 152 votes. The outcome came asa jolt to the Opposition bloc, whose hopes of securing at least 320- plus votes appeared to havebeen dashed by cross-voting. NDA nominee Radhakrishnan, 67, an old-time RSS and BJP leader, secured 452 of the 752 valid votes polled - with 15 declared invalid - against Reddy's 300.
