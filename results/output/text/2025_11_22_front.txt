===== ARTICLE 5 =====
Tejas fighter crashes during air showin Dubai, IAF pilot killed

Amrita Nayak Dutta New Delhi, November 21

AN INDIAN Air Force Tejas Mk! fighter jet crashed during an aerial display Friday at the Dubai Airshow 2025, killing its pilot. The IAF identified the pilot as Wing Commander Namansh Syal. Speaking to The Indian Expressfrom Coimbatore in Tamil Nadu, Wing Commander Syal's father Jagan Nath Sayal, a retired school principal from Kangra district in Himachal Pradesh, said his daughter-inlaw too is a Wing Commander. The IAF has twooperational squadrons of the Tejas Mk1, one each at Sulur near Coimbatore and Naliya in Gujarat. This is the second crash of a Tejas aircraft. In March 2024, a Tejas Mk1 aircraft went down near Jaisalmer during atraining sortie. Its pilot ejected safely. A Court of Inquiry is being constituted to ascertain the

cause of the Dubai accident, the IAF said in a statement. "IAF deeply regrets the loss of life and stands firmly with the bereaved family in this time of grief." it said inits statement. Multiple visuals ofthe crash on social media platforms showed the aircraft going down during an aerial display at the Dubai air show, erupting into a fireball as soon as it hit the ground. Large plumes of black smoke were seen emanating from the site.
