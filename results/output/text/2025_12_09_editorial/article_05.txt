SAARC launch

The seven South Asian leaders launched the world's most populous regional grouping with the hope that it would improve these-curity environment in the region and that cooperation among themin the economic and cultural fields will result in a better life for their peoples. On the final day of their first-ever summit, the heads of state of Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, the Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka adopted a charter giving birth to the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC).
