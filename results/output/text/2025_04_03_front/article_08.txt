In letter to Sonia, PMs' Museum seeks access to Nehru papers taken in 2008

AHEAD OF the first annual general meeting ofits newly formed Society, the Prime Ministers' Museum & Library (PMML) is learnt to have written to Congress leader Sonia Gandhi, seeking "access" to the papers from Jawaharlal Nehru's private collection which she had reclaimed in 2008. Sources said Sonia's office has notresponded to PMML's communication. As first reported by The indian Express, the then PMML Society had discussed the status of the papers in Sonia's custody at its

last annual general meeting in February 2024. But this is the first time thatit has put the matter on record in an official communication to Sonia. Sonia's office did not respond to an email sent by The Indian Express on the matter. According to sources, the PMML in its letter to Sonia, has "sought access to the papers for researchers".
