Regulator eases entry norms for foreign investors, relaxes IPO rules for large firms

INORDER to enhance ease of doing business for foreign portfolio investors (FPIs), the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Friday announced a proposal to introducea single automatic window for foreign investors. The board also proposed to relax initial public offering (IPO) norms for large issuers and extended timelines to meet the minimum public shareholding for issuers.
