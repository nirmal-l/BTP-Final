Cong ex-MP Sajjan convicted of killing two in another 1984 anti-Sikh riots case NIRBHAY THAKUR

A DELHI court Wednesday convicted former Congress MP Sajjan Kumar ina case related to the killing of two men in Saraswati Viharon November 1, 1984, during the anti-Sikh riots that followed the assassination of Indira Gandhi. Arguments on the quantum of sentence in the case will take place on February 18.
