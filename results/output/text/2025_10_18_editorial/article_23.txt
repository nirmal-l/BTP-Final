THE STUDENTS FEDERATION of India (SFI). student wing of the Communist Party of India (Marxist), has won all the four posts of office-bearers of the JNU Students Union (JNUSU) elections. T K Arun of the SFI was declared elected president. He polled 601 votes against the 346 polled by his nearest rival, SN Malakar ofthe All India Students Federation (AISF), student wing of the Communist Party of India.
