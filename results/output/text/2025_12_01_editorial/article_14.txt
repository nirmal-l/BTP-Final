Teiroristskilltwo policemen

SUSPECTED TERRORISTS ambushed a police motorcycle and killed two police personnel in Ferozepur district. In another incident, Arjan Singh, a Nirankari of village Rampurain Ludhiana district, was shot at and seriously injured by two unidentified youths in the village. He was rushed to ahospitalin Ludhiana where his condition was stated to be out of danger. Chief Minister Surjit Singh Barnalatold UNI in Chandigarh that, according to details received at the state headquarters, the police officials who were on patrol duty were taken unawares. The assailants had positioned themselves in advance forthe ambush after hearing the sound of the motorcycle's engine.
