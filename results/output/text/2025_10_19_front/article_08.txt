Afghanistan pulls out of tri-series in Pakistan after 3 players killed in attack

Stand in solidarity with ACB, says BCCI

AFGHANISTAN CRICKET Board (ACB) has pulled out its team from participating ina tri-series in Pakistan next month, after three local cricketers died ina Pakistani military strike. Ina post on X, the ACB said that following the airstrikes, it decided not to send its team to Lahore and Rawalpindi for the T20 tri-series in November, which also involved Sri Lanka. The Pakistan Cricket Board is yet to respond to the issue, and it is unclear whether the series will go ahead with just Pakistan
