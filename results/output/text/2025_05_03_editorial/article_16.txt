May 3, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

DEFENCE DEAL PUNJAB PROBLEM

THEPRIMEMINISTER, Rajiv Gandhi, indicated that India would rather forego the £45 million aid being offered by Britain than buy the un-Suitable Westland helicopters whose puichase wastied toit Answering a question by Suresh Kalmadiin the Rajya Sabha, Gandhi said no letter of intent had been signed for the helicopters. He said the question was whether the helicopter was suitable for India or not.

AFIVE-JUDGE CONSTITUTION Bench of the Supreme Court set aside a judgment of the Delhi High Court which had held that a statutory tenancy was not heritable. The High Court had held that on the death of a statutory tenant, the tenant's heirs had no right to remain in possession ofthe premises, as tenancy was not heritable and the protection afforded to a tenant by the Delhi Rent Control Act 1958 was not applicable to the heirs and legalrepresentatives of the statutory tenant.
