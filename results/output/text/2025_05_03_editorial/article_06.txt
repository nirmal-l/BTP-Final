RESTORE THE FLOW

Plan to rejuvenate Yamuna in Delhi ticks some right boxes. Much more needs to be done for river to get its freshwater back HE YAMUNA IN Delhi spans only 22 km of the river's more than 1,400-km stretch. Yet, the Capital accounts for 75 per cent of the Yamuna's pollution. In March, a report of the parliamentary standing committee on water resources pointed out that the river is virtually non-existent in Delhi. Itunderlined that the river loses all its environmental flow - the volume and quality of water required by a water body to maintain its health and perform its ecological functions - downstream of the Wazirabad Barrage.
