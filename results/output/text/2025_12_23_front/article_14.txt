After studying till Class 4 at a school near her home, Deepika moved totheSchool of the Blind at Kunigal in Karnataka till Class 7, and later went to the Ranga Rao Memorial School for the Disabled in Mysuru till Class 10, where she picked up cricket. But the distance between her home and school meant that every time her father wanted to visit her, he had to borrow money and workon the farm of the money lender. "He used tocome and visit me once every six months. He used to borrow Rs 500-1,000. When I realised that, I told him there was no need to come. Whenever he visited, I used to check his pockets to see if he had money to go back home. When they gave me food inthe hostel or any sweets or cake, I used to cry a lot, wondering if my brothers had eaten or not," Deepika said.
