Seventy-five per cent of autos in the world are in India, providing livelihoods to hundreds ofdrivers. Autos add colour to the city landscape with creative quotes on the back that range from the sophisticated (No money for Volkswagen, thus auto) to the wise (Buri nazar wale, tere bhi ho bhala).
