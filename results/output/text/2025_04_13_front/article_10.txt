Sukhbir back as Akali Dal president, flags 'conspiracy' against party

Was forced toresign as party chief in December

FORMER PUNJAB Deputy Chief Minister Sukhbir Singh Badal was on Saturday re-elected as the Shiromani Akali Dal (SAD) president, a post that has been held by a member of the Badal family since 1990. About 500 delegates of the Akali Dal, who were elected by the party members duringits recent organisational polls, unanimously elected Sukhbir Badal as the party chief barely five months after his resignation from the post.
