Shah links vote chorito Nehru, Indira and Sonia; says Cong loss its own leadership's doing

New Delhi, December 10

Vikas Pathak

MOUNTING A sharp attack on the Congress over thespecial intensive revision of electoral rolls during a Lok Sabha discussion on electoral reforms where he had an exchange with Leaderof Opposition Rahul Gandhi, Union Home Minister Amit Shah Wednesday accused the Nehru-Gandhi family of "vote chori" since Independence. He said the reason for the Congress loss of power was itsleadership and not EVMs, and "one day Congress workers themselves will ask you questions".
