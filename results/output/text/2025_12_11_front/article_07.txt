At CIC selection meet, Rahul flags lack of backward, minority names in shortlist

~RECORDS DISSENT AT MEETING

Manoj CG New Delhi, December 10

AT A meeting Wednesday of the three-member panel headed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi to choose the next Chief Information Commissioner and Information Commissioners and a Vigilance Commissioner, Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi recorded his dissent while pointing out that the shortlists lacked names from the Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, OBC, EBC and minority communities. The third member of the panel is Union Home Minister Amit Shah. Sources said Gandhi had asked the government several weeks ago to provide him with the caste composition ofthe applicants.
