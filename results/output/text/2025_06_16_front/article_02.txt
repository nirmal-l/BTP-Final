Helicopter flying out of Kedarnath crashes amid bad weather, 7 killed

CHOPPER SERVICES UNDER DGCA LENS Police file case against operator: 'Gross negligence'

A HELICOPTER carrying Char Dham yatra pilgrims from Kedarnath crashed near Gaurikund in Rudraprayag district early Sunday, killing all seven people on board, including a two-year-old child. Inthe evening, the local police registered an FIR, naming two managers of the helicopter operator Aryan Aviation, under the chargeof culpable homicide. The FiRaccused theoperator of "gross negligence" and listed several alleged lapses. It said the helicopter took off around 50 minutes before its allotted time, during conditions of poor visibility caused by clouds and fog. Earlier in the day, Inspector General (Law and Order) Nilesh Bharne said the helicopter was headed to Guptkashi from Kerdarnath. "The helicopter went missing after taking passengers from Kedamath en route to Guptkashi. It crashed in Gaurikund, leading to seven deaths," he said.
