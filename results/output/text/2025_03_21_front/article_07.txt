THE POPULAR new class of weight-loss drugs that has reshaped obesity treatment - especially inthe United States and Europe - isnow makingits way to India.

US-based pharmaceutical giant Eli Lilly announced the launch of Tirzepatide, marketed as Mounjaro, in India on Thursday. The medication comes in a single-dose vial and is designed to be self-administered as a weekly injection. In India, it will be available on prescription, with a 2.5 mg vial priced at Rs 3,500 anda 5 mgvial atRs4,375. Depending on the prescribed dosage, patients would spend at least Rs 14,000 per month. "Mounjaro (tirzepatide) will be available, upon obtaining a valid prescription, in registered pharmacies in the coming weeks," the company said. Tirzepatide is part of anew group of medicines called incretinmimetics, which work by imitating natural guthormones. These hormones help the body in three ways: they boost insulin production, reduce the release of glucagon(which triggers glucose productionin the liver),and stow digestion to curbappetite.
