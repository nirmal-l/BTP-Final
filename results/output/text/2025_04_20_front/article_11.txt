Dargah razing: Nashik civic body had said part of area 'earmarked for Pir'

THE DEMOLITION of a dargah in Nashik, which spiralled into violence this week, came despite a strong pushback from the Maharashtra Waqf Tribunal and the civic body itself acknowledging that the dargah has existed fordecades. Located in Nashik's Kathe Galli and nestled among several residential buildings, the decades-old Hazrat Saatpeer Sayed Baba Dargah stood on a large tract of land with seven graves believed tobe of Sufi saints.
