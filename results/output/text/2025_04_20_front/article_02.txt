After latest murder, Delhi slams Dhaka: 'Persecution of Hindu minorities'

58-YEAR-OLD ABDUCTED, KILLED Victim taken from home, sent back assaulted, unconscious: Daily Star

DAYS AFTER a Hindu community leader in Bangladesh was killed, the Ministry of External Affairs Saturday strongly condemned his alleged abduction and murder and said it follows a "pattem of systematic persecution" of Hindu minorities in Bangladesh. MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal urged the interim govemment in Dhaka to "live up to its responsibility" of protecting minorities without "inventing excuses." According to Bangladesh media reports, the man has been identified as Bhabesh Chandra Roy, 58, and he was the vice-president of the Biral unit of the Bangladesh Puja Udjapan Parishad and a prominent leader of the Hindu community in the

The Parishad mobilises members of the community across Bangladesh for religious festivals. The incident happened inthe Dinajpur area near the border

between northern Bangladesh and north Bengal, more than 300 km away from Dhaka. Dhaka-based newspaper The Daily Star reported that Roy was allegedly abducted from his home and beaten to death Thursday afternoon, according to police and family members. "Police recovered Bhabesh's body around 10 pm...His wife, Shantana Roy, told The Daily Star that Bhabesh was at home inthe afternoon when he received a phone call around 4.30 pm.
