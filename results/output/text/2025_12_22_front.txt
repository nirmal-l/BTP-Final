vandalism across Bangladesh, including Chittagong. In a statement Sunday, the Indian Visa Application Centre Bangladesh said services had been halted for an indefinite period.

"Due to a recent security incident at AHCI Chittagong, Indian visa operations at [VAC Chittagong will remain suspended from 21/12/2025 until further notice," it stated. The IVAC said the decision to resume services will depend on a thorough assessment of the local environment.
