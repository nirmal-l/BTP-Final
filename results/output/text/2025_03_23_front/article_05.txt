WITH THEIR family member missing for almost three months now in the African country of Guinea, a Barmer family has sought help from all quarters - including the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) - to figure out his whereabouts and bring him back home to India. In July last year, Jivraj Beniwal, about 30, had left for Conakry - the capital and largest city of Guinea, a country on the west coast of the African continent. Hailing from Barmer's Rampura Garal village, Jivraj had left to start his tilesbusiness. Talking to The Indian Express, Jiveaj's older brother Virama Beniwal, 34, said that Jivraj last spoke to the family around 8 pm on December 29 last year "We usually spoke over a WhatsApp call and he called his wife onthe evening of December 29, Later, he also called another person in our village but that person missed the calls as it was past midnight here - there is a 5:30 hour time difference between the two countries," he said.
