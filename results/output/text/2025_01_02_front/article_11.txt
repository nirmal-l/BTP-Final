New turn to Battle of Begums: Bangladesh rewrites textbooks on 1971 liberation war

'Khaleda husband Ziaur Rahman, not Sheikh Mujib, declared independence' EXPRESS NETWORK >

TEXTBOOKS IN Bangladesh will now state that ZiaurRahman - not 'Bangabandhu' Sheikh Mujibur Rahman - declared the country'sindependence in 1971, The Daily Star reported on Wednesday. The new textbooks have also removed Mujib's title of 'Father of the Nation'. "The new textbooks for the 2025 academic year will state that 'on March 26, 1971,ZiaurRahman declared the independence of Bangladesh, andon March 27, he made another declaration of independence on behalf of Bangabandhu'"ProfA KM Reazul Hassan,chainman of the National Curriculum and Textbook Board,
