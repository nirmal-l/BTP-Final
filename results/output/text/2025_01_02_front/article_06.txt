Air India first airline to roll out Wi-Fi for select flights in country

AIR INDIA on Wednesday rolled out Wi-Fi intemet connectivity services on board domestic flightsoperated by select aircraft in its fleet - Airbus A350, Boeing 787-9. and some Airbus A321neo. The Tata Group-owned airline was already offering internet connectivity on international flights operated by these aircraft as part of a pilot programme. Air India is the first Indian airline to offer intemet connectivity on domestic flights. In a statement, the airline said Wi-Fi will be offered free of charge for a limited introductory period, but did not specify the duration.
