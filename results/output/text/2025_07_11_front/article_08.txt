REALISING THE imperativeto acquire expertise on engine technology, the government has decided to pursue this objective by pushing Defence Research & Development Organisation (DRDO) to join hands either with Britain's Rolls-Royce or France's Safran to co-develop aircraft engines in India. "Engines are a constraining factor... a strategic decision has tobe taken. Both Rolls-Royce and Safran are contenders for fifthgeneration Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft. A Cabinet note willbe prepared from the DRDO side and circulated," an official told The Indian Express. Both the companies have offered tocollaborate with DRDO's Bengaluru-based Gas Turbine Research Establishment lab and provide full ToT and IPR, the government official said. A Cabinet note will be floated for this soon and the process has been initiated bythe DRDO, the official said.
