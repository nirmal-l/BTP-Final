Agri, auto in focus as India team heads back to US next week for trade talks

RECIPROCAL TARIFF PAUSE RUNS OUT AUG1

WITHA fresh window for negotiations before the US 'reciprocal tariffs' come into effect on August 1, Indian negotiators led by Rajesh Agrawal, Special Secretary in the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, are likely to leave for Washington next week toiron out pending issues like agriculture and auto sector tariffs that are holding back the interim trade agreement on goods, a government official told The Indian Express. The Indian team had returned from the last round ofnegotiations on July 4, days before the reciprocal tariff pause was earlier set to run out on July9.

Last week, the US said thenew tariffrates would come into effect on August 1, and there would be "no extension". It said that if the August 1 deadline was not adhered to, countries would go back

to the April 2 tarifflevels - theUS had announced 26 per cent reciprocal tariffs on India then. "Aproposal for anotherround of trade negotiations with the US has been sent to the top brass of the government. Negotiators are confident that the differences with the US team on agriculture, which havebeen holding back the agreement, willbe resolved.
