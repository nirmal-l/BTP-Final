Tennis player shot dead by her father at Gurgaon home

RADHIKA YADAV (25), a state-level tennis player, was shot dead, allegedly by her father, at their residence in Gurgaon's Sushant Lok-I on Thursday. Police said the accused, Deepak Yadav (54), was arrested, and the licensed revolver allegedly used in the crime was recovered. According to police, Deepak Yadav faced "taunts" for relying on his daughter's income.
