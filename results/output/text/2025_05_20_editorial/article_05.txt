As economy grows, credible and timely datais non-negotiable NDIA'S MINISTRY OF Statistics and Programme Implementation has unveiled anew I and improved version of the Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) and, with that, started providing timely dataon the state of the labour market. The PLFS was launched in 2017 as an annual survey to replace the quinquennial Employment-Unemployment Surveys. Up until now, apart from the annual picture of India's unemployment, PLFS would provide a quarterly update, but only for urban areas, Last week, the MoSPI shared the first-ever monthly PLFS data - for April.
