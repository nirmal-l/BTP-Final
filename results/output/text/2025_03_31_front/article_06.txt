Il coaches of Bengaluru-Kamakhya train derail in Odisha, one killed

AT LEAST one person died and several people were injured on Sunday after 11 coaches of the Bangalore-Kamakhya Superfast Express derailed near Nergundi station in Odisha's Cuttack. Sources in the East Coast Railway (ECoR) said the derailment occurred at 11.54am. ECoR officials identified the dead passenger as West Bengal resident Subhankar Roy. A woman passenger suffered serious injuries and two men were being treated for minor injuries, Officials said. Several others who were injured were given first aid at the site of the derailment.
