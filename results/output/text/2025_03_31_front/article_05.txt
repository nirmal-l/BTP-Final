Maharashtra: Explosion in Beed mosque, two arrested

TWO PERSONS were arrested on Sunday, hours after they allegedly planted gelatin sticks inside a mosque in Maharashtra's Beed district triggering an explosion. Although no one was injuredin the incident, the low-intensity explosion caused cracks in the structure, Police identified the two suspects as Vijay Rama Gavhane and Shriram Ashok Sagde. They said that after the blast occurred around 2.30 am, Gavhane posted a video of him holding gelatin sticks on his Instagram account, leading to his and Sagde's arrests, According to the FIR, a procession was organised in Beed's Ardhamasla Saturday evening in which people from all communities participated. However, around 9.30 pm, the two suspects arrived at the spot and started making communal remarks, the FIR said.
