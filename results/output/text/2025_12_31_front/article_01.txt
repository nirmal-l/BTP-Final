Lalmani Verma Lucknow, December 30

This is the third change in the SIR schedule in Uttar Pradesh since the exercise started in 12 states/Union Territories on October 27. Uttar Pradesh is set to be the state with the highest deletions, with Tamil Nadu cominginsecond and third, as per the states' respective draft rolls published earlier this month.

AS THE Election Commission Tuesday postponed publication of the Uttar Pradesh draft roll from Wednesday to January 6, provisional data accessed by The Indian Express shows the ongoing Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls could lead to the deletion of nearly 2.89 crore electors or 18.70% of the electorate from the rolls.
