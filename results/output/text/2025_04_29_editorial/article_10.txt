APRIL 29, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

THE ARMY WAS putonalertin Surat afterthe police fired two rounds to disperse a violent mob that set fire to a large number of huts in the Ambanagarslum area. Incidents of arson, rioting, looting of shops and stone-throwing were reported. Four people were injured in the firing. Twocolumns of Army jawans have reached Surat to help the civilian authorities in maintaining peace.
