Ourfriendship started on that sultry afternoon; he shared anecdotes of his home in Doharpara, Pabna; his neighbours; his days as a promising poet and literary editor in the 1970s; his expulsion from Bangladesh; the years in Kolkata; his association with the family of the eminent poet and essayist, Annada Shankar Ray; as a student of Comparative Literature at Jadavpur University, and howhe left for Germany in 1987, having spent more than a decade in India. Idon'trecall exactly when started calling him "Daud da" and selecting poems together tobe translated.
