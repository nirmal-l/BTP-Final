US clears sale of Excalibur shells, Javelin missile systems to India

OVER $90-MILLION DEAL ECONOMY pacer

AmritaNayak Dutta New Dethi, November 20

THE US has approved the sale of Excalibur guided artillery projectiles and the anti-tank Javelin missile system and related equipment worth over $90 million to India, markinga significant step forward for Delhi's big-ticket military procurements from Washington.
