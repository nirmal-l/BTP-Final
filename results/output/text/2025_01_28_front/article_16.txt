As part of an experiential learning programme under the Centre's National Education Policy 2020, since January 15, 2024, students from districtsacross the country have been spending time at the Vadnagar school. It was at Vadnagar Kumar Shala Number 1 - part of the same complex as the new Prerana school - that Prime Minister Narendra Modi wasadmitted toClass 1 on June 13, 1956. At the Prerana school, each batch has 20 students from 10 districts - one boy and a girl aged between 14 and 17 years per district - who stay on campus and learn, among other things, whatit means b live with others from different cultures and sampledifferentcuisines in the mess. The students also do yoga each day and visit the local historical sites. Though 43 batches of 860 students from Classes 9-12 have already spent a week each at Prerana since itopened last year, on January 15, 2024, thecampus was formally inaugurated on January 16 this year by Union Home Minister Amit Shah, accompanied by Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan.
