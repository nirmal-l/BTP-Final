DAYS AHEAD of Prime Minister Narendra Modi's likely visit to Manipur, the Centre on Thursday claimed that Kuki civil society groups had agreed to allow free movement in Manipur. The Centre also announced the signing of anew Suspension of Operations (SoO) agreement with Kuki militant groups, under which they would relocate their camps from near Meitei areas and agree to "the territorial integrity of Manipur". The start of free movement between the Kuki and Meitei areas would be the most significant step towards normalcy in the state that has been in the grip of ethnic violence since May 2023.
