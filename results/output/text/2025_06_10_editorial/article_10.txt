18 KILLED IN GUJARAT

INAGRUESOME orgy of violence, atleasteight persons, includingthree women and two children, were bumt alive in their houses and six others were killed in Army firing in the curfew-bound Dariapur-Kalupur area of Ahmedabad. The day's toll in Gujarat was 18. Violence flared up in the Dariapur-Kalupur area under Army control whena mob bolted doors of some houses and set them on fire.
