In first, J&K AAP MLA held under PSA for 'disturbing public order'

JAMMU AND Kashmir's lone Aam Aadmi Party MLA from Doda, Mehraj Malik, was apprehended bythe police on Monday on charges of disturbing public order and detained under the stringent Public Safety Act for one year.
