We need to work towards a strategic aim with Pakistan that makes it shun proxy wars once and for all

It is imperative to appreciate that India and Pakistan have lived with wars since their birth as sovereign nations. This 'limited' near-war is surely going to add to that legacy for generations on both sides. In order to become a developed country, India needs to resolve the Pakistan issue once and for all. The current near-war may be an opportunity to revisit the legacy of past wars while crafting a well-thought-out and patient response to achieve 'strategic peace' with Pakistan.
