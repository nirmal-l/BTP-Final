May 10, 1985, Forty YEARS AGO

INDIA-SRI LANKA TIES

INDO-SRI LANKAN RELATIONS touched their lowest ebb in the recent past. Intemperate statements were made by the Sri Lankan Minister criticising the Indian Minister of External Affairs, Khurshid Alam Khan's remarks, followed by the arrest of the Press Trust of India correspondent Krishan Anand in Colombo. The Sri Lankan Deputy High Commissioner was called to the Foreign Office, and India's disapproval was conveyed.
