In Bengal, officials flag deletion fears as EC generates notices to electors

BIHAR PLAYBOOK GETS PUSHBACK Write to CEO: EROs may not even know of deletions, but will be blamed

Atri Mitra, Ravik Bhattacharya & Damini Nath Kolkata, New Delhi, December 26

AS ELECTORAL Registration Officers (EROs) in West Bengal prepare to hold hearings from Saturday to decide whether lakhsofelectors will remainon the rolls, an association ofstate service officers has flagged a seriousconcern over the potential of large-scale "system-driven" deletions the EROs could be blamed for, despite not being involved in generating the notices. In a letter to West Bengal Chief Electoral Officer Manoj Agarwal, with a copy to Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar's office, the West Bengal Civil Service (Executive) Officers' Association on Wednesday flagged the "suo motu system-driven deletion ofelectors from the draft electoral rolls in West Bengal in the ongoing SIR process bypassing the statutory role of EROs". The EROs are the sole and competent authority to issue notices if they doubt an elector's eligibility, including citizenship, as per the Representation of the People Act, 1950.
