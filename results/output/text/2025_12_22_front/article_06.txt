President Murmu grants assent to VB-G RAM GBill

Express News Service New Delhi, December 21

Murmu has given her assent to the Viksit Bharat-Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Bill (VB-G RAMG), 2025, which aims to repeal the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA), 2005, and provide fora newruraljobs programme with astatutory wage employment guarantee of 125 days every financial year for rural households.
