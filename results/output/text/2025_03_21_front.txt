The medication comes in a single-dose vial and is designed to be self-administered as a weekly injection. "Mounjaro (tirzepatide) will be available, upon obtaining a valid prescription, in registered pharmacies in the coming weeks," the company said. Tirzepatide is part of anew group of medicines called incretinmimetics, which work by imitating natural guthormones. These hormones help the body in three ways: they boost insulin production, reduce the release of glucagon(which triggers glucose productionin the liver),and stow digestion to curbappetite.
