This hardening of stance has primanily todo with politics, both domestic and US President Donald Trump's tariff attacks devoid of any economic rationale. Trump has muddied the waters for India to enter into any constructive negotiations on agricultural trade. That's unfortunate. Trade in farm produce between the two countries is booming. Based on the trends during January-June, both exports from India tothe US and from the latter to the former are set to touch record highs in 2025.
