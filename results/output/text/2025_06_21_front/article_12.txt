'They said we were taking cows, must be killed... beat us and made a video'

BEFORE DYING, BHOPAL MAN RECORDED STATEMENT TO POLICE

Bhopal residents Junaid and Arman were returning from Dhanaura village near Vidisha after loading their vehicle with cattle when, at around 2.30 am, another vehicle blocked their way, forcing them to stop. A group of around 10-15 people approached, started assaulting them, and recorded videos.

"They were making a video of us...while theybeatus... After this, they sat in our vehicle and made us sit in the car, continuing to beat us, and brought me and Arman to Sanchi police station," a seriously injured Junaid

told police in Vidisha district's Sanchi, after which an FIR was registered on June 6. On June 17, Junaid succumbed to his injuries. Purported videos of the incident have since gone viral, including those uploaded on social media by the main accused, Dhruv Chaturvedi.
