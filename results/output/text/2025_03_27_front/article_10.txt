GUWAHATI, MARCH 26

ASSAM POLICE arrested journalist Dilwar Hussain Mozumdar on Tuesday night afterdetaining him for several hours when he went to cover a protest against the Assam Co-operative Apex Bank (ACAB). On Wednesday evening, hewas grantedbail by thecourt of the Chief Judicial Magistrate. However, his lawyer Santanu Borthakur said that because it was evening by the time he was granted bail, the bail bonds could not be submitted and he will spend the night in police custody. Hussain is a reporter for the Assam-based digital media portal The CrossCurrent and the assistant general secretary of the Guwahati Press Club.
