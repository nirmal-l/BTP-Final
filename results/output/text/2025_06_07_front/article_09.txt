Bengaluru stampede: RCB head of marketing among 4 arrested, CM's political secretary sacked

TWO DAYS after the stampede in Bengaluru which left 11 people dead, police on Friday arrested four people, including the marketing head of Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) and the vice-president of the event management firm, DNA. The Karnataka government also removed Chief Minister Siddaramaiah's political secretary K Govindaraju from his post, and replaced the Additional Director General of Police (ADGP) of state intelligence, Hemant M Nimbalkar.
