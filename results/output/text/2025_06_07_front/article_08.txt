RBI surprises with 50 bps cut in key rate to boost growth CRR slashed by 100 bps for liquidity; interest rates on loans, deposits to fall

THE RESERVE Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee delivered a frontloaded rate cut Friday by cutting the repo rate by 50 basis points to 5.50 per cent in amove aimed at propelling economic growth. Alongside the steeper than expected rate cut, the RBI also announced a stag-gered reduction in the cash reserve ratio by 100 basis points, starting September 6, 2025, to facilitate faster monetary policy transmission. Borrowers, particularly those with home loans, will benefit from the rate cut, with lending rates expected to decrease by 50 basis points immediately.

However, depositors and savers can expect lower returns on their bank deposits in the coming months as banks revise their deposit and savings bank rates. The 50 bps reduction in the policy repo rate, taken ina 5:1 majority, was against the market consensus of a 25 bps cut and marks the third consecutive rate cut and a total reduction of 100 bps since February 2025. One basis point (bps) is one-hundredth of a percentage point. The reduction in the CRR - the percentage of a bank's total deposits that itis required to maintain in liquid cash with the RBlasa reserve - would release primary liquidity of aboutRs2.5 lakh crore to the banking system by December 2025.
