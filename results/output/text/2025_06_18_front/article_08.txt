One link ties all seven Portuguese nationals in A-I crash - Diu roots

THERE WERE seven Portuguese nationals on the passenger list of the Air India flight from Ahmedabad to London, which crashed on June 12. It turns out ail seven had their roots in the island of Diu, the former Portuguese colony on the western coast of India. According to local officials, a total of 14 deaths from the crash were of passengers with links to Diu, including nine from Bucharwada gram panchayat. The sole survivor,

Viswashkumar Ramesh, is among others with roots in Diu whoare British nationals.
