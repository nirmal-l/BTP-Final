12 states & UTs, 51 cr voters: After Bihar, SIR next round from Nov 4

ASSEMBLY POLLS IN 4 OF 12 NEXT YEAR

Assam excluded for now; EC asks voters to submit forms by Dec 4

THEELECTION Commission (EC) on Monday announced the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) ofelectoral rolls in 12 states and Union Territories, where all of the registered 51 crore electors would have to submit enumeration forms by December 4 to remain on the draft roll. All those who cannot be traced back to the last intensive revision of rolls in 2002-2005 would be required tosubmit documents to establish their eligibility to remain on the final list.
