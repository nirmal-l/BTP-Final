CLASHES IN GUJARAT NAM & NaMIBIA LONGOWAL VISIT

IN AHMEDABAD, AFTER the army moved in, most parts of the walled city remained quiet but tense, but the most thickly populated area of Khadia witnessed a battle between stone-pelting mobs and the police, who resorted to firing, killing one person. An indefinite curfew was clamped in Surat when 3,000 pro-reservationists indulged in violence. The police prevented a communal clash in another areaby arresting 74 people.

THE MADHYA PRADESH government appears to have forgotten that, way back in December,

it had announced - amid much fanfare - a judicial inquiry into the Bhopal gas leak disaster that had claimed over 2,000 lives. The terms of reference for the inquiry were announced on December 6, three days after the tragedy, and the single-member commission, headed by Justice N K Singh of the Madhya Pradesh High Court, was asked to submit its report by March 15. The commission's report is nowhere in sight.
