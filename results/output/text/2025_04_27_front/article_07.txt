Bengaluru-based _ start-up Sarvamto build the countrys first indigenous artificial intelligence (Al) large language model (LLM) amid waves made by China's low cost model DeepSeek. The start-up, chosen from among 67 applicants, will receive support from the government in terms of compute resources to build the model from scratch. Sarvam is the first start-up to get approved for sops under India's ambitious Rs 10,370 crore IndiaAl Mission to build a model, with the government currently

assessing hundreds of other proposals. Sarvam said its model willbe capable of reasoning, designed for voice, and fluent in Indian languages, and it will be ready for population-scale deployment.
