India's oil imports from US surged over 270% in first four months of year

INDIA'S OIL imports from the US jumped over 270 per cent yearon-year in the first four months of 2025, underscoring Delhi's strategy of enhancing American imports amid trade pact negotiations and diversifying its

sources of crude oil in a volatile geopolitical and geo-economic environment. Negotiations between India and the US for an interim trade deal by July 9 have entered their final phase.
