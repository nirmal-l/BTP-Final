MEHLI MISTRY, a close confidant of Ratan Tata, ison his way outof Tata Trusts after a majority of trustees voted against his reappointment to the boards of the Sir Dorabji Tata Trust and the Sir Ratan Tata Trust. Three ofthe six trustees in the two trusts opposed Mistry's renomination through a circular resolution circulated last week. Mistry was first inducted as trustee in 2022, with his current three-year term expiring on October 28. He will have to step down from the governing boards of the Tata Trusts.
