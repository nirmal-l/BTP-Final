Three arrested over murder of journalist, property of key accused razed

CHHATTISGARH

ADAY after police discovered the body of 32-year-old joumalist Mukesh Chandrakar in a septic tank in Chhattisgarh's Bijapur district, three personshave been arrested, with the district authoritiesalso bulldozing alleged "illegal property" belonging toa key accused, Suresh Chandrakar. State Home Minister Vijay Sharma said Suresh is a Congress leader - acharge denied by the party, which claimed he joined the BJP in December. While Suresh is on the run, the three arrested accused have been identified as his brothers Ritesh Chandrakar and Dinesh Chandrakar, and construction supervisor Mahendra Ramteke. Police said it was Mukesh's cousin Ritesh who allegedly committed the murder with help from Mahendra, while Dinesh and Suresh helped dispose of the body.
