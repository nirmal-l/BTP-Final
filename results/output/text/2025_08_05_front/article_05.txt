'If true Indian, you would not say these things': SC raps Rahul for Army remarks

DEFAMATION CASE

THE SUPREME Court on Monday censured Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi over his alleged remarks against the Army in the wake of the 2020 Galwan clash with the Chinese, saying, "If you are a true Indian, you would not say all these things."
