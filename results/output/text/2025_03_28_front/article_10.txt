Gadkari meets Chandrashekhar Azad meets Akhilesh: Party to beat all parties

ARRIVING AT the birthday celebrations of Rashtriya Loktantrik Party (RLP) founder and Nagaur MP Hanuman Beniwal's son Ashutosh, Union minister Nitin Gadkari told the 10-year-old jokingly, "Become anything but a leader."

But going by the celebrations for Ashutosh's birthday, with a

VVP visitor list that started coming in Tuesday and has not flagged since, the boy's fate may be predestined. Beniwal is the lone MP of his party, with no MLAs. But Ashutosh's birthday festivities have drawn several Union ministers and former Chief Ministers, as wellas a surfeit of sitting and former MPs and MLAs.
