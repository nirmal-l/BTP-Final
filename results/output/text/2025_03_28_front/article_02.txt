3 policemen killed in gunbattle during J&K search for terrorists 3 from terror group also killed, 5 days after forest sighting; likely came from Pak

INCIDENT IN JAMMU'S KATHUA DISTRICT

THREE POLICEMEN and three militants were killed in a fierce gunbattle that broke out Thursday in Jammu and Kashmir's Kathua district, sources said. The militants are suspected to be part of a fresh group of around half a dozen who crossed over from Pakistan, and were

first spotted by a local couple in the forests at Saniyal village near the International Border on Sunday. After the local residents raised the alarm, security forces

began searches, and on Sunday evening, briefly exchanged fire with the militants. The encounter on Thursday took place on the fifth day of the

combing operation by joint security forces in Hiranagar subdivision of Kathua. The policemen killed Thursday were identified as Selection Grade Constables Tariq Ahmad from Reasi, Jaswant Singh from Londi in Hiranagar, and Balwinder Singh from Kanna Chak in Kathua. Sub-Divisional Police Officer (Border) Dheeraj Katoch was among four injured security personnel. The other injured personnel were Constable Bharat Chalotra, Special Police Officer Happy Sharma, and Army's Naik Gurinder Singh.
