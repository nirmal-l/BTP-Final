Chenab runs dry near Pak as sluice gates of Salal, Baglihar dams are closed

ARUN SHARMA

WATER IN the Chenab river in Jammu's Akhnoor area fell below waist level for the first time in years, prompting many surprised locals to gather on the riverbed on Monday.
