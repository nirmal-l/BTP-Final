Putin dials Modi, echoes Delhi: Bring Pahalgam terrorists, backers to justice

HOURS BEFORE the UNSecurity Council in New York was set to discuss the tense situation between India and Pakistan following the Pahalgam terror attack, Russian President Vladimir Putin echoed Delhi's position Monday when he spoke to Prime Minister Narendra Modi over phone and, according to the Indian government, said that the "perpetrators of the heinous attack and their supporters must be brought to justice". Moscow's readout of the call said Putin expressed his sincere condolences and "both sides emphasised the need foran uncompromising fight against terrorism in all its manifestations".
