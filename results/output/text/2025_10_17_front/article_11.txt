India's T20 captain Suryakumar Yadav is guest at Adda today

ONLY 15 months after inheriting India's T20 captaincy, Suryakumar Yadav has heiped build a halo of fearlessness around his team of young cricketers - more so, after India won the Asia Cup in Dubai last month after beating Pakistan in three politically charged encounters, including the final. His captaincy record in the shortest format is phenomenal with India having won 23 of 29 games for a win percentage of 82.75 per cent, the best among all Indian captains.
