IN THE early 1990s, far away from her home country that was in the midst of a bruising civil war, a girl from Galle sat on the bench by the window in Classroom 27 of Delhi's Hindu College, dreaming of home, debating Karl Marx and simply leading a "wonderful gang life"

with her friends. On October 16, she returned to that same seat, this time as Harini Amarasuriya, Prime Minister of Sri Lanka. "It is lovely to be back, lovely to see the current students. I become so hopeful when I see them," said Amarasuriya, dressed in a striking blue sari as she addressed students and teachers at her alma mater.
