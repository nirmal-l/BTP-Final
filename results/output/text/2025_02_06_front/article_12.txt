How Dalit couple argued own case, won damages for intellectual property loss

WITH THEIR laptop allegedly stolen and decades of research data destroyed duringan alleged eviction attempt by their house owner's son in Nagpur in 2018, Dalit scholars Dr Kshipra Kamlesh Uke and Dr Shiv Shankar Das say the only option

they had was to "fight it out". It wasa fightthat would end up in the courts, which sat in judgment on a fundamental question of law: does 'damage to property' in the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Preventionof Atrocities) Act include intellectual property (IP)?
