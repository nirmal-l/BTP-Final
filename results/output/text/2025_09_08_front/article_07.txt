Railway cops extorting passengers in Mumbai, 13 suspended in 5 months

PASSENGERS TARGETED AT LUGGAGE CHECK

OVER THE past five months, 13 Government Railway Police (GRP) personnel, including a Senior Inspector, have been suspended on charges of extorting money from passengers at railway stations across Mumbai in what officers say is an "unprecedented" crackdown against an "organised racket". Express that the racket was found to be operating at railway stations in and around Mumbai, especially those that cater to long-distance trains, suchas Mumbai Central, Dadar, Kurla, Bandra Terminus, Borivali, Thane, Kalyan and Panvel.
