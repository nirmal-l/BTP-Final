OCTOBER 13, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

PLOT TO KILL PM JANATA RESOLUTION

A PLOT TO assassinate Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi during his visit to the UK has been foiled by the British police. In day-long raidsin the north of England, the Midlands and London, the police took under preventive detention 15 people, including six activists of the International Sikh Youth Federation. The others belonged to the extremist Jammu and Kashmir Liberation Front.
