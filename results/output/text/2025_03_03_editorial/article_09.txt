MARCH 3, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

BIHAR POLL VIOLENCE

THE FIRST PHASE of the assembly elections, whichwasby and large peaceful in Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh, was marred by violence at several polling booths in Bihar whichleftat least 20 persons dead and over 100 injured. All the 320 Assembly constituenciesin Madhva Pradesh and parts of Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and Maharashtra were covered inthe first phase of the poll. The remaining constituencies in these three states will be covered on March 5. Polling was suspended in several booths following violent group clashesand attempts tosnatch ballot boxes.
