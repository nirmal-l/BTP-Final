AGP emergeslargest party

IN THE Assam Assembly elections, thenewly formed Asom Gana Parishad (AGP) was emerging as the singlelargest party with Congress-l and others far behind. The AGP had won 38 of the 68 seats declared so far. TheCongress-I could manage to win only 13 seats. The United Minority Front came third with sixseats. The Chief Minister, Hiteswar Saikia, won from Nazira but seven of his ministerial colleagues were defeated.
