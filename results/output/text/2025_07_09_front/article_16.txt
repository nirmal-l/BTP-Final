MNS-Sena (UBT) hit Mumbai streets on Marathi issue, BJP & Sena try to firefight

EXPRESS NEWS SERVICE

THE PROTESTS over Marathi blewupinMaharashtraTuesday as political workers of the Maharashtra Navnirman Sena (MNS) and ShivSena(UBT), and activists of the Marathi Ekikaran Samiti, hit the streets in Thane. As the ruling coalition of the BJP and Shiv Sena tried to contain the damage, Transport Minister Pratap Sarnaik came to join the protest, but was turned away.

The protest had been called in response to abandh by shopkeepers in Thane last week over the assault on one of them in Mira-Bhayandar by MNS supporters, who demanded he speak in Marathi. The MNS had announced soon after the July 3 protest that it would hold a counterprotest on July 8, along with its new partner, Shiv Sena (UBT).
