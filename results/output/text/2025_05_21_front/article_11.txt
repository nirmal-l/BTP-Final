Delegations good move... members to convey govt stand: Chidambaran

REFLECTING THE friction within the Congress over the stand to take on different aspects of Operation Sindoor, senior party leader and former Union home minister P Chidambaram has said he welcomes the government decision to send all-party delegations to foreign capitals to explain India's position. While the Congress has objected tothe government picking the dele-gates on its own, Chidambaram underlined that the teams were there not to represent any party's stand but the government's.
