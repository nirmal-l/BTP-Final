4 Indian firms among over 'involvement' in Iran crude oil trade

THE UNITED STATES has sanctioned four India-based companies over their alleged involvement in trade and transportation of Iranian crude oil and petroleum products. These four are among the 30 persons and vesselsin various countries on which sanctions were imposed by the US Departmentof the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), and the US Department of State as part of the Trump administration's "maximum pressure campaign" on Iran.
