To build confidence, draw tourists, Omar and his Cabinet to meet in Pahalgam today

TOINSTIL confidence in the wake of the Pahalgam terror attack and encourage tourists to return to Kashmir, Chief Minister Omar Abdullah and his cabinet will move out of the capital, Srinagar, to tourist resorts of Pahalgam and Gulmarg for two days.
