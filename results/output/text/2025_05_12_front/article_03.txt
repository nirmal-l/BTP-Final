IC-814 and Pulwama perpetrators among 100 terrorists killed at terror sites: DGMO

THE PAKISTAN Army lost 35-40 personnel along the Line of Control in heavy crossfire over the last fewdays, andthe Pakistan Air Force lost "a few" aircraft, apart from suffering massive damage to assets and air bases during retaliatory high-precision strikes by Indian armed forces as part of Operation Sindoor. These details were made public Sunday, a day afterthe announcement of an end to military actions by the twocountries,

at a media briefing by Director General of Military Operations Lt General Rajiv Ghai and his Indian Air Force and Navy counterparts, Air Marshal AK Bharti and Vice Admiral A N Pramod.
