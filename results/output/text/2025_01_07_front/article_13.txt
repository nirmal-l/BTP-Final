Unprecedented rush to be in Delhi voter list after last date, needs greater scrutiny: CEO AAP vs BJP over additions, deletions from electoral list

AHEADOF the announcementof the Assembly election schedule for Delhi - it isexpected any day now - its Chief Electoral Officer Monday flagged an "unprecedented" rush for new voter registration since December 16 and said this "needs greater scrutiny". The office of the CEO, ina statement, said it received new voter registration applications from 5.1 lakh people after the

cut-off date of November 28. It said this was over and above the 3.08 lakh new voters added to the roll during the special summary revision in October. "This trend of unprecedented increase... and that too after 20 days of the last date of receiving claims and objections as scheduled.. is unexpected and needs greater scrutiny," the CEO said. "Directions are issued to... scrutinise each and every form strictly as per ECI guidelines. 100% field verification shall be done... to identify the genuineeligible persons. EROs are alsoconducting personal hearings in doubtful cases as per ECI guidelines," the statement said, adding that eight FIRs had been filed against 24 people for submission of false or tampered documents for obtaining voter IDs. Over the past month, the AAP has accused the BJP of filing objections letters and getting the names of "bona fide voters" deleted from the voter list.
