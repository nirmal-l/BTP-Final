May 21, 1985, Forty YEARS AGO

FIVE-DAY WEEK SARKARIA COMMISSION INDIRA GANDHI CASE

CENTRALGOVERNMENT OFFICES and establishments all over the country will observe the five-day week schedule with Saturday and Sunday as holidays from June 3. Government offices will have an extra hour every day to offset the loss in the number of working hours. The official press note claimed that a five-day week, which had been found successful in advanced countries, would increase the overall efficiency of the governmental machinery.
