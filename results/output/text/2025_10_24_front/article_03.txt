SEAT-SHARING DEAL STILL AWAITED

(From left) VIP chief Mukesh Sahani, CPI (M-L) Liberation's Dipankar Bhattacharya, RJD leader Tejashwi Yadav and Cong leader Ashok Gehlot in Patna on Thursday. Rahul Sharma

AFTER DAYS of negotiations, the Congress Thursday finally announced RJD leader Tejashwi Yadav as the chiefministerface of the Mahagathbandhan. In addition, Vikassheel Insaan Party (VIP) chief Mukesh Sahani was declared the alliance's deputy chief minister face, with Congress leader Ashok Gehlot saying they could consider other names too as Deputy CMs after the polls.
