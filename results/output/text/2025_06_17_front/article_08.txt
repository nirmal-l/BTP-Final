Sibal questions SC inquiry, move to impeach Justice Varma: Why didn't police seize cash, file panchnama, FIR

SENIOR ADVOCATE and Rajya Sabha MP Kapil Sibal, who has been closely involved with two impeachment proceedings against judges in the past, has said there is no evidence to initiate the same against Justice Yashwant Varma. The government is planning to bring an impeachment motion against the Delhi High Court judge, who was transferred to Allahabad after stacks of cash were allegedly discovered at his official residence.

Pointing to the fact that the Delhi Police had not filed an FIR in the case, nor produced the alleged half-burnt currency notes, Sibal questioned the in-house Supreme Court probe against Justice Varma.
