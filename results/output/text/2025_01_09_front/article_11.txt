India hands over defence equipment and stores to the Maldives

INDIA "HANDED over defence equipment and stores to the Makdives" and Defence Ministers Rajnath Singh and Mohamed Ghassan Maumoon met in New Delhi Wednesday as the two countries moved to restore ties after a hiatus that spread rancour in the twocapitals. Maumoon, 44, isthe younger son of former Maldives President Maumoon Abdul Gayoom who ruled the country for three decades. He had served inthe Presidency of hisuncle, Abdulla Yameen Abdul Gayoom, as State minister at the President's office and later at Social Affairs.
