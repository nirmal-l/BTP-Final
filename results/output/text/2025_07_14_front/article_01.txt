GST reforms: Home Minister set to open talks for consensus on rate rationalisation Contentious issues on table, require Central ministries and states on board

TO IMPART urgency to the process of overhauling the Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime, Union Home Minister Amit Shah is set to initiate discussions withall stakeholders - states as well as Central ministries - to build consensus and resolve contentious outstanding issues.

Amongst the many proposals on the table, the one to do away with the 12 per cent slab has been hanging fire for long. This involves shifting some items to the lower 5 per cent slab, and some others to the higher 18 per cent slab. Though this would simplify the multiple rate structure, it is estimated to result in significant revenue losses of Rs 70,000-80,000 crore for the Centre and states combined, top sources said.
