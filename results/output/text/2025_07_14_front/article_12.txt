CM Omar slams 'Martyrs' Day' detentions, says he was under house arrest too

'Unelected government locked up the elected government'

JAMMU AND Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah claimed that he was put under "house detention" Sunday, hours after several leaders made similar allegations, as the Valley observed its Martyrs' Day. Until the abrogation of Article 370 in 2019, July 13 was officially commemorated as Martyrs' Day in Jammu and Kashmir and a public holiday was observed.
