THE MISSING SOVIET diplomat, lgor Gueja, has defected tothe US, where he has been granted political asylum, official sources in New Delhi said. Gueja,a 37-year-oldthird secretary in the Soviet information departmentin New Delhi, had vanished on March 17 while on a morning stroll in the Lodi Gardens. A Soviet embassy source described the report of Gueja's defectionas "unbelievable".
