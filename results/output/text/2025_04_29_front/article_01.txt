ONE-DAY ASSEMBLY SESSION OVER PAHALGAM TERROR ATTACK

DESCRIBING PUBLIC outrage in Kashmir over the killing of 26 tourists in Pahalgam on April 22 as "the beginning of the end of terrorism", MLAs across the Treasury and Opposition Benches in the Jammu & Kashmir Assembly here Monday resolved "toresolutely defeat the nefarious designs of those who seek to disturbthecommunal harmony and progress of the nation and of J&K". "This House is mindful of the sinister design behind the selective targeting of the victims of this attack," said a three-page resolution unanimously adopted by the Assembly, which heid a one-day special session Monday in the wake of the Pahalgam terror attack.
