Govt blocks 16 Pak YouTube channels, objects to BBC use of word 'militants'

THEGOVERNMENT hasbanned 16 Pakistani YouTube channels for disseminating provocative and communally sensitive content, and false and misleading narratives and misinformation

against India, its Army and security agencies following the Pahalgam terror attack, officials said Monday. It has also conveyed its displeasure to the BBC over its coverage of the terror attack, objecting to the use of the word "militants", instead of terrorists. The Pakistani YouTube channels that have been blocked include some leading channels like Dawn News, Samaa TV, Ary News, and Geo News. A source said the decision to block the channels was taken on the back of the Pahalgam terror attack, and the recommendation was made by the Ministry of Home Affairs. The 16 channels have 63.08 million followers.
