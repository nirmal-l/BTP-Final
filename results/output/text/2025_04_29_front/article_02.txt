The Rafale-M can operate from Indian carriers with short deck and ski jump

Officials told The Indian Express that a key India-specific enhancement will be that the aircraft will be able to take off and land from a short deck with a ski jump, which is a feature of Indian aircraft carriers. INS Vikrant, for instance, uses a Short Take-Off But Arrested Recovery (STOBAR) configuration. This means it launches aircraft using a ski jump and recovers them using arrestor wires. French aircraft carrier Charles de Gaulle has alarge, flat flight deck design with steam catapults and arresting gears. Officials said that among other weapons, ASTRA Mk1 airto-air Beyond Visual Range Missile will be integrated into the aircraft.
