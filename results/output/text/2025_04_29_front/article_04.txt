Owaisi: Hindu brothers killed, Pak wants to instigate riots

AIMIM chief and Lok Sabha MP Asaduddin Owaisi

DEMANDING THAT Islamabad be placed in the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) grey list for terror financing, AIMIM president and Lok Sabha MP Asaduddin Owaisi said Monday that Pakistan wants to instigate communal riots in Indiaand that "our Hindu brothers were killed" by terrorists in Pahalgam to senda message that "non-Muslims cannot" visit Kashmir. Addressing a press conference in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Owaisi said, "Be it ISI or ISIS or the deep state establishment of Pakistan, they want communal riots between Hindusand Muslims. The reason why our Hindu brothers were killed was that they wanted to tellus that non-Muslims cannot come to Kashmir. That's their motive. We appeal to Indians to keep political differences aside and not allow those who want to weaken the country's unity." Owaisi also demanded that Pakistan be placed in the FATF grey list. "They are funding terrorism from illegal money. Soit is important to bring Pakistan in the grey list. We will demand this from the Government. We should also do acyber attack on them, we have ethical hackers. We can also doa naval and air force blockade," he said. The Hyderabad MP said that as the Opposition, itis important to take action.
