Maj Gen Cardozo fought three wars: 1962, 1965 and, most notably, the India-Pakistan war of 1971 thatled to the creation of Bangladesh. In the final days of the war, during the Battle of Sylhet, Cardozo is known to have stepped on a landmine, following which his leg had to be amputated. He went on to become the first war-disabled officer to be approved forcommand ofan infantry Battalion.
