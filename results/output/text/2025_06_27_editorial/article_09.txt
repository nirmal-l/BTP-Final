JUNE 27, 1985, Forty YEARS AGO

AIR INDIA CRASH TTOHRA ON PUNJAB POLL SRI LANKA TALKS SOON

INVESTIGATORS SAY THEY may never determine what caused the crash of the Air India jet, which killed all 329 people on board. So far onlytwo per cent of the jet's fuselage has been recovered and its flight recorders, with vital data, are deep beneath the sea. Themeagre evidence prompted one US investigator to say he was not optimistic about finding the cause of the crash of the Boeing 747.

THESRI LANKAN government will hold preliminary talks with representatives of the

Tamil United Liberation Front and the militant groups in Thimphu, Bhutan "very soon" toresolve the island's ethnic problems. The Indian government will provide facilities like transport for all delegates. Officials declined to specify who would head the government team but indicated the possibility that it might be at the ministerial level.
