Maya and Murphy, last hope to find Telangana tunnel workers' bodies

ON MARCH6, Mayaand Murphy, two Belgian Malinois dogs that belong to Kerala Police'scadaver dog squad, briskty alighted from an Indian Air Force chopper and went straight to the Srisailam Left Bank Canal(SLBC) tunnel near Domalapenta of Telangana's Nagarkumool district. Here, they were handed the grim task of finding the bodies of eight men who were trapped in the

Telangana tunnel collapse that occurred on February 22.
