INDIA BEAT AUSTRALIA

INDIA BUNDLED AUSTRALIA out of the World Championship of Cricket on March 3, winning thefinal group'A' match by eight wickets. Australia lost the toss and, sent in to bat, were 163 all out in 49,3 overs. India reached 165 for two in 36.1 overs to lead the points table in group 'A' and go through to the semi-finals, along with Pakistan, who finished runners-up after victories over Australia and England. Australia, disappointingly for the hast country, ended with two points and England with nil. Ravi Shastri, the Indian all-rounder, was named man of the match after taking one wicket for 34 from 10 overs and hitting a valuable 51.
