Marcu 4, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

REUNITED PARTY

THE TWO FEUDING groups of the Kerala Congress - both constituents of the ruling United Democratic Front - formally came together to form a single party, marking an end to 68 months of mutual bickering and mud-slinging. P J Joseph, Revenue Minister and leader of the Joseph group, will be the chairmanofthe united KeralaCongress and KM Mani, Finance Minister and leader of the Mani group, the leader of the parliamentary party. With the inclusion of of P C Thomas, who moved over from the Democratic Janata Party, the united Kerala Congress will have 15 members in the state assembly. Both the Mani andJoseph groupswill have equal representation inthe party forums at all levels.
