REPOLL IN UP, BIHAR

REPOLLING WILL BE held in 24 polling stationsin Uttar Pradesh and 123 booths spread over 16 districts in Bihar following booth capturing and violence. While the Election Commission secretary, V K Rao, made the formal announcement with regard to there-polling in UP, the decision on fresh elections in Bihar was madein Patna One ofthecommission secretaries, who is now camping in Patna, is looking into the complaints of violence in the state. The death toll in the poll violence in Bihar shot up to 29, according to reports received from various parts of the state. There have been a large number of complaints of violence, snatching of ballot papers or ballot boxes in both UP and Bihar.
