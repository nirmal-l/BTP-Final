'WORLD BANK ON INDIA

DAVID HOPPER, THE World Bank's top policy Official for South Asia, said that the WB would expand its lending to India in the next three years. Hopper added that India was one of the world's most creditworthy nations and had been following every prudent fiscai policy, He projected a growth rate of six per cent over the next three years.

A SIGNIFICANT BREAKTHROUGH in India's peace efforts was achieved when the Sri Lankan government conceded two majordemands of the militant Eelam National Liberation Front (ENLF) regarding the functioning of the proposed ceasefire monitoring committee. Leaders of six Sri Lankan Tamil groups in New Delhi accepted Colombo's set of proposals.
