Captain among two Army personnel killed in IED blast along LoC

TWO ARMY personnel, including a Captain, were killed and another injured in anIED explosion near the Line of Control(LoQ)in Akhnoor sector of Jammu district Tuesday. TheAmny personnel wereon patrolling duty along the barbed wire that marks the LoC when they were hit by a powerful explosion around 3.50 pm, sources said, adding that the IED appeared to have been planted by militants from across the border. While two of the injured personnel later died of their injuries,

a third soldier was admitted to hospital where his condition was stated to be stable. Confirming the deaths, the Army identified the two dead as Captain Karanjit Singh Bakshi and Naik Mukesh. Ina post on X, Udhampurbased Northern Command said: "Lt Gen MV Suchindra Kumar and ali ranks of Dhruva Command solemnly salute the supremesacrificeofbravehearts Capt Karamjit Singh Bakshi and Naik Mukesh ina suspected IED blast in Akhnoor. Dhruva Command stands firm with the bereaved family in this hour of grief." Nagrota-based White Knight
