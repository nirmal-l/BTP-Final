SC to poll panel: Don't erase EVM data while it's being verified

THE SUPREME Court Tuesday asked the Election Commission of India (ECI) to ensure that polling data is not deleted from the Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) while these are being venfied.

The bench of Chief Justice of India Sanjiv Khanna and Justice Dipankar Datta said this while seeking the ECI's response toa plea by NGO Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR) which alleged thatthe ECI's procedure for verification of EVMs was not in accordance with the April 2024 order of the Supreme Court.
