The Big Bang theory suggests that the universe has a definite beginning, anda possible end. In contrast, the steady state theory, which was the mainstream theoryin the 1950s and 1960s, main-tains that the universe has always been the way it is, without any beginning or anend. The idea has become less popular over time, with mounting evidence that supports the Big Bang theory.
