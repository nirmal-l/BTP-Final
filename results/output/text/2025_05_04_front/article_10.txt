Govt bans backdoor imports, shuts doors on Pak-origin goods

TO PLUG LOOPHOLES and eliminate any possibility of Pakistani goods entering its territory, the government has imposed animmediate ban on the import or transit of all goods originating in, or exported from, Pakistan, aMay 2 notification of the Commerce and Industry Ministry said. This follows the termination of direct trade, marked by the closure of the Integrated Check Post (ICP) at AttarionApril24in the wake of the Pahalgam terror attack.
