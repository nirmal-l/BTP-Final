Centre notifies domicile rules to address Ladakh job, quota worries Ceiling on total reservation raised to 85 per cent excluding EWS category

THE CENTRE has notified aseries of regulations aimed at addressing Ladakh's concerns over jobs, quotas and cultural preservation. The new legal framework introduces adomicile-basedjobreservation system, recognition oflocal languages, and procedural clarity in civil service recruitment. As per the new regulations, a person has to be a resident of Ladakh for 15 years to get a

domicile certificate and be eligible for jobs, while the ceiling on total quotahas been lifted to 85%, excluding reservation for EWS. Since Ladakh is 90% tribal, it would virtually give reservation toall the indigenous Ladakhis. The regulations, issued on Mondayand Tuesday, come amid demands in Leh and Kargil for tribalautonomy and legal protection following Ladakh's bifurcation from Jammu and Kashmirin 2019. Way short of Sixth 1®@ Schedule

THE NEW regulations stop way short of guarantees in the Sixth Schedule. Akey omission is the absence of any restriction on land ownership by outsiders. The LAHDCs as they exist today remain administrative bodies without legislative power.
