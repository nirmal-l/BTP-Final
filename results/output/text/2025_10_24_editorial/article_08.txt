THE PRIME MINISTER, Rajiv Gandhi, has said the Non-Aligned Movement faced anew threat ofinterference and pressure from power blocs and called for the strengthening of the UN to resist pressures from powerful nations. THE PRIMEMINISTER, Rajiv Gandhi, conferred with the leaders of the two important neighbouring countries, China and Pakistan, in New York, Gandhi and Zhao Ziyang, Prime Minister of China, during theirhour-longtalks, resolved to settle the long-standing border dispute between the two countries.
