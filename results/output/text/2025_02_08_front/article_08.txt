To check doping and age fraud, Govt scraps cash awards for junior athletes

JUNIOR ATHLETES winning international medals will no longer receive cash awards from the government. This major Sports Ministry policy shift, which came into effect on February 1, is aimed at tackling the twin menace of doping and age fraud as well as "keeping the hunger of youngsters alive".

According to theold system, a gold medal at the World Championship would fetch ajunior athlete approximately Rs 13 lakh while a top of the podium finish at Asian or Commonweakh had acash prize of Rs 5 lakh. A Sports Ministry official said oneof the key factors behind this decision was to promote junior competitions as developmental events rather than making it all about a podium finish.
