'Lost my mother in Kumbh stampede... now doing the rounds for death certificate'

AFTER LOSING his mother inthe stampede at the Maha Kumbh on January 29, Dhananjay Kumar Gond (24) returned home to Bihar's Gopalganj - only to be told by the local administration and the police that he needed her death certificate towrapup the formalities. He retumed to Prayagrajon Monday and has since been making desperate rounds of hospitals and govemment offices - but in vain. With little clarity on who will issue death certificates, Dhananjayis one of several family members of stampede victims who have beenrunning pillar to post in Prayagraj.
