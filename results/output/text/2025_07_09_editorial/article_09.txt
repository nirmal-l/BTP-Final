9,1985, Forty YEARS AGO

SEVERAL OPPOSITION LEADERS have inter-preted the PM's press conference remarks about the Emergency as a threat that it might be reimposed. Leaders of the Janata Party, the BJP and the Lok Dal expressed shock at the PM's remarks and declared that they would mobilise the people against such dictatorial trends, The BJP president, Atal Bihari Vajpayee, said he was shocked to read the PM's statement that the Emergency was justified.
