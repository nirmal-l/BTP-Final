Subsidised grain scheme THE FINANCE Minister, V P Singh, announced three new measures in Parliament to improve the lot of those below the poverty line. The measuresinclude ascheme to provide subsidised food grains to people in the integrated tribal development projects inthe country. Singh also announced that ration card holders all over the country will not have any restriction on the quantities of wheat they can draw from fair price shops from nowtill March 31, 1986.
