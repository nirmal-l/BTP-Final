Gunshots fired, stones hurled as crowd clashes with police in Bareilly

TENSION MOUNTED in Bareilly in Uttar Pradesh Friday after police stopped people carrying 'I Love Muhammad' placards from assembling and used force to disperse them when some inthe crowd protested and allegedly hurled stones and raised slogans. Police said some protesters even opened fire. DIG Ajay Kumar Sahni said most people offered the Friday prayers peacefully and dispersed.
