SKY, Rauf fined 30% of match fee each, Farhan gets warning

ASIA CUP ROW

Skipper Suryakumar Yadav, Pakistan pacer Haris Rauf

INDIA CAPTAIN Suryakumar Yadav and Pakistan pacer Haris Rauf have been found guilty of breaching the International Cricket Council's code of conduct at the ongoing Asia Cup in the UAE.

After the hearings that concluded Friday, match referee Richie Richardson has fined both players 30 per cent of their match fee, while another Pakistan player Sahibzada Farhan has been let off with a warning for his gun celebration after reaching 50.
