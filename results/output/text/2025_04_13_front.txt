What makes the project complex yet exceptional, according to experts involved in it, is how it uniquely blends traditional building materials suchas lime plaster and sandstone with out-of-the-box ones - suchas a mixture of jaggery, urad (black gram), fenugreek, and wheat - to help reinforce its foundation. that the workremains faithful to the original.
