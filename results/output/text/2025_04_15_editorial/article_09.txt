ApRIL 15, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

INDIA AND BANGLADESH will begin talks in Dhakaat the foreign secretary level ina bid to open anew chapter in their bilateral relations, The foreign secretary will immediately start formal talks with his Bangladeshi counterpart. Officials said the entire gamut of bilateral relations, including outstanding disputes, would come up for review during the talks, which will be spread over two days.
