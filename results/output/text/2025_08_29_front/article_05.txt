RSS workers free to join agitations for Kashi and Mathura, says Bhagwat

'Only a matter of three (sites)... other side should offer for brotherhood sake'

ON THE third and final day of his three-day lecture seriesin Delhi, RSS Sarasanghchalak Mohan Bhagwat indicated that the Sangh supported the demand that the sites of the Gyanvapi Mosque in Varanasi and the Eidgah at Mathura be given to Hindus. Sangh swayamsevaks were free to associate themselves with any movement for these sites, and the other side could respond by giving up their claims as it was a "matter of just three", said Bhagwat.
