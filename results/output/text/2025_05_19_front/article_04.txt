Ashoka prof held for Op Sindoor remarks; deplorable, says Opp

A DAY after the Centre announced that the Opposition would be part of the global outreach on Operation Sindoor, the Haryana Police Sunday arrested Ali Khan Mahmudabad, associate professor and head of the Department of Political Science at Ashoka University in Sonipat, for remarks he posted on social media that, an FIR alleged, "disparaged women officers in the Indian Armed Forces and promoted communal disharmony". Sonipat DCP (Crime) Narinder Kadian said Mahmudabad was arrested in Delhi based ontwo separate FIRs lodged on complaints received from the Haryana State Commission for Women chair-person Renu Bhatia and Jatheri village sarpanch Yogesh Jatheri whois also the general secretary of BJP Yuva Morcha in the state.
