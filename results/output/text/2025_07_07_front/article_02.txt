DOMINATED BY upper-caste households, Saurath village of Madhubani in Bihar is known across the region for its annual "sabha" when Brahmin men of "marriageable age" getdressed as groomsand await a"wedding offer". These days, however, the wait in Saurathis for somethingelse. "Out of 2,200 domicile applications submitted from our panchayat alone, only 150 certificates have been issued so far," says Vibhakar Jha, son of Saurath panchayat mukhiya Kamini Devi.
