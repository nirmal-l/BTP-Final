After 25 years, man wanted for killing cabbies, selling off their vehicles held

DELHI POLICE has arrested a49- year-old man who was wanted for the past 25 years for allegedly robbing and killing four taxi drivers in Delhi and Uttarakhand between 1999 and 2001, a senior police officer said Sunday. "The accused, Ajay Lamba alias Bansi, was declared a proclaimed offender; he had been absconding for the past 25 years. He was wanted in connection with a murder case registered at New Ashok Nagar police station in 2001," said DCP (Crime Branch) Aditya Gautam. After extensive manual and technical surveillance, ateam led by Inspectors Rakesh Kumar and Anuj Kumar arrested Lamba in Delhi Saturday, the DCP said.
