THE PROPOSEDLIBERALISATION of imports with a view to exposing the Indianindustry to foreign competition and curtailment of the present export subsidies have become issuesof major controversyon the eve ofthe budget. The Finance Ministryseems to be in favour of both these measures but is facing stiff opposition from other ministriesas well as some experts. The matter has been taken to the Prime Minister who seems to have ordered a detailed investigation intoall aspects of the issuebeforea final decision is taken.
