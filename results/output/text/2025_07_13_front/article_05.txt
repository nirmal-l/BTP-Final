IN THE June 12 crash of Air India flight AI 171 in Ahmedabad, the Boeing 787-8 aircraft's two engine fuel control switches transitioned from 'RUN' to'CUTOFF position within a second of each other moments after lift-off, according to a preliminary report into the investigation of the worst aviation disaster in four decades involving an Indian airline. The report released post-midnight by the Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau (AAIB), an office attached with the Ministry of Civil Aviation and tasked with probing accidents and serious incidents involving aircraft, also said that on the cockpit voice recorder, one of the pilots can be heard asking the other why he cut off the fuel, to which the other pilot responded saying he did not. Clive Kunder was the co-pilot flying the aircraft while Sumeet Sabharwal was the pilot-in-command monitoring.
