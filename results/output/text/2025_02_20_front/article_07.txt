Hearing PIL on stampede, Delhi HC asks Rlys: Why sell excess tickets?

DAYS AFTER 18 people were killed in a stampede at the New Delhi Raitway Station, the Delhi High Courton Wednesday questioned the Railwayson the saleof excess tickets at railway stations. HearingaPiLonthe February 15 stampede, the division bench of Chief Justice DK Upadhyaya and Justice Tushar Rao Gedela sought a response from the Centre and Railways. "Ifyou fix the numberof passengers to be accommodated in a coach, then why do you sell... why do the number of tickets sold exceed that number? Thatis a problem... Ifyou implementa simple thing in letter and spirit, such a situation can be avoided. On rush days, you may increase that number to accommodate the rush... but not fixing the strength to beaccommodated in a coach, this provision appearsto have been neglected all along," the bench said in oral remarks.
