The oath of office and secrecy was administered to Deopura along with three more ministers by the Governor ata brief but solemn ceremony at the Raj Bhawan. THEBAIL APPLICATIONS of the three accused inthelndiraGandhi assassination case were rejected by the Additional Chief Metropolitan Magistrate. The magistrate held that the chargesheet against Satwant Singh, Kehar Singh and Balbir Singh had been submitted within 90 days of their arrest and as such they were not entitled to bail. The magistrate directed the prosecution to supply the accused with the copies of documents and statements of witnesses.
