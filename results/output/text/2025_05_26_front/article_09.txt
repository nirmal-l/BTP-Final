South Delhi house to travel: Bribes flowed via web of entities, finds ED

FORMER CMD OF UCO BANK ARRESTED

THE ENFORCEMENT Directorate, which arrested former UCO Bank CMD Subodh Kumar Goel on May 16 in connection witha case of alleged corruption and money laundering, has told a PMLA special court in Kolkata that he created a complex web of nine companies with cross holdings to receive kickbacks. According tothe ED, its probe

found that Goel received bribes from an iron ore company, a loan defaulter, through these entities and used the funds to buy property. He also forced the loan defaulterto fund his family's travels and purchase of personal items

such as goggles and dry fruits, the agency claimed. Goel is being probed in connection with an over Rs 6,000- crore loan default by Concast Steel and Power Ltd (CSPL), promoted by Sanjay Sureka, where UCO Bank alone had an exposure of Rs 1,400 crore.
