We had better understand the world view of the man under the bridge in ripped jeansif we are to come to grips with what the times portend. For, his demeanouris that of someone who has, actually, upended ways of thinking about modem life itself My 55-year-old visitor began life as a hand-to-mouth dairy farmer, progressing to becoming a middle man for major real-estate companies, who was trusted by villagers to deal with "outsiders" in the sale of their land. Hisbiography forms anintegral part of thestory of the makingofa new Indian type: Heis part of avery significant section of the Indian population whose lives make itimpossible to think through the binaries of "modem" and "pre-modern". The crux of the reenchantment lies - unlike the Weberian understanding of modernity - in what the anthropologist Arjun Appadurai correctly identified as 'the capacity to aspire'. Rather, the new cultures of aspirations - now spread across all strata of society, rather than limited to a privileged minority - have given rise to an imagination that ascribes success to the power of magic and the divine.
