IAEA: No radiation leak or release from any nuclear facility in Pak

THE GLOBALnuclear watchdog, International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), has said that there has been "no radiation leak" from any nuclear facilityin Pakistan after the escalated military engagement with India, The Vienna-based global nuclear watchdog's reply, toa query from The Indian Express, ties in with the earlier response by the Indian Air Force that India has not hit any target in Pakistan's Kirana

Hills, which is reported to house some nuclear installations.
