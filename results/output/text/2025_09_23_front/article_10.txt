2020 riots: SC notice to Delhi Police on bail pleas of Umar Khalid, 4 others

Khalid and the others were booked under UAPA

THESUPREME Court on Monday sought the response of the Delhi Police on the bail pleas of Umar Khalid, Sharjeel Imam, Gulfisha Fatima, Meeran Haider and Shifa Ur Rehman, all charged as conspirators in the 2020 Delhi riots. The notice was issued by a bench of Justices Aravind Kumar and N VAnjaria. The matter was last listed before a bench of Justices Aravind Kumar and Manmohan on September 19, but it was not taken up.
