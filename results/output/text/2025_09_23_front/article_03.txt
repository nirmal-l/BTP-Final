UP govt 'prohibits' caste-based rallies: 'Contrary to public order, national unity'

SP says cosmetic move, BJP allies say trying to understand order

FOLLOWING THE Allahabad High Court directive seeking removal of caste displaysin public, the Uttar Pradesh government issued a government order late Sunday night prohibiting "caste-based political rallies", calling them a threat to "public order" and "national unity". The move isexpected to have political implications ahead of the 2027 Assembly elections, with all UP-based parties drawing their strength from specific caste groups. The UP government order alsocoincides with the firstever caste count to be held in the country since 1933, in the coming Census, which followed a long agitation led by the Opposition, especially the Congress, seeking the same.
