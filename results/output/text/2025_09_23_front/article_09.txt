Kanpur FIR over Prophet signboard sparks protests in state, several arrested

SEVERAL PEOPLE have been booked and arrested after they took out protests across Uttar Pradesh, days after an FIR was lodged by Kanpur police over a board displaying the message, "I love Mohammad", which was put up as part of decorations for the annual Barawafat procession

to commemorate the birth of Prophet Mohammad. Protests against the Kanpur FIR were witnessed over the weekend at several districts, including Kaushambi, Pilibhit, Bareilly, Bhadohi, Lucknow, Unnao, and Kanpur.
