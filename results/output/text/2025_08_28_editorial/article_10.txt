COUP IN NIGERIA

THE 20-MONTH-OLD military government of Gen Mohammed Buhari in Nigeria was over-thrown in what appears to be abloodiesscoup. The nation he inherited was in aterrible financial state. The price of oil, virtually its only export earner, was in the doldrums, and foreign debts totalled about $15 billion. Afterthe coup, critics of the government complained of slow progress towards recovery, The army chief, Ibrahim Bahangida, has been named president.
