First, the US's focus has moved on from GMOs and other agricultural products to the purchase of Russian oil. it has not imposedtariffs on the EU and China, the two largest buyers of energy from Russia. Even if India had stopped the purchase of Russian oil, the goal-posts could have easily shifted to stopping purchases of Russian defence equipment, walking out of BRICS and not trading in the currencies of its other bilateral partners.
