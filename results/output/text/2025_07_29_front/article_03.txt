Consider Aadhaar and voter card, SC tells EC again; no stay on draft roll

BIHAR SPECIAL INTENSIVE REVISION Should be en masse inclusion, not en masse exclusion, says top court

THE SUPREME Court Monday refused to stop the Election Commission (EC) from publishing the draft electoral roll for Bihar, drawn up following the Special Intensive Revision (SIR), adding that the petitioners' concems had been taken care of by its July 10 order asking it to also consider Aadhaar and Electoral Photo Identity Cards (EPIC) for the exercise. A Bench of Justices Surya Kant and Joymalya Bagchi said the EC can continue to consider Aadhaar and voter cards for the enumeration, and told the petitioners that the Court can always step in and strike it downifthere is any illegality.
