Class 1 student dies, 2 injured as school gate falls on them in Jaisalmer

ACLASS 1 student died and two others were injured when the main gate of a school and the column holding it fell on them in Jaisalmer on Monday afternoon, Officials said. The incident took place atthe Government Girls' Senior Secondary School in Poonamnagar village after classesended for the day at around 1 pm Monday. The student who died has been identified as Arbaaz Khan. According to officials, he had come there from his school and was waiting for his sister. Another student, Priya, and teacher Ashok Kumar Soni sustained injuries. Following Arbaaz's death, his relatives protested outside the school.
