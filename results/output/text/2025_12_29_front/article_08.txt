Aravalli row simmering, Rajasthan opened 'urban hill terrain' to 'low-density activities Regulations approved in April allow farmhouses, resorts in areas that include Aravalli parts; open to review: Minister

Hamza Khan Jaipur, December 28

EVEN AS the Centre faces the heat over a potential threat to the Aravallis from mining, and the SupremeCourtinitiates suo motu proceedings that will begin Monday, the Rajasthan government, earlier this year, formally opened large parts of urban hill terrain to a range of

"low-density" activities. This was done under the Model Regulations for Conservation of Hills in Urban Areas, 2024, which the state government first came out with last yearand approved in April 2025. The Regulations, which replaced the 2018 hill conservation norms, classify hills into three categories based on slope.
