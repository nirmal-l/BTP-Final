GST on health, term insurance may be scrapped or cut to 5%

THE GOODS and Services Tax (GST)reforms expected around Diwalithis year could likely lead to scrapping of the tax on insurance policies - or at least a calibrated reduction to 5 per cent - making insurance more affordable, improving financial security for millions and accelerating insurance penetration in India. The long-anticipated reductionin GST on insurance is under active consideration by the govemment, althoughitis expected to result in annual revenue loss of approximately Rs 17,000 crore, said a source.
