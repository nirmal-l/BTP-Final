Daud Haider, exiled Bangladeshi poet, dies in Berlin at 73

THE FIRST exiled poet, writer and columnist of independent Bangladesh, Daud Haider - who lived in India for 13 years before leaving for Germany - died in Berlin on Saturday, his family and friends confirmed on Sunday. He was 73. A prominent figure in modern Bengali poetry, Haider was exiled by the Sheikh Mujibur Rehman governmentin 1974 after his poem criticising radicalism and bigotry in the country was published in a Bangla daily.
