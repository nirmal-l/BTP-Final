Amid judicial scrutiny, TN ministers Senthil Balaji, Ponmudy quit

ARUN JANARDHANAN

IN A reshuffle carried out amid legal scrutiny and political turbulence within the DMK, senior Tamil Nadu government ministers V Senthil Balaji and K Ponmudy resigned on Sunday, with Governor R N Ravi accepting their resignations after acting upon the recommendations of Chief Minister M K Stalin. The reshuffle, meant to project the DMK government's efforts to project stability amid mounting controversies and judicial scrutiny, had been anticipated following a week of speculation.
