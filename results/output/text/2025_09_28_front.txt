"There's amemory of what we were. We will build on that. The stream will become a river, the trees will grow back, but this time, we won't cut them. They hold the soil," Hamsa says, pointing to the timber logs that the boulders brought down with them, a visible sign of how deforestation made the soil too fragile to hold the rains. Caked mud, boulders strewn all over and the naked earth seem eerily similar to the trail of destruction after the recent land-slides in Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh.
