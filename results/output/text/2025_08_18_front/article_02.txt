SUO MOTU PLEA TO BE HEARD TODAY

THE SUPREME Court will hear on Monday a suo motu writ petition titled "Cadets disabled in military training struggle" after taking cognizance of reports published in The Indian Express on the plight of officer cadets who were discharged from the nation's top military training institutes on medical grounds - and have since been battling severe disability with shattered dreams and poor benefits. A bench of Justices B V Nagarathna and R Mahadevan will hear the matter. Sources said that a bench presided by Justice Nagarathna took cognizance of The Indian Express reports and placed the matter before Chief Justice of India B R Gavai. The Chief Justice then decided that the bench presided by Justice Nagarathna will hear the petition, the sources said.
