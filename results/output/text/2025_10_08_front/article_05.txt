Shoe thrown at CJI: Lawyer unapologetic; police question YouTuber

Speaking to The Indian Express, Additional Deputy Commissioner of Police (Noida) Sumit Shukla said YouTuber Ajeet Bharti was questioned at the ACP office "over his comments on the CJl (on social media) and was released later".
