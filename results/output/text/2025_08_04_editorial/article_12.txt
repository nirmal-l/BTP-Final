PRIME MINISTER RAJIV Gandhi called upon the police force to evolve new methods and techniques to combat terrorism, "which obtainsin most parts of the country inone form or the other". Gandhi observed that traditional police methods wouid not work to face the new challenge posed by terrorism. Right now, "even at the drop of the hat, we are calling the para-military forces and the Army. We must stop this", Gandhi pointed out

FORMER CHIEF MINISTER Farooq Abdullah has declared that he would cooperate with

Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi for ending the prevailing uncertainty in the state. He said he was for a newera of stability, peace and progress in Jammu and Kashmir, where people have been suffering because of the "failures" of the Shah government. He was pleased that PM Gandhi had himself started taking a "serious" view of the Kashmir situation and taken certain steps to find a solution.
