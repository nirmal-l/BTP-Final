A GRASSROOTS TEACHER

Pratima Barwa gave wings to dreams of young Adivasi hockey players HANCES ARE THAT most outside the modest Indian hockey universe would not have heard of Pratima Barwa. She did what few could - identify and nurture young talents in one of the sport's biggest catchment areas, Jharkhand's Simdega.
