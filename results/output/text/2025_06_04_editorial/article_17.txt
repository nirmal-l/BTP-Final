ASSAM TALKS JOINT STATEMENT

THE MONTH-LONG talks between the Centre and the Assam agitation leaders on resolving thevexed foreign nationals issue seem to have reached a crucial stage. Both Governor, Bhishma Narain Singh and Chief Minister, Hiteshwar Saikia are now in the capital to be available for consultations. However, according to informed sources, there are still some differences on the cut-off year as well as the question of disenfranchisement..
