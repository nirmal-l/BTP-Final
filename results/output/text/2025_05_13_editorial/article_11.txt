May 13,1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

AHMEDABAD VIOLENCE BLAST ARRESTS

THEARMY, WHICH took charge of the walled city, was given additional control of the labour areas of Gomtipur, Bapunagar and Saraspur where large scale violence broke out in the morning and continued unabated throughout theday. With seven bodies brought tothe civil hospital from Gomtipur, the death toll rose to 19. Twelve persons were killed - six in police firing and six in stabbing - till 5 pm as large-scale arson and stabbing incidents erupted.

WATER IN BHOPAL is not safe. A very high level of thiocyanates - a form in which

cyanide exists in the human body - hasbeen found in the sub-soil, lake and filtered water of Bhopal more than 100 days after methyl isocyanate leaked from the Union Carbide pesticide plant killing over 2000 people last December. This is the finding of the medical survey of Bhopal gas victims conducted by some doctors of the KEM Hospital, Bombay.
