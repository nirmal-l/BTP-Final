Doctor couple, their three kids took selfie before new start - then tragedy struck

ON THE PASSENGER MANIFEST

UNTIL THURSDAY'S Air India plane crash, Dr Koni Vyas's family was onthe brink of anew life. A pathologist working at a private hospital in Rajasthan's Banswara, Koni, 33, had resigned from her position last month so she could begin a new life with her husband Pratik Joshi, 36, a London-based doctor, and their

three children - five-year-old twin boys Pradyut and Nakul and 10-year-old daughter Miraya. Before the flight took off, the family clicked a selfie - the beaming parents on one side of the aisle and the children onthe other. Minutes later, tragedy would strike.
