Modi plans Manipur visit, warring groups say expect to be heard

MORE THAN two years after Manipur erupted in ethnic violence, Prime Minister Narendra Modi is expected to visit the state this month. The PM is scheduled to be in Assam and Mizoram on September 13-14, and sources said he may visit Manipur too. "The PM's visit to the state is imminent. We cannot say the exact date now as too many factors have to be taken into account before making the final announcement. But he is visiting the state," an informed source in Manipur said.
