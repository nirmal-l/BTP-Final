Delhi riots: HC den bail to 10 who have spent 5 years in jail

"'WELL-ORCHESTRATED CONSPIRACY'

UNDERLINING THAT the 2020 Delhi riots were not a "regular protest" but a "premeditated, well-orchestrated conspiracy," the Delhi High Court Tuesday declined to grant bail to Umar Khalid, Sharjeel Imam, Gulfisha Fatima and seven others charged as conspirators of the riots.
