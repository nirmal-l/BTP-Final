SC relief to Pratapgarhi: Can't be so shaky on our basics over a poem or stand-up comedy

QUASHES GUJARAT POLICE FIR OVER POST

QUASHING AN FIR by Gujarat Police against Congress Rajya Sabha MP Imran Pratapgarhi over an Instagram post, the Supreme Court said Friday that "even if a large number of persons dislike the views expressed by another, the right of the person toexpress the views must be respected and protected". It said "literature, including poetry, dramas, films, satire and art make the life of human beings more meaningful', and "75 years into our republic, we cannot be seen tobe so shaky on our fundamentals that mere recital ofa poem or for that matter any form of art or entertainment, suchas stand-up comedy, canbe alleged to lead to animosity or
