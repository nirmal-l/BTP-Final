Cabinet clears %23k-cr scheme for electronic components, targets 92k jobs

THE UNION Cabinet on Friday cleared a Rs 22,919 crore incentive scheme for manufacturing electronic components, spread over a period of six years. The scheme aims to deepen India's presence in global value chains, while increasing domestic value addition in the country. On March 17, The Indian Express had first reported that the government was working on the scheme, along with the outlay and its contours. The government is hoping that at least 91,600 direct jobs will be created as part of the scheme, andhas tied participating entities' yearly subsidies to the numberof jobs they create.
