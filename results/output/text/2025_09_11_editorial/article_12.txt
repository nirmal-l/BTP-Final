THE JAMMU AND Kashmir Assembly, by a majority vote, decided to ignore the directions of the state High Court to allow three Opposition members to participatein the proceedings ofthe House. There were heated exchanges and two walkouts by National Conference (Farooq) members, according to whom the action of suspension of the three members contravened the rules of procedure of the House.

THE BANGLADESH FOREIGN Minister, Humayun Rashed Choudhury, once again denied the presence of"any illegal Bangladeshis in Assam". He said Indira Gandhi had announced in Parliament in 1972 that all refugees who had gone to India returned to Bangladesh. "So, quoting the Indian Prime Minister we can say that there are no Bangiadesiinationais in India," hesaid.He also added, "There is no evidence that illegal immigrants from Assam are being pushed into Bangladesh."
