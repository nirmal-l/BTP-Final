Siddaramaiah and Shivakumar call truce, for now: 'Will work together'

Johnson TA Bengaluru, November 29

KARNATAKA CHIEF Minister Siddaramaiah and his deputy D K Shivakumar signalled a truce in their power struggle Saturday following a breakfast meeting held at the CM's residence at the instance of the Congress central leadership. Both leaders put up a show of unity after their meeting, saying that any decision regarding achange of guard in Karnataka would be taken by the Congress high command.
