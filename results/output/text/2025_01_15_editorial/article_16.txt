JANuARY 15, 1985, FoRTY YEARS AGO

INDIA-SL TENSIONS

INDIA TURNED DOWN Sri Lanka's request for the unconditional retum ofits patrolcraft seized by Indian coast guards off Rameshwaram and made it clear that the intrusion notonly involved violation of Indian territorial waters but alsoits municipal laws. Foreign Secretary told the Sn Lankan High Commissioner that these intrusions were "provocative" and asked for firm assurance thatthere would benorecurrence of such acts.

SRI LANKA VIOLENCE TAMIL SEPARATISTS BLEW up five bridges

on roads to the tip of the northern Jaffna peninsula, residents said. The explosions came early into the Tamil harvest festival which some militants had chosen for the declaration of an independent Tamil state.
