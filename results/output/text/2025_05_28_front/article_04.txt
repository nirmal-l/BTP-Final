US pauses student visa interviews, plans social media vetting: Report

THE US administration led by President Donald Trump has asked its embassies and consular sections to "pause scheduling new interviews for student visa applicants" as it considers requiring all foreign students to undergo social media vetting as part of their application, Politico reported on Tuesday, citing a diplomatic cable.
