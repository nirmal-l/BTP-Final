'8+ jump, I miss dad': Young long jumper's tribute to father, and a promise for India

ON THE most important day of his budding career, 18-year-old long jumper Shahnavaz Khan tookamomentto rememberhis late father Naseemudin. Before stepping onto the runway at the Kalinga Stadium in Bhubaneswar for the final of the World Athletics Bronze Level Continental Tour on Sunday, he wrote a message on

the back of his bib. It read: "8+ jump, miss dad." Youngest of the 12 jumpers in the final comprising some of India's best, the teenager stunned the field with 8.04 metres - his personal best - in the fourth round to go into the lead before Commonwealth Games and Asian Games medalist Murali Sreeshankar fought back to clinch gold with 8.13 metres.
