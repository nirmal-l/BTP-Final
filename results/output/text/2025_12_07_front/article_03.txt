UNDERLINING INDIAS "freedom of choice" in its relationships, External Affairs Minister § Jaishankar on Saturday said Russian President Vladimir Putin's two-day visit won't complicate India's ties with the United States. He was responding to a question on Putin's visit amid trade tensions with the US, after the Donald Trump administration imposed an additional 25% penalty tariff on India for importing Russian oil.
