Gravity figured in Vedic texts, Newton found it much later, says Governor

Governor Bagde was speaking at the IGNOU convocation in Jaipur

Haribhau Kisanrao Bagde on Wednesday said that the theory of gravity was mentioned in the Vedic texts long before Isaac Newton discovered it in 1687. Addressing the convocation ceremony of the Indira Gandhi National Open University's Regional Centre in Jaipur, the Govemor said, "India has been the best in the world inthe traditon of knowledge. India gave the decimal system to the whole world. Newton told the world about the theory of gravity much later. In India, this was mentioned in Vedic texts long ago." Bagde said that several inventions, be it dectricity or airplanes, find a mention in our historical texts, including the Rigveda. He said that Maharshi Bhardwaj wrote a book mentioning airplanes, and that 50 years ago, NASA wrote a letter seeking this book.
