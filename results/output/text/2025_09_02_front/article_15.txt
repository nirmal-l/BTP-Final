LIFE IN Matanomadh, a thinly populated village nearly 100 km west of Bhuj town in Gujarat's Kutch district, has never been easy. Surrounded by thorny shrubs, the terrain is harsh and unforgiving, unfit for cultivation orlarge humansettlements. Yet, this village might end up as apotential test bed for the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) ahead of its Mangalyaan- 2 mission to Mars.
