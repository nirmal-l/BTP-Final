Rahul wraps up yatra, says 'vote chori hydrogen bomb' coming

AS RAHUL Gandhi and Tejashwi Prasad Yadav's Voter Adhikar Yatra drew to a close Monday, after covering 25 Bihar districts over a fortnight, the Congress had reasons to be pleased. After more than twodecades, Congress flags dominated at a Bihar event, along with the RJD's, even as other INDIA bloc allies turned up to show their support.
