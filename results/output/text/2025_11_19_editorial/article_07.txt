THE PAKISTAN President, General Zia-ul-Hag, will visit India on December 16 atthe invitation of the Prime Minister, Rajiv Gandhi, to continue their dialogue on the normalisation of relations. This was decided at a meeting between the two leaders, who are in Muscat to attend celebrations marking 15 years of rule by Sultan Qaboos bin Said of Oman. Zia will visit India "fora few hours on his way back home from an official visit to the Maldives".
