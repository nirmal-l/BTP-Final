Brothers wanted for Goa blaze deported, being taken to Goa

LUTHRAS RAN CLUB WHERE 25 DIED

Pavneet Singh Chadha, Alok Singh & Nirbhay Thakur Panaji, NewDelhi, Dec 16

NINE DAYS after they fled to Thailand within hours of the blaze at their Goa nightclub where 25 people died, Saurabh Luthra and Gaurav Luthra were deported to India Tuesday. The Goa Police took them into custody shortly after the Luthra brothers landed at the

Delhi airport in the afternoon. Police obtained a transit remand for their transfer to Goa. They are to be flown to Goa Wednesday morning.
