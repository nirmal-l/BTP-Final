'They spread vote bank ka virus': PM targets Cong for opposing Waaf law

PRIME MINISTER Narendra Modi on Monday targeted the Congress for opposing the Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025, accusing itof spreading "vote bank ka virus". wanted to bring equality, but the Congress spread vote bank ka virus in the country. He wanted every poor person to live with dignity, with heads held high, to dream and fulfill them.
