18 yrs on, S&P upgrades India's rating, says US tariff impact 'manageable'

S&P GLOBAL Ratings on Thursday upgraded its assessment of India to BBB from BBB-, with a stable outlook, saying India is "among the best performing economies in the world". The upgrade by the American rating agency - the world's largest - comes 18 years after it had last raised its India assessment in January 2007 to BBB- in the rating scale.
