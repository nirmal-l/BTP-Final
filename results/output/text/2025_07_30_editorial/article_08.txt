THETWENTIETH JNANPITH Award for the year 1984 has been given to Thakazhi Sivasankara Pillai, popular Malayalam novelist and short storywriter. This is the third time that the award has gone to Malayalam. The annual Jnanpith Award ofRs 1.5 lakh, given for the best creative writingby an Indian citizeninany of the 15 languages, has gone four times each to Hindi and Kannada, thrice to Bengali, and once each to Assamese, Gujarati, Marathi, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.
