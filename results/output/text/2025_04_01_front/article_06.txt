THE CONTROVERSY over Mohanlal-starrer Empuraan may have been "manufactured" by the movie's producers to get publicity for the film, but the credit for tuming it into a political row should goto Kerala Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan, newly elected state BJP president Rajeev Chandrasekhar has said. Chandrasekhar said he wanted to watch the movie because it was a sequel to the hit film Lucifer, but dropped the plan on Sunday after the makers of Empuraan said they were incorporating 17 cuts, and Mohanlal also expressed regret.
