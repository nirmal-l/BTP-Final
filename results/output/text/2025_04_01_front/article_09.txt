Haryana folk singers vs the CM's OSD: Lyrics hit a rough pitch

BETWEEN TADKE pavei laas nahar mein (At dawn, the body will be found in the canal) and 'Aankh aag te laal teri, aur asla hardam sath rhve (Your eyes are burning with fire, you are never without agun),, there may be little to choose. But choose the Haryana Police is doing, as its cyber wing cracks down on folk songs "promoting violence" - which has ended up pitting the singers against one of their own, Gajender Phogat, whois now an Officer on Special Duty (OSD), Special Publicity, to Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini. Masoom Sharma, six of whose songs have been banned - the most among all the singers - has questioned the criteria used. A few days back, Sharma was stopped from singing one of the banned songs during a public performance in Gurgaon.
