THE CITY AND I

While most cities across the country were busy renaming their streets to either change their memory or reassign new meanings to them, my new host didnt bother much. She was happy with a "main road". Her stories were not etched in the hot asphalt that, with every new layer, covers up old wounds; rather, they lived on - sometimes as oral history, other times, through common refrain, as local jibes. It is the story of her struggle to achieve a separate identity, toembrace her jal, jungle', jameen'.
