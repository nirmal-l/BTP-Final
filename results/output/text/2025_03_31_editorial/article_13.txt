JHUGGIS GUTTED IN FIRE

ABOUT 500 JHUGGIS were gutted ina devastating fire that raged for about two hours in a jhuggi colony in Samaipur in North Delhi. Since most of the occupants were sleeping outside, they escaped major bums. However, a few received minor burns. By the time the fire was brought under control, most of the belongings of the jhuggi dwellers had burnt.
