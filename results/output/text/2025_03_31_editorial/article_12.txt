MARCH 31, 1985, FORTY YEARS AGO

CPM LEADS CHARGE GRENADE SPARKS PANIC

THE COMMUNIST PARTY of India (Marxist) has taken an initiative to hold a meeting of the 12 opposition parties that were represented at the Calcutta conclave in January last year, to discuss its proposal to launch a joint campaign against the government's "anti-people" policies and "large-scale rigging" in the recent Lok Sabha and assembly elections. Significantly, the CPM hasalso initiated a move for rapprochement with the CPI, with which it is engaged in a war of words that started when the two parties opposed each other in the recent Lok Sabhaand assembly elections,

PANIC AROSE IN Chandigarh when a grenade, HE-36, was detected by the side of the road near the Sector 19-27 Lights Crossing by patrolling CRPF personnelonthe eve of President Zail Singh's visit. It was launched near the electricity maintenance booth. The police rushed immediately and the grenade was covered with sandbags.
