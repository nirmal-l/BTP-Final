Patel wanted to merge all of Kashmir with India, Nehru didn't let him: PM

PRIME MINISTER Narendra Modi on Friday targeted the Congress for adopting a "spineless attitude" while dealing with issues related to the country's sovereignty, and said that while Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel wanted "all of Kashmir" to be united with India, Jawaharlal Nehru "did not let his wish be fulfilled".
