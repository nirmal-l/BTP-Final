Site of NREGS launch, 'transformed' Andhra village has few fears onchanges in scheme

Sreenivas Janyala Bandlapalli (Andhra Pradesh), December 20

GONE ARE the huts and dilapidated houses, and the barren fields, Bandlapalli village now has pucca houses paintedin attractive colours, with glinting water taps at their doorsteps, crowded lanes where tractors jostle with motorcycles, and fields of mangoes, orchids, pomegranates among other crops, and micro dairy farms. Thestory of thistransformation istold by a plaque in black stone. It marks the launch, on February 2, 2006, of the National Rural Employee Guarantee Programme fromthe Bandlapalli gram panchayat, by then Congress Prime Minister Manmohan Singh, in the presence of Congress leader Sonia Gandhi and then Congress Chief Minister of united Andhra PradeshY S Rajasekhara Reddy.
