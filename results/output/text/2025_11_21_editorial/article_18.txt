Tamils shouldreturn

INDIA WOULD like the Sri Lankan Tamil refugees in India to go back to SriLanka with honourand security, the Prime Minister, Rajiv Gandhi, told the Lok Sabha during question hour. Hesaid, "We treat the Tamil problemasa SriLankan problem and not an Indian problem." However, on questions such as human rights violations, the country did take up such matters with the countries concerned. He said India had expressed concern over the ceasefire violations on both sides and certain actions of the Sri Lankan security forces.
