Heena Khandelwal Mumbai, December 17

ZAKIR KHAN dons many hats - stand-up comedian, writer, poet, lyricist and actor - and has emerged asone of the most recognisable voices of contemporary Indian storytelling. Over the last decade, he has built a loyal following through a deeply personal brand of humour that blends romance, heartbreak, family, ambition and quiet resilience. Most recently, in August 2025, he became the first Indian comedian to headline a full-length Hindi stand-up show at the iconic Madison Square Garden in New York.
