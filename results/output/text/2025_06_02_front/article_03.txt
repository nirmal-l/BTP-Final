Over 2,000 illegal immigrants 'pushed back' into Bangladesh since Op Sindoor Fearing crackdown, about 2,000 also reached border voluntarily: Official

ACTION ALONG BORDER IN TRIPURA, MEGHALAYA, ASSAM

MORE THAN 2,000 alleged illegal Bangladeshiimmigrants are estimated to have been "pushed back" across the border by Indian authorities since Operation Sindoor began in the early hours of May 7, following a nationwide verification exercise, Government sources told The

Indian Express. During the same period, they said, a similar number of immigrants showed up near the India-Bangladesh border voluntarily to cross over amid the fear generated by the crackdown.
