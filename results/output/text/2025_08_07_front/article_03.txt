Unreasonable, unjustified, unfair: India hits back at Trump tariff

INDIA ON Wednesday hit back at the United States for doubling the tariff to 50 per cent, reiterating that the action was "unfair, unjustified and unreasonable". The government said it would take "all actions necessary" to "protect its national interests". "The United States has in recent days targeted India's oil imports from Russia. We have already made clear our position on these issues, including the fact that our imports are based on market factors and done with the overall objective of ensuring the energysecurity of 1.4 billion people of India," the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) official spokesperson, Randhir Jaiswal, said in a statement on Wednesday evening.
