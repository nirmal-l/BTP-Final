Day after Uttarkashi flash floods, 60 feared missing, search ops on

ON THE second day of rescue operations at Uttarakhand's Dharali, carried out primarily by the Army, the search continued for those missing in the aftermath of the flash floods, estimated to be over 60, as heavy rainslashed the state and several rivers breached the danger level.
