Centre to SC: Setting deadline for President, Governors can cause constitutional disorder

ANANTHAKRISHNANG

OPPOSING THE Supreme Court's decision to fix a timeline for the President and Governors to act on Bills forwarded by State Legislatures, the Centre has underlined the separation of powers between the executive, legislature and judiciary, and said the order has the potential to "destabilise the constitutional balance between (the) organs of State". It has emphasised that

"gubematorial assent is a high prerogative, plenary, non-justiciable power'.
