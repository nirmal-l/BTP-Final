The day after, the agonising hours: Families line up for DNA tests to identify kin

THE DAY after Air India's London-bound Al 171 crashed soon after taking off from Ahmedabad, staff at the BJ Medical College, where the aircraft fell and broke into pieces, ushered a solemn group of people into a large hail on the ground floor of the main building and seated them on rows of benches where the students usually write their exams. These were family members and relatives of many of the 241 passengers and crew who had died in Thursday's crash - arriving from different parts of the country, waiting to provide their DNA samples so that they could identify whatever was left of their loved ones, According to rescue personnel, most of the bodies were charred beyond recognition, and many died with their seatbelts stillon.
