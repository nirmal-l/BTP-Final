SIM card probe: Pak operatives 'in touch' with Army personnel posted in J&K

CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE agen-cies have found that Pakistanbased operatives using Indian SIM cards, allegedly smuggled out by a Nepal national, had reached out to at least 75 Indian Army personnel posted in J&K and Mathura and were "in touch" with them mainly through WhatsApp, it is learnt. "After identifying the details (of the Army personnel), we are likely to inform their unit heads and summon them for questioning in the coming days.
