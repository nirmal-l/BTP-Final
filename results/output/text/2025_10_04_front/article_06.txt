AIIMS study shows how training gap in certifying brain death hits organ donation

ASURVEY of 177 of the country's top neurosurgeons, neumlogists and critical care specialists shows that over half (59.2 per cent) were not trained in medical colleges to certify brain death - aknowledge gap thatimpacts organ donations, according toa study published recently by doctors at the All India Institute of Medical Science (AIIMS), Delhi. While nearly three-fourth (74.5%) of the doctors surveyed worked at teaching hospitals, only 10 per cent said they regularly trained their residents in brain death certification.
