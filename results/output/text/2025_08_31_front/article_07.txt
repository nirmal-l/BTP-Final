Nearly 30,000 deleted electors in Bihar seek inclusion: Poll panel

WITH TWO days to go for the deadlineofSeptember 1,Election Commission data released Saturday showed that 29,872 electors out of the 65 lakh electors whose names were deleted in the draft stage of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoralrolls in Bihar had applied for

inclusion in the final roll. And 13.33 lakh new electors who have turned 18 years and above have also submitted enrolment forms. According to the EC's SIR order issued on June 24, all registered electors (7.89 crore) of Bihar were required to fill enumeration forms by July 25 to make it to the draft roll published on August 1.
