31 Maoists, 2 security personnel killed in Chhattisgarh gunfight

AT LEAST 31 Maoists, including 11 women, and two security personnel were killed in an encounter on Sunday at Indravati National Park in Chhattisgarh's Bijapur district. The encounter, which began around 8 am, took place inside the national park, located about 15 km from the Maharashtra border and 28 km from Bijapur's Farsegarh, the nearest village.
