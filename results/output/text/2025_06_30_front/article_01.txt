FROM Aplush condominium inside an exclusive gated community in the National Capital Region to a small three-room house in a Haryana village and then through a rented room on a terrace in the suburbs of Hyderabad to 15 morestates. All it took was just a few minutes for asum of nearly Rs 6 crore to be stolen through this circuitous route to 28 bank accounts across the country, and 141 more later, before vanishing into thin air. And thisis just one of the 1.23 lakh cases involving Rs 1,935 crore that were reported in 2024, almost thrice the numbers logged in 2022, of digital arrests - where scamsters scare and ensnare victims through fake inter-rogations on video calls before cleaning out their bank accounts. Tracking the trendsbeing reported by state police forces and cyber fraud units all over the country, The Indian Express zeroed in on an illustrative case in Gurugram, involving a 44-year-old advertising executive.
