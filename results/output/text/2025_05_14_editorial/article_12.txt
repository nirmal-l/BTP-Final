PM Modi has ensured that every move - from suspending the Indus Waters Treaty to launching military strikes on terror camps - was carefully planned and timed. The government chose strategy over impulse. This prevented Pakistan and terror groups from anticipating India'sresponse. Itensured that Operation Sindoor was executed with surprise, precision, and full impact.
