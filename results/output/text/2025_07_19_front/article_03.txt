Liquor 'scam' probe: ED arrests Baghel's son Chaitanya, gets his custody for 5 days

JAYPRAKASH S NAIDU

CHAITANYA BAGHEL son of former Chhattisgarh Chief Minister and Congress leader Bhupesh Baghel, was arrested on Friday by the Directorate of Enforcement (ED) on charges of money laundering inconnection with the alleged Rs2,161-crore liquor scam. He was subsequently produced before a special Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) court in Raipur and remanded in ED custody for five days. According to the ED, the alleged scam involved a syndicate of senior state bureaucrats, politicians, and excise department officials who are accused of running a "parallel" excise department, wherein liquor was sold to the public, but no money cametothe state exchequer, causing the governmenta loss ofaround Rs 2,161

crore, It is alleged to have taken place between 2019 and 2022, during the previous Congress governmentin Chhattisgarh that was helmed by Bhupesh Baghel. Around 7 am on Friday, the ED begansearchesat the Baghel residence at Bhilai city in Durg district. At around noon, the agency arrested Chaitanya. Speaking to the media, Chaitanya's lawyer Faisal Rizvi said, "This matter has been goingon since 2022. The first prosecution complaint (filed by the ED) was submitted in court, following which three more supplementary prosecution complaints were submitted... but neither was Chaitanya's name mentioned nor was his statement recorded in them."
